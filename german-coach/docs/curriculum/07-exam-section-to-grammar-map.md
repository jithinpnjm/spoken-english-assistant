# Exam Section to Grammar Map

## Purpose

This document maps grammar to the exam/life section where the learner actually needs it. The UI should show exam sections first and then show supporting grammar under each section.

## A1 mapping

### A1 Hören

Grammar support:

- sein / haben recognition
- W-questions
- yes/no questions
- time expressions
- modal verbs recognition
- nicht / kein recognition

Why:

- learner must understand short audio about names, times, appointments, places, and simple actions

### A1 Lesen

Grammar support:

- nouns and capitalization
- articles recognition
- present tense
- time/date phrases
- simple negation
- imperative exposure in signs

Why:

- learner must read signs, notices, short messages, opening times, and simple ads

### A1 Schreiben

Grammar support:

- verb position 2
- sein / haben
- modal verbs
- accusative basics
- time expressions
- simple connectors
- capitalization

Why:

- learner writes short messages, fills forms, and gives appointment/personal information

### A1 Sprechen

Grammar support:

- personal pronouns
- present tense
- W-questions
- yes/no questions
- articles in common nouns
- modal verbs for requests

Why:

- learner answers personal questions, asks simple questions, spells name, and gives phone/address

## A2 mapping

### A2 Hören

Grammar support:

- Perfekt recognition
- dative prepositions
- modal verbs
- weil / dass / wenn recognition
- separable verbs

Why:

- learner listens to longer everyday conversations and needs to catch past events, reasons, appointments, and changes

### A2 Lesen

Grammar support:

- Perfekt
- dative/accusative
- two-way prepositions
- subordinate clauses
- comparatives/superlatives

Why:

- learner reads ads, notices, workplace messages, instructions, and travel/housing information

### A2 Schreiben

Grammar support:

- Perfekt production
- dative basics
- accusative basics
- weil / dass clauses
- word order in subordinate clauses
- polite modal verbs

Why:

- learner writes semi-formal emails, explains problems, reschedules appointments, and requests information

### A2 Sprechen

Grammar support:

- Perfekt for past events
- weil for reasons
- dative/accusative in daily objects
- comparative forms
- reflexive verbs

Why:

- learner explains problems, makes plans, talks about past, and compares options

## B1 mapping

### B1 Lesen

Grammar support:

- subordinate clauses
- relative clauses recognition
- passive recognition
- connectors
- adjective endings recognition
- nominalization recognition

Why:

- learner reads longer articles, forum posts, ads, and opinion texts under time pressure

### B1 Hören

Grammar support:

- subordinate clauses in speech
- Konjunktiv II recognition
- passive recognition
- connectors
- prepositional verbs

Why:

- learner listens to announcements, conversations, opinions, and radio-style information

### B1 Schreiben

Grammar support:

- subordinate clauses
- connectors for argument structure
- Konjunktiv II for polite requests
- relative clauses basics
- passive basics
- adjective endings
- prepositional verbs
- formal register

Why:

- learner writes requests, complaints, opinions, and structured emails

### B1 Sprechen

Grammar support:

- suggestion patterns with Konjunktiv II
- connectors for opinions
- weil / dass / obwohl
- comparative structures
- repair phrases
- formal/informal register

Why:

- learner plans something with another person, presents a topic, and answers follow-up questions

## UI implication

When learner opens a section, grammar appears as supporting subtopics:

```text
A2 -> Schreiben
  Request information
  Appointment reschedule
  Explain problem
  Grammar support:
    weil/dass
    dative basics
    polite modal verbs
    word order
```

This keeps grammar connected to practical use and exam readiness.
