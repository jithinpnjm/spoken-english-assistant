## Exam sections observed

The A2 model test includes:

```text
Lesen
Hören
Schreiben
Sprechen
```

## Lesen

Observed structure:

- 30 minutes
- four parts
- learners read an email, information, and articles from newspaper/internet contexts
- each task has one correct answer
- dictionaries and mobile phones are not allowed

Observed task families:

1. Newspaper/internet text with multiple-choice questions
2. Information-board lookup task
3. Practical information extraction
4. Reading for relevant detail in everyday texts

App implications:

- A2 reading should move beyond A1 short signs into longer but still practical texts.
- The reading engine should train scanning, detail recognition, and choosing the only correct option.
- Texts should include emails, information boards, articles, internet notices, apartment/travel/service/workplace information.

Original app task examples:

- Read an information board and choose where to go.
- Read a short internet article and answer five multiple-choice questions.
- Read an email about an appointment change and identify the correct statement.

## Hören

Observed structure:

- 30 minutes
- four parts
- learners hear radio broadcasts, conversations, answering-machine messages, and announcements
- learners first read the tasks, then hear the text
- each task has one correct answer

Observed task families:

1. Radio-style information or announcement
2. Conversation with picture matching / schedule matching
3. Five short conversations with multiple-choice details
4. Interview with yes/no questions

App implications:

- A2 listening should include more varied audio than A1.
- The system should train schedule matching, detail extraction, and yes/no comprehension.
- Some texts are heard once and some twice depending on task family.
- The transcript should be revealed only after the learner attempts the task.

Original app task examples:

- Listen to a weekly plan conversation and match activities to weekdays.
- Listen to five short conversations and select correct details.
- Listen to an interview and answer yes/no statements.
- Listen to an announcement and identify what action is required.

## Schreiben

Observed structure:

- 30 minutes
- two parts
- learners write an SMS and an email
- writing should be clear and not in pencil
- dictionaries and mobile phones are not allowed

Observed task families:

1. Short SMS of 20-30 words, covering three bullet points
2. Email covering specified content points

App implications:

- A2 writing engine should support short SMS and email practice.
- It should enforce all bullet points.
- It should train apology, reason, new time/place, thanks, acceptance/decline, and polite tone.
- Review should include task completion, word count range when relevant, structure, grammar, and register.

Original app task examples:

- Write a 20-30 word SMS saying you are late, why, and giving a new meeting place/time.
- Write an email accepting a boss's invitation and asking one follow-up question.
- Write an email explaining a problem with an appointment and asking for a new appointment.

## Sprechen

Observed structure:

- speaking includes multiple parts
- one observed part asks learners to use cards to ask four questions and answer partner questions
- another observed part asks learners to speak about their own life using a card
- the document includes assessment criteria and examiner guidance in later pages

Observed task families:

1. Ask and answer personal questions from cards
2. Talk about your own life based on a prompt card
3. Interactive planning / negotiation task, according to the exam overview

App implications:

- A2 speaking should be more interactive than A1.
- The live agent should ask card-style questions.
- The learner should produce longer answers about personal life, routines, money, weekend, work, family, hobbies, and plans.
- The agent should run partner-style interaction tasks.

Original app task examples:

- Ask four questions from cards: Geburtstag, Wohnort, Beruf, Hobby.
- Speak for 60-90 seconds about what you do at the weekend.
- Plan a small trip or meeting with the agent.
- Answer follow-up questions after a personal topic.

## Required grammar support for A2

The observed task demand supports:

- Perfekt
- Präteritum basics: war, hatte
- dative basics
- dative prepositions
- two-way prepositions
- weil / dass / wenn
- separable verbs
- modal verbs
- word order in subordinate clauses
- comparatives and superlatives
- reflexive verbs
- adjective endings introduction
