## Exam sections observed

The B1 model test includes modules:

```text
Lesen
Hören
Schreiben
Sprechen
```

Observed timing:

- Lesen: 65 minutes
- Hören: 40 minutes
- Schreiben: 60 minutes
- Sprechen: 15 minutes for two participants

## Lesen

Observed structure:

- Lesen module has five parts
- learners read multiple texts and answer tasks
- each task has only one correct answer
- no dictionaries or mobile phones are allowed

Observed task families:

1. Reading text and deciding right/wrong statements
2. Matching or detail-comprehension tasks
3. Practical information extraction
4. Opinion or longer text comprehension
5. Time-managed reading across multiple parts

App implications:

- B1 reading must be timed and multi-part.
- The app should train global, selective, and detailed reading.
- The learner must practice identifying relevant details and avoiding distractors.
- B1 reading should include opinion/forum texts, notices, ads, articles, and public/work information.

Original app task examples:

- Read a forum post and decide which statements are correct.
- Match advertisements to user needs.
- Read a short article and answer multiple-choice questions.
- Practice timed reading with five short parts.

## Hören

Observed structure:

- Hören module has four parts
- learners hear several texts and solve tasks
- learners read tasks first, then hear the audio
- one correct answer per task
- five minutes are given after listening to transfer answers

Observed task families:

1. Five short texts, each heard twice, with two tasks per text
2. One longer text heard once with multiple-choice tasks
3. One conversation heard once with true/false tasks
4. One discussion heard twice with speaker-to-statement matching

App implications:

- B1 listening must include short texts, longer informational text, conversation, and discussion.
- The app should support heard-once and heard-twice modes.
- It should include true/false, multiple choice, and speaker matching.
- It should train note-taking and answer-transfer discipline.

Original app task examples:

- Listen to five short public/service messages and answer two questions each.
- Listen to a museum/tour-style information text and answer five detail questions.
- Listen to a conversation at a bus stop and mark statements true/false.
- Listen to a discussion and match statements to speakers.

## Schreiben

Observed structure:

- Schreiben module has three parts
- in tasks 1 and 3, learners write emails
- in task 2, learners write a discussion contribution
- learners may begin with any task
- no dictionaries or mobile phones are allowed
- total writing time is 60 minutes

Observed task families:

1. Email
2. Discussion post / opinion contribution
3. Email

App implications:

- B1 writing engine must support multiple writing genres.
- It must train structured emails and discussion/opinion writing.
- It should score task completion, structure, grammar, connectors, tone, and clarity.
- It should force a rewrite after feedback.

Original app task examples:

- Write an email requesting information from a service provider.
- Write a complaint email about a housing or appointment problem.
- Write a discussion post giving an opinion with reasons and examples.
- Write an informal email explaining a situation and asking for advice.

## Sprechen

Observed structure:

- Sprechen is 15 minutes for two participants
- preparation time is 15 minutes
- learners prepare alone
- learners may make notes
- in the exam they should speak freely
- dictionaries and mobile phones are not allowed

Observed app implications:

- B1 speaking practice must train prepared but free speaking.
- The learner should prepare notes but not read full sentences.
- The app should include partner-style interaction and follow-up questions.
- The live teacher should score fluency, task completion, grammar, vocabulary, and interaction.

Expected B1 speaking task families for app design:

1. Plan something together
2. Present or speak about a topic
3. Answer follow-up questions / discussion

Original app task examples:

- Plan a weekend activity with the agent.
- Present a short topic about learning German, work, transport, or living in Germany.
- Answer follow-up questions about your opinion.
- Practice polite disagreement and clarification.

## Required grammar support for B1

The observed task demand supports:

- subordinate clauses
- connectors for reasons, contrast, sequence, and conclusion
- Konjunktiv II for polite requests and suggestions
- relative clauses
- passive recognition and basic production
- adjective endings
- prepositional verbs
- formal vs informal register
- argument structure
