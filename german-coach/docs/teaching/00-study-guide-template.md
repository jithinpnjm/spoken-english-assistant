# German Study Guide Template

## Purpose

Every German subtopic should have a detailed study guide. The learner is a beginner living in Germany and preparing for Goethe exams, so explanations must be clear, practical, and exam-connected.

## Required structure for each subtopic

```text
1. Goal
2. Why this matters for living in Germany
3. Why this matters for Goethe exam
4. English explanation
5. German pattern
6. Examples with English meaning
7. Common mistakes
8. Mini drills
9. Chat practice questions
10. Live speaking prompts
11. Writing task
12. Listening task
13. Review checklist
14. Goethe-style exam task
```

## Example: A1 Schreiben -> Appointment cancellation

### Goal

The learner can write a short German message saying they cannot attend an appointment and asking for a new appointment.

### Survival use

Useful for:

- doctor appointment
- German class
- Kita
- Ausländerbehörde
- work meeting

### Goethe exam use

Useful for A1 writing tasks where the learner must write a short message with required points.

### English explanation

In German, short appointment messages need three things:

1. Greeting
2. Problem/reason
3. Request or next step

### German pattern

```text
Hallo Frau/Herr ...,
ich bin krank.
Ich kann heute nicht kommen.
Können wir einen neuen Termin machen?
Viele Grüße
...
```

### Examples

```text
Ich bin krank.
I am sick.

Ich kann heute nicht kommen.
I cannot come today.

Können wir einen neuen Termin machen?
Can we make a new appointment?
```

### Common mistakes

Wrong:

```text
Ich komme nicht heute.
```

Better:

```text
Ich kann heute nicht kommen.
```

Wrong:

```text
Ich habe ein Termin.
```

Correct:

```text
Ich habe einen Termin.
```

Reason:

- Termin is masculine: der Termin
- after haben, use accusative: einen Termin

### Mini drills

1. Fill the blank: Ich ___ heute nicht kommen.
2. Translate: I am sick.
3. Correct the sentence: Ich habe ein Termin.
4. Write a message: You are sick and cannot come today.

### Live speaking prompts

- Hast du heute einen Termin?
- Warum kannst du nicht kommen?
- Kannst du den Satz wiederholen: Ich kann heute nicht kommen?

### Writing task

Write a short message to your German teacher:

- say you are sick
- say you cannot come today
- ask for homework

### Listening task

Teacher reads a short message. Learner answers:

- Who is sick?
- Can the person come today?
- What does the person ask for?

### Review checklist

- greeting included
- reason included
- request included
- correct word order
- correct modal verb
- nouns capitalized

### Exam-style task

Write a short message. You cannot come to your German class today. Write:

- why you cannot come
- that you are sorry
- ask what homework you have

## Quality rule

A study guide is not complete if it only lists grammar. It must include explanation, examples, mistakes, drills, speaking, writing, listening, and exam-style practice.
