# German Curriculum – Maintainer Documentation

This folder is the design and methodology source for the German course in `language-portal/docs/german/`.
It is **not** published as part of the Docusaurus site (it lives outside `docs/`). Learner-facing
explanations of the same ideas live in `docs/german/course-guide/`.

It replaces the former planning portal `german-coach/docs/` (35 planning documents), whose knowledge was
migrated here in the "German curriculum migration" (see `migration-map.md`). Nobody needs the old
folder to understand, extend, or teach this course.

## Non-negotiable principles (summary)

1. The learner is an English-speaking **genuine beginner living in Germany** who also wants Goethe certificates (A1 first, then A2, long-term B1).
2. **Teach in English, train in German** at A0/A1; German share grows with level.
3. One concept at a time: what is it → why do I need it → how does it work → pattern → real German examples → guided production → real-life use → (where relevant) exam use.
4. Every learner answer is reviewed; after correction the learner **must rewrite or repeat** the corrected German.
5. Nouns are taught with article and plural; verbs with their case/preposition pattern.
6. Practice must make the learner **produce** German, not only recognise it.
7. Exam claims must be verifiable against official Goethe material. Daily-life relevance and exam relevance are stated separately. Not every chapter needs an exam link.
8. Original practice content only – never copy official exam tasks, textbook exercises, or third-party scripts.
9. Medical and administrative roleplays practise communication; the AI never diagnoses, prescribes, or states uncertain legal rules as fact.
10. Quality consistency matters more than structural consistency. A short excellent chapter beats a long shallow one.
