# Everyday Functions

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** handle simple real-life situations politely and clearly, without translating from your first language.

An everyday **function** is something you *do* with language: greet, ask, order, book, explain, thank, apologise. For each function, English has ready-made patterns. Learn the pattern and fill in the details.

## The pattern

> **Polite starter + clear request or information + detail if needed.**
> *Excuse me, could you help me? I'm looking for the train station.*

| Polite starter | Use |
|---|---|
| *Excuse me, …* | to get attention (a stranger, staff) |
| *Could you …, please?* | to ask someone to do something |
| *Could I have …, please?* / *I'd like …* | to ask for something (food, a ticket) |
| *I need …* | to state a need (appointment, help) |
| *Sorry, …* | to interrupt, apologise, or ask for repetition |

In service situations, *Give me…* and *I want…* sound rude in English, even if the direct translation is polite in your language. Use *Could I have…?* and *I'd like…*.

## Model sentences

- *Excuse me, could you help me find this address?*
- *I'd like a coffee and a croissant, please.*
- *How much does this cost?*
- *I'd like to book an appointment for tomorrow morning.*
- *I've had stomach pain since yesterday.*
- *Could we have the bill, please?*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Give me coffee.* / *I want coffee.* | *Could I have a coffee, please?* / *I'd like a coffee, please.* | Polite request language in service situations. |
| *Where station?* | *Where is the station?* | A question needs the verb. |
| *Where is going train station?* | *How do I get to the train station?* | Ask for the route with *How do I get to…?* |
| *How much this?* | *How much is this?* | Include *is*. |
| *Help me this.* | *Could you help me with this?* | *help someone **with** something*. |
| *I want doctor.* | *I need to see a doctor.* | Natural phrase for a health need. |
| *I want appointment tomorrow.* | *I'd like to book an appointment for tomorrow.* | *book an appointment **for** a time*. |

## Practice drills

1. **Frame:** *Could I have ___, please?* / *Could you help me with ___?* / *I'd like to ___.*
2. **Directions:** ask the way to three places, then give directions to your home: *Go straight, turn left at the traffic lights, it's on the right.*
3. **At the doctor's:** describe one symptom, how long you've had it, and ask one question.
4. **Small talk:** start and end a two-minute chat with a neighbour.
