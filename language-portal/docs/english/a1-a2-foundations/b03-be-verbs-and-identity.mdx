# Be Verbs and Identity

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** introduce yourself and describe people and things with *am, is, are*.

The verb **be** connects a person or thing to information about it: identity, job, nationality, age, place, feeling, or description. Many languages leave this verb out (*"I Alex"*, *"She tired"*). English never does — every sentence needs a verb.

## The pattern

| Subject | be | Information |
|---|---|---|
| I | **am** ('m) | Alex. / a nurse. / 34. / at home. |
| He / She / It | **is** ('s) | my friend. / from India. / tired. |
| You / We / They | **are** ('re) | engineers. / in Berlin. / late. |

- **Negative:** *I'm not late. She isn't (is not) at home. They aren't (are not) ready.*
- **Question:** move *be* to the front: *Are you ready? Is she your sister? Where is the station?*
- **Short answers:** *Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. · Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.* (Never *Yes, I'm.*)

## Lessons in this module

| # | Lesson | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I am / you are / he is | *I am Alex. You are my teacher. He is my brother.* |
| 2 | Names, jobs, and nationalities | *She's a doctor. We're Indian. He's an engineer.* (jobs need *a/an*) |
| 3 | Age and basic personal details | *I'm 34.* (not *I have 34 years*) · *My phone number is …* |
| 4 | This is / these are | *This is my wife. These are my children.* |
| 5 | There is / there are | *There's a pharmacy near the station. There are two bedrooms.* |
| 6 | Be-verb negatives | *I'm not busy. It isn't expensive. They aren't here.* |
| 7 | Be-verb questions | *Are you from Kerala? Where is your office?* |
| 8 | Short answers with be | *Is it far? — No, it isn't.* |

## Model sentences

- *Hi, I'm Alex. I'm from India, and I'm a software engineer.*
- *This is my colleague, Sandra. She's new in the team.*
- *There's a supermarket on the corner.*
- *Are you free this evening? — Yes, I am.*
- *We aren't late. The meeting is at ten.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I Alex.* | *I am Alex.* / *I'm Alex.* | English needs a verb. |
| *She are my friend.* | *She is my friend.* | *is* with he/she/it. |
| *They is at home.* | *They are at home.* | *are* with you/we/they. |
| *I have 30 years.* | *I am 30 (years old).* | Age uses *be* in English. |
| *He is engineer.* | *He is an engineer.* | Jobs need *a/an*. |
| *Yes, I'm.* | *Yes, I am.* | No contraction in a positive short answer. |
| *There have two rooms.* | *There are two rooms.* | Existence uses *there is / there are*. |

## Practice drills

1. **Complete:** *I am ___ · She is ___ · They are ___* (a name, a job, a place).
2. **Introduce a person:** *This is ___. He/She is ___. He/She is from ___.*
3. **Question and short answer:** ask *Are you…? / Is it…?* and answer *Yes, I am / No, it isn't.*
4. **Describe a room:** three sentences with *There is / There are*.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] introduce myself with name, job, nationality, and city
- [ ] give my age with *I'm … years old*
- [ ] introduce another person with *This is…*
- [ ] make negatives and questions with *be* and answer with short answers
- [ ] describe a place with *there is / there are*
