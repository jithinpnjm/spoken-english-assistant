# Nouns, Articles and Quantity

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** use articles and quantity words naturally when you talk about food, shopping, and things at home.

Articles and quantity words tell the listener whether you mean **one thing**, **a specific thing**, **things in general**, or **an amount**. Many languages (Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Russian, Chinese…) have no articles, so they feel optional — but missing articles are one of the first things English listeners notice.

## The pattern

| Word | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a** / **an** | one thing, not specific, first mention; *an* before a vowel **sound** | *I need a bag. She's an engineer. It takes an hour.* |
| **the** | a specific thing we both know | *The bag is on the table.* |
| no article | things in general, plurals, uncountables | *I like dogs. Water is important.* |
| **some** | an amount (mostly positive sentences, offers, requests) | *There's some rice. Would you like some tea?* |
| **any** | an amount in negatives and most questions | *We don't have any milk. Do you have any questions?* |
| **many** / **much** | a large number (countable) / a large amount (uncountable) | *How many apples? How much water?* |
| **this / that / these / those** | near / far · singular / plural | *This bag is heavy. Those shoes are nice.* |

**Countable** nouns have a plural: *apple → apples, book → books, child → children.*
**Uncountable** nouns have no plural and no *a/an*: *water, rice, milk, money, information, advice, furniture, luggage, news, homework.*

### a or an? Listen to the sound, not the letter

- *an hour, an honest man, an MBA* (vowel sound)
- *a university, a European city, a one-way ticket* (consonant sound /j/ or /w/)

## Model sentences

- *I need a bag. The bag on the left is mine.*
- *We have two apples and some rice.*
- *Do you have any milk? — Sorry, we don't have any.*
- *How much is this jacket? How many sizes do you have?*
- *Could I have some information about the course?*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I need apple.* | *I need an apple.* | One singular countable noun needs *a/an*. |
| *I bought two book.* | *I bought two books.* | Plural after numbers above one. |
| *How many water?* | *How much water?* | *water* is uncountable. |
| *Can you give me an advice?* | *Can you give me some advice?* | *advice* is uncountable. |
| *She is doctor.* | *She is a doctor.* | Jobs need *a/an*. |
| *The life is beautiful.* | *Life is beautiful.* | General abstract idea: no article. |
| *a university → an university* | *a university* | *university* starts with a /j/ sound. |

## Practice drills

1. **Choose:** *a / an / the / some / any* — *I need ___ umbrella. Is there ___ coffee? ___ keys are on ___ table.*
2. **Shopping list:** say what you need with correct quantity words: *some eggs, a loaf of bread, two kilos of rice, a bottle of water.*
3. **How much / how many:** ask five shop questions.
4. **First and second mention:** *I saw a cat. The cat was black.* Make three pairs.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] use *a / an / the* correctly in short sentences
- [ ] make plurals and know common uncountable nouns
- [ ] use *some / any* and *much / many*
- [ ] point with *this / that / these / those*
