# Classroom and Learning Language

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** ask for help during a lesson or conversation instead of getting stuck in silence.

Learning language lets you **control the conversation**: you can ask someone to repeat, slow down, give an example, check your pronunciation, or wait. These phrases are the most useful thing a beginner can learn, because they keep every other conversation going.

## The pattern

> **Polite starter + exact need.**
> *Could you repeat that, please?* · *How do I say … in English?* · *Can you give me an example?*

## Lessons in this module

| # | Lesson | Key phrases |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Saying you don't understand | *Sorry, I don't understand.* · *I don't understand this word.* |
| 2 | Asking the teacher to repeat | *Could you repeat that, please?* · *Could you say that again more slowly?* |
| 3 | Asking how to say a word | *How do I say "…" in English?* · *What's the English word for …?* |
| 4 | Asking for examples | *Can you give me an example?* · *Can you use it in a sentence?* |
| 5 | Checking pronunciation | *How do you pronounce this?* · *Is this correct: "…"?* |
| 6 | Saying you need more time | *Just a moment, please.* · *I need a little more time to think.* |
| 7 | Giving a simple self-evaluation | *I think I understand now.* · *I'm still not sure about …* |
| 8 | Repeating and correcting yourself | *Sorry, I mean …* · *Let me say that again.* |

## Model sentences

- *Sorry, I didn't understand that. Could you repeat it, please?*
- *What does "deadline" mean?*
- *How do you spell that?*
- *Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?*
- *Can you give me one example?*
- *Let me try again: I went to the office yesterday.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I didn't understood.* | *I didn't understand.* | After *didn't*, use the base verb. |
| *Repeat me, please.* | *Could you repeat that, please?* | You repeat *something* (that, the question), not a person. |
| *Explain me this.* | *Could you explain this to me?* | *Explain something **to** someone.* |
| *What is the meaning of this word in English?* (to ask for a translation) | *What's the English word for …?* | Ask for the word, not its meaning, when you know the idea. |
| *How to say this?* | *How do I say this?* | A spoken question needs *do* + subject. |

## Practice drills

1. **Frame drill.** Complete: *Could you ___, please?* — repeat that / speak more slowly / spell that / give me an example.
2. **Self-repair.** Say a sentence, then correct it with *Sorry, I mean…*: *I go yesterday — sorry, I mean I went yesterday.*
3. **Word hunt.** Ask *What's the English word for …?* for three objects around you.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] say I don't understand and ask for repetition politely
- [ ] ask how to say or spell a word
- [ ] ask for an example
- [ ] ask for time to think instead of going silent
- [ ] correct myself with *Sorry, I mean…*
