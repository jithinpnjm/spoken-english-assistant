# Basic Prepositions

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** say where something is, when something happens, and where someone goes.

Prepositions are small words that show **place, time, direction, connection, or purpose**. They rarely translate one-to-one from other languages, so learn them in **chunks** (*at home, on Monday, in the kitchen*), not as single words.

## The pattern

### Time: the AT → ON → IN ladder (small → big)

| at | on | in |
|---|---|---|
| clock times: *at 10 o'clock, at noon* | days: *on Monday* | months: *in March* |
| *at night, at the weekend* (UK) | dates: *on 5 June* | years: *in 2025* |
| *at Christmas* | *on my birthday, on Monday morning* | *in the morning / afternoon / evening* |

**No preposition** before *today, tomorrow, yesterday, next, last, this, every*: *See you **next** Monday.* (not *on next Monday*)

### Place

| at — a point | on — a surface / line | in — inside a space |
|---|---|---|
| *at the bus stop, at the door* | *on the table, on the wall* | *in the kitchen, in the box* |
| *at home, at work, at school* | *on the second floor* | *in Berlin, in India* |
| *at the station, at 12 Park Road* | *on the left / right* | *in the car, in bed* |

### Movement and connection

- **to** (direction): *I go **to** the office.* — but *I go **home**.* (no *to*)
- **from** (origin): *I'm **from** India. The train **from** Munich is late.*
- **for** (purpose / duration): *a table **for** two · I waited **for** ten minutes.*
- **with / without**: *coffee **with** milk · I came **without** my keys.*
- **near / next to / between / opposite**: *The bank is **next to** the pharmacy, **between** the café and the post office.*

## Model sentences

- *I'm at home. The laptop is on my desk in the bedroom.*
- *I have a meeting at 10 on Monday.*
- *I'm going to the office by bus.*
- *I came to Germany from India in 2022.*
- *The pharmacy is next to the supermarket, opposite the bank.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I am in home.* | *I am at home.* | Fixed chunk: *at home*. |
| *I go to home.* | *I go home.* | *home* after a movement verb has no *to*. |
| *Meeting is on 10 o'clock.* | *The meeting is at 10 o'clock.* | Clock time → *at*. |
| *I go in office.* | *I go to the office.* | Direction → *to*. |
| *See you on next week.* | *See you next week.* | No preposition before *next / last / this*. |
| *I am in the bus.* | *I am on the bus.* | Buses, trains, planes → *on*; cars, taxis → *in*. |
| *I arrived to Berlin.* | *I arrived in Berlin.* | *arrive in* a city, *arrive at* a building. |

## Practice drills

1. **Ladder:** say one sentence each with *at + time, on + day, in + month*.
2. **Where is it?** Describe five objects in your room with *on, in, next to, between, under*.
3. **Travel line:** *I'm going **to** ___ **by** ___. The train leaves **from** ___ **at** ___.*
4. **Correction lab:** fix *in home, on 5 pm, in the bus, to home*.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] use *at / on / in* for time correctly
- [ ] describe where things are with *at / on / in / next to / between*
- [ ] talk about travel with *to, from, on the train, at the station*
- [ ] avoid *in home, to home, on next week*
