# Beginner Fluency

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** speak for 30-60 seconds about everyday topics in short, connected sentences.

Beginner fluency does **not** mean speaking fast. It means speaking in small, clear **chunks** without stopping after every word, and connecting them with simple words: *and, but, because, so, then*.

## The pattern

> **Main idea → one detail → a reason or example → a closing sentence.**
>
> *I live in Berlin (main idea). I have a small flat near the park (detail). I like it because it is quiet (reason). It's a good place for my family (closing).*

### Connectors you need

| Connector | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **and** | add | *I work from home, and my wife works in an office.* |
| **but** | contrast | *The weather is cold, but I like walking.* |
| **because** | reason | *I like Indian food because it's tasty.* |
| **so** | result | *It was late, so we took a taxi.* |
| **then / after that** | sequence | *I have breakfast, then I check my email.* |

## Vocabulary and meanings

| Word or phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **in my free time** | when I am not working or studying | *In my free time, I like reading.* |
| **spend time** | use time doing something or being with someone | *I spend time with my family in the evening.* |
| **enjoy + -ing** | like doing an activity | *I enjoy walking in the park.* |
| **be interested in** | want to know or learn more about something | *I'm interested in learning languages.* |
| **usually** | on most days or in most situations | *I usually cook dinner at home.* |
| **prefer A to B** | like A more than B | *I prefer the train to the bus.* |

For more expressions with meanings and examples at each level, see [Speaking Confidence: Vocabulary and Meanings](../vocabulary/speaking-confidence.mdx).

## Model answer (self-introduction, ~40 seconds)

> *Hi, my name is Alex, and I'm from Kochi in India. I live in Berlin with my wife and our son. I work as a software engineer, so I use English every day at work. In my free time I like cooking and walking in the park. I'm learning English because I want to speak more confidently in meetings.*

### See how the answer is built

| Job | Model phrase | Add your own information |
|---|---|---|
| Introduce yourself | *Hi, my name is Alex, and I'm from Kochi.* | name + city/country |
| Add a present detail | *I live in Berlin with my family.* | where you live + who with |
| Say what you do | *I work as a software engineer.* | job, studies, or daily role |
| Extend with a reason | *I use English every day at work, so I want to feel more confident in meetings.* | one reason English matters to you |
| Close with an interest | *In my free time, I enjoy cooking and walking.* | one or two real interests |

Pause between ideas, not between every word: *I'm Alex / from Kochi. // I live in Berlin / with my family.* The model is a starting point; change the details so the answer sounds like you.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Myself Alex.* | *My name is Alex.* / *I'm Alex.* | Direct translation; not used in English. |
| *I am having one child.* | *I have one child.* | Family relationships use *have* (not continuous). |
| *I like because tasty.* | *I like it because it's tasty.* | After *because*, a full clause: subject + verb. |
| *My home very good.* | *My home is very nice.* | Needs *is*; *nice / comfortable* sound more natural than *good* for a home. |
| *I no speak long.* | *I can't speak for a long time yet.* | *can't* + base verb. |
| *And then and then and then…* | *First… Then… After that… Finally…* | Vary your connectors. |

## Practice drills

1. **Four-sentence answer:** pick a topic from the table and use *main idea → detail → reason → closing*.
2. **Stretch it:** say one sentence, then make it longer twice: *I like tea. → I like tea because it's relaxing. → I like tea because it's relaxing, so I drink it every evening.*
3. **Add a follow-up:** after your answer, ask *What about you?* or *Do you like that too?* and answer the other person's reply.
4. **Retell with one change:** replace one detail (the place, person, time, or reason) and say the answer again without reading it.
5. **Timer:** speak for 30 seconds, then 45, then up to 60. A short pause is fine; continue with your next idea rather than restarting.
6. **Repeat after correction:** say the full improved answer again, then use the corrected pattern in a new sentence.

## Guided speaking workout (15 minutes)

### Round 1 — Build from keywords (3 minutes)

Use only these notes: **weekend · cook with my brother · pasta · relaxing**. Make four connected sentences.

> *At the weekend, I cook with my brother. We usually make pasta. I enjoy cooking because it's relaxing. After that, we watch a film.*

Now change two details and speak again: choose a different person, activity, or reason.

### Round 2 — Answer and ask back (3 minutes)

Practise this exchange aloud. Cover the model answer and use your own details on the second try.

> **A:** What do you like doing after work? <br />
> **B:** I usually go for a walk because it helps me relax. What about you? <br />
> **A:** I like listening to music. Where do you usually walk? <br />
> **B:** Near my home, in a small park.

### Round 3 — Topic cards (5 minutes)

Choose one card. Give a 30-second answer, answer the follow-up, then choose a different card.

| Topic | Main question | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Which room do you like most? | What do you usually do there? |
| Work or study | What does a normal day look like? | What is the best part of it? |
| Food | What do you enjoy eating or cooking? | Who do you usually share it with? |
| Travel | Tell me about a place you visited. | What would you recommend doing there? |
| Family or friends | Who do you spend time with? | What do you enjoy doing together? |
| Weather | What weather do you like best? | What do you do on a rainy day? |

### Round 4 — One-minute checkpoint (4 minutes)

Pick a topic you have **not** just practised. Take 20 seconds to note three keywords. Speak for up to one minute using at least one detail, one reason, and one closing thought. If you get stuck, use *Let me think…*, *For example…*, or *What I like is…*.

After speaking, check: Did I communicate the main idea? Did I add a reason or example? Did I ask or answer a follow-up? Choose **one** thing to improve, then repeat the answer.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] introduce myself for 30-60 seconds
- [ ] talk about family, home, work, food, travel and weather in four connected sentences
- [ ] use *and, but, because, so, then* correctly
- [ ] repeat an improved version after correction
- [ ] answer a follow-up and ask one question back
- [ ] continue after a pause by using a familiar phrase

## Homework

On three different days this week, answer *"Tell me about yourself"* for up to one minute. Use no more than three keywords. Each time, keep one thing that worked and improve one thing; finish by answering a new follow-up question.
