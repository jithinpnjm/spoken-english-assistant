# Present Continuous

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** describe what people are doing now, and contrast *today* with *usually*.

The **present continuous** talks about an action happening now, around now, or only for a limited time. Compare:

- *I **work** from home.* — my normal routine (present simple)
- *I **am working** from the office today.* — only today, temporary (present continuous)

## The pattern

| Subject | be | verb + -ing |
|---|---|---|
| I | am ('m) | cooking |
| He / She / It | is ('s) | sleeping |
| You / We / They | are ('re) | waiting |

- **Negative:** *I'm not working today. She isn't listening.*
- **Question:** *Are you coming? What is he doing?*

### Spelling of -ing

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| most verbs: + ing | work → working, eat → eating |
| silent -e: drop e | make → making, write → writing |
| short vowel + consonant: double | run → running, sit → sitting, stop → stopping |
| -ie → -ying | lie → lying, die → dying |

### Verbs that are usually *not* continuous (stative verbs)

*know, understand, believe, like, love, hate, want, need, prefer, own, belong, seem, mean*

*I **know** the answer.* (not *I am knowing*) · *I **need** help.* (not *I am needing*)

## Model sentences

- *I'm cooking dinner right now — can I call you back?*
- *She's working from home today.*
- *We're waiting for the bus.*
- *What are you reading at the moment?*
- *I usually drink tea, but today I'm having coffee.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I cooking now.* | *I am cooking now.* | Needs *am/is/are*. |
| *She is cook dinner.* | *She is cooking dinner.* | Needs *-ing*. |
| *I am usually drink tea.* | *I usually drink tea.* | Habits use present simple. |
| *I am knowing him.* | *I know him.* | *know* is stative. |
| *What you are doing?* | *What are you doing?* | Question: *be* before the subject. |
| *He is siting.* | *He is sitting.* | Short vowel + consonant → double. |

## Practice drills

1. **Now:** describe three things people around you are doing right now.
2. **Today vs usually:** *I usually ___, but today I'm ___.*
3. **Picture talk:** describe a busy street photo in five present-continuous sentences.
4. **Correction lab:** fix *She is sleep*, *They watching TV*, *I am wanting a coffee*.
