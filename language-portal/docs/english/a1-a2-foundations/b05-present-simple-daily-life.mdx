# Present Simple for Daily Life

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** talk about your daily routine, your family's routine, and your workday clearly.

The **present simple** talks about things that happen regularly (habits, routines, schedules) or are generally true (facts). It is *not* for actions happening right now — that is the present continuous (next module).

## The pattern

| | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | *I **work** from home.* | *I **don't work** on Sundays.* | ***Do** you **work** on Sundays?* |
| he / she / it | *She **works** from home.* | *She **doesn't work** on Sundays.* | ***Does** she **work** on Sundays?* |

After **do / does / don't / doesn't** the verb goes back to its base form: *Does she **work**?* — never *Does she works?*

### Spelling of he/she/it + s

| Verb ending | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| most verbs | + s | work → works, eat → eats |
| -ch, -sh, -x, -ss, -o | + es | watch → watches, go → goes, fix → fixes |
| consonant + y | y → ies | study → studies, carry → carries |
| vowel + y | + s | play → plays, say → says |
| have | irregular | have → **has** |

### Frequency adverbs

*always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never*

They go **before the main verb** but **after be**: *I usually cook dinner.* · *She is always on time.*

## Model sentences

- *I wake up at seven and usually have tea.*
- *She works from home on Mondays and Fridays.*
- *We eat dinner together every evening.*
- *Do you drink coffee? — Yes, I do.*
- *He doesn't eat meat, so we often cook vegetarian food.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *She work from home.* | *She works from home.* | he/she/it + **s**. |
| *Do she cook?* | *Does she cook?* | *does* with he/she/it. |
| *Does she cooks?* | *Does she cook?* | Base verb after *does*. |
| *I am go to work every day.* | *I go to work every day.* | Present simple has no *am/is/are*. |
| *She don't like it.* | *She doesn't like it.* | *doesn't* with he/she/it. |
| *I go always by bus.* | *I always go by bus.* | Frequency adverb before the main verb. |

## Practice drills

1. **Frame:** *I usually ___ · My partner usually ___* (watch the -s).
2. **Question chain:** turn each sentence into a question: *She works late.* → *Does she work late?*
3. **Negative swap:** say one thing you *don't* do on weekdays and one thing your partner *doesn't* do.
4. **Routine in five:** describe your morning in five sentences with *first, then, after that*.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] describe my daily routine in five sentences
- [ ] use -s with he/she/it every time
- [ ] ask and answer *do/does* questions
- [ ] use frequency adverbs in the right position
