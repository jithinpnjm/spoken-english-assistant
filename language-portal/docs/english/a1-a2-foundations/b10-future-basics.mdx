# Future Basics

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** talk about tomorrow, next week, plans, and appointments — and make simple decisions and offers.

English has no single "future tense". You choose the form by asking **when did I decide, and how fixed is it?**

## The pattern

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **going to** + verb | a plan you already decided | *I'm going to visit my sister next week.* |
| **will** + verb | a decision made now, an offer, a promise | *It's heavy — I'll help you. I'll call you later.* |
| **present continuous** | an arrangement with a fixed time/place | *I'm seeing the doctor at 3 tomorrow.* |
| **want to / need to / have to** + verb | intention / necessity / obligation | *I need to book an appointment. I have to work on Saturday.* |

### Quick decision tree

1. Did you decide just now? → **will** (*The phone's ringing — I'll get it.*)
2. Is it in the calendar with a time? → **present continuous** (*We're meeting at 10.*)
3. Did you decide before? → **going to** (*I'm going to learn to drive.*)

**Forms to watch:** *will* + base verb (no *to*): *I will call* · *going to* needs *am/is/are*: *I **am** going to call*.

## Model sentences

- *I'm going to visit my friend tomorrow.*
- *I'll call you later, I promise.*
- *I need to book an appointment with the dentist.*
- *We're travelling to Munich next week — the tickets are booked.*
- *Will you come to the party on Saturday?*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I going to market tomorrow.* | *I'm going to the market tomorrow.* | *going to* needs *am/is/are*. |
| *I will to call you.* | *I will call you.* | No *to* after *will*. |
| *Tomorrow I go doctor.* | *Tomorrow I'm going to the doctor.* | A planned appointment → present continuous / going to. |
| *I will going.* | *I will go.* | *will* + base verb. |
| *I must to work.* | *I have to work.* / *I must work.* | No *to* after *must*. |
| *See you in next week.* | *See you next week.* | No preposition before *next*. |

## Practice drills

1. **Plan:** *Tomorrow, I'm going to ___.* Make three sentences.
2. **Quick decision:** react to *It's raining!* / *The printer is broken.* with *I'll…*.
3. **Calendar:** read three appointments from your calendar with the present continuous.
4. **Obligation:** say two things you *have to* do and one thing you *want to* do this week.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] talk about plans with *going to*
- [ ] make offers and quick decisions with *will*
- [ ] describe fixed appointments with the present continuous
- [ ] book a simple appointment and ask future questions
