# Basic Sentence Order

**Level:** A1-A2 · **Goal:** make short, complete sentences without missing verbs or translating word by word.

English shows *who does what to whom* mostly through **word order**. *The dog bit the man* and *The man bit the dog* use the same words and mean completely different things. That is why the order is fixed.

## The pattern

> **Subject + Verb + Object + (Place) + (Time)**
> *I eat breakfast at home every morning.*

| Rule | Right | Wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Every sentence has a subject and a verb | *It is cold.* | *Is cold.* |
| Object comes after the verb | *I drink coffee.* | *I coffee drink.* |
| Adjective comes before the noun | *a red car* | *a car red* |
| Place before time at the end | *I work in Berlin on Mondays.* | *I work on Mondays in Berlin.* (possible, but less natural) |
| Frequency adverb before the main verb | *I always walk to work.* | *I walk always to work.* |

## Lessons in this module

| # | Lesson | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subject + verb + object | *She likes music. We need help.* |
| 2 | Adjectives before nouns | *a small flat, a new job, good food* |
| 3 | Adverbs in simple sentences | *I usually cook. She speaks slowly. He is always late.* |
| 4 | Making complete spoken sentences | *This food is very good.* (not *Very good this food.*) |
| 5 | Avoiding missing verbs | *My friend **is** working. The shop **is** closed.* |
| 6 | Avoiding word-by-word translation | *I have a question.* (not *I have one doubt.*) · *My name is…* (not *Myself…*) |
| 7 | Simple connectors: and, but | *I like tea, but my wife likes coffee.* |
| 8 | Basic sentence repair | Fix broken sentences into natural English order |

## Model sentences

- *I drink coffee in the morning.*
- *She likes music, and she plays the guitar.*
- *We live in a small flat in Berlin.*
- *The baby is sleeping.*
- *I need help today, but it isn't urgent.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I coffee drink.* | *I drink coffee.* | Subject + verb + object. |
| *Very good this food.* | *This food is very good.* | Subject + *be* + adjective. |
| *My friend working.* | *My friend is working.* | Continuous needs *am/is/are*. |
| *Is raining.* | *It is raining.* | English needs a subject, even *it*. |
| *I like very much English.* | *I like English very much.* | *very much* goes after the object. |
| *Myself Alex.* | *My name is Alex. / I'm Alex.* | Direct translation; not used in English. |

## Practice drills

1. **Order the words:** *coffee / I / drink / every day* → *I drink coffee every day.*
2. **Add an adjective:** *I have a car.* → *I have an old car.*
3. **Connect:** join two short sentences with *and* or *but*.
4. **Repair lab:** fix *Very tired I am today* and *Tomorrow meeting have I*.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] build sentences with subject + verb + object
- [ ] put adjectives before nouns and frequency adverbs before the main verb
- [ ] notice and add a missing verb or subject
- [ ] join two ideas with *and* or *but*
