# Intermediate Review

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Goal:** find *your* recurring mistakes, fix them with spaced review, and prove you can use the whole B1-B2 course in connected speech.

At this stage, progress comes less from new rules and more from **removing your personal recurring errors**. Most learners make the same five to ten mistakes again and again. This module shows you how to find them, how to review them so they stay fixed, and how to test yourself.

## Step 1 — Build your mistake map

Collect corrections from lessons, the AI coach, and colleagues. Group them by **type**, and count how often each appears:

| Mistake type | Example (wrong → right) | Count | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tense with finished time | *I have met him yesterday → I met him yesterday* | 6 | recurring |
| Articles | *I found issue → I found an issue* | 9 | recurring |
| Preposition chunks | *discuss about → discuss* | 3 | improving |
| Question order | *What you are doing? → What are you doing?* | 2 | new |
| he/she + s | *She work → She works* | 1 | mastered |

**Status meanings:**
- **new** — seen once
- **recurring** — seen two or more times without improvement
- **improving** — you corrected it yourself at least once
- **mastered** — you got it right three times in a row in review

## Step 2 — Review with spacing

Review a mistake **before you forget the fix** — and less often as it gets stable. This schedule worked well in the previous version of this course:

| Situation | Next review |
|---|---|
| Seen 5+ times, still happening | **tomorrow** |
| Seen 2-4 times | in **2 days** |
| Seen once | in **3 days** |
| Review attempt fully correct | in **5 days** |
| Review attempt partly correct | in **2 days** |
| Review attempt wrong | **tomorrow** |
| Mastered (3 correct reviews in a row) | in **14 days** — then only if it comes back |

**What a review looks like** (two minutes per mistake):
1. Read the correction and the one-line rule.
2. Write or say **one new sentence** using the same rule — about your own life.
3. Check it. Correct → it's improving. Wrong → back to tomorrow.

Always start with the item that is **due** and has the **highest count**.

## Mixed grammar self-test

Correct each sentence. (Answers at the bottom.)

1. *I am working here since 2021.*
2. *Can you tell me where is the meeting room?*
3. *We discussed about the budget in last meeting.*
4. *She don't have any informations about it.*
5. *If it will rain, we will cancel.*
6. *You mustn't come if you're busy — it's optional.*
7. *I have visited my parents last weekend.*
8. *He is responsible of the deployment.*
9. *Although it was late, but we continued.*
10. *I did a big mistake yesterday.*
