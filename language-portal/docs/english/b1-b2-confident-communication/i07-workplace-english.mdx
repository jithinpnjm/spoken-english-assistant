# Workplace English

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Goal:** give clear, professional updates and handle the ten most common speaking situations of a working week.

Workplace English is **structured** English. Every situation below has a shape — once you know the shape, you only need to fill in the facts. The shapes also keep you out of the two classic traps: vague updates (*"Status is going good"*) and blame (*"Not my mistake, platform team issue"*).

---

## 1 — Daily standup update

**Shape:** yesterday (past simple) → today (will / going to) → blocker or "no blockers" → next step.

- *Yesterday, I fixed the login issue and tested the change in staging.*
- *Today, I'll review the deployment logs and prepare the release checklist.*
- *My blocker is that I need access to the production dashboard.*
- *I'm on track, but I need clarification on the rollback plan.*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *Yesterday I am fixing one issue.* | *Yesterday, I fixed one issue.* |
| *Today I do release.* | *Today, I'll work on the release.* |
| *No blockers only confusion logs.* | *No blockers, but I need clarification on the logs.* |

Keep it under five sentences.

## 2 — Explaining blockers

**Shape:** what is blocked → why → impact → the exact help or decision you need.

- *I'm blocked because I don't have access to the production logs.*
- *This blocks the validation step, so the release may be delayed.*
- *Could someone confirm who owns the API gateway configuration?*

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I have blocker with logs.* | *I'm blocked because I can't access the logs.* | Say what is blocked and why. |
| *Security team not giving access, so I can't do anything.* | *I'm waiting for security approval before I can continue.* | Neutral, not blaming. |

## 3 — Status report

**Shape:** current state → what changed → risk → next action / next update time.

- *The deployment is in progress and the first validation passed.*
- *Staging is complete, but production validation is still pending.*
- *The main risk is a possible delay in security approval.*
- *I'll send another update by 4 p.m.*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *Status is going good.* | *The task is on track, and staging validation is complete.* |
| *Everything completed but some pending.* | *Most tasks are complete, but production validation is still pending.* |

## 4 — Asking for clarification

**Shape:** name the unclear part → ask a specific question → confirm your understanding.

- *Could you clarify what you mean by "rollback validation"?*
- *Do you mean we should deploy today, or prepare it for tomorrow?*
- *Just to confirm: we test in staging first, correct?*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *What you mean?* | *What do you mean?* |
| *Explain me this.* | *Could you explain this to me?* |

## 5 — Polite disagreement

**Shape:** acknowledge → state the concern with a reason → offer an alternative.

- *I understand the urgency, but I'm concerned about skipping the validation step.*
- *I see your point. My concern is that this could increase rollback risk.*
- *Could we deploy tomorrow morning instead, after smoke testing?*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *You are wrong, this won't work.* | *I see your point, but I have a concern about this approach.* |
| *This will not work only.* | *I'm concerned this may not work because the dependency is unresolved.* |

## 6 — Giving an estimate

**Shape:** number → assumption → confidence → what could change it.

- *I expect this to take around two hours, assuming the test environment is stable.*
- *My rough estimate is one day, but it depends on the API issue.*
- *I'm about 70% confident we can finish by tomorrow afternoon.*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *It will take two hours sure.* | *I expect it to take around two hours, assuming the environment is stable.* |
| *Maybe tomorrow maybe not.* | *It may be ready tomorrow, but it depends on the test results.* |

## 7 — Explaining a delay

**Shape:** reason → impact → what you are doing about it → new timeline.

- *The task is delayed because the test environment was unavailable this morning.*
- *This affects release validation, but not the code freeze.*
- *I'm working with the platform team to restore access. The revised timeline is tomorrow morning.*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *Not my mistake, platform team issue.* | *The delay is caused by an environment issue, and I'm coordinating with the platform team.* |
| *I will finish maybe later.* | *I expect to finish by tomorrow morning.* |

## 8 — Requesting support

**Shape:** context → exact request → urgency → what happens once you get it.

- *I need support with the API timeout issue before release validation.*
- *It's urgent because the release window closes at 5 p.m.*
- *If you can confirm the error pattern, I can make the rollback decision.*

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *Please help this issue.* | *Could you help me investigate this issue?* |
| *Can you check?* | *Could you check the error logs from 10:15 to 10:30?* |

## 9 — Summarising a meeting

**Shape:** decisions → action items → owners → deadlines → open questions.

- *Here's a quick summary. We decided to proceed with the staging deployment today.*
- *Priya will prepare the rollback checklist by 3 p.m.; Tom will confirm the test results by tomorrow morning.*
- *The open question is whether we need security approval before production.*

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I will do rollback checklist until 3 p.m.* | *I'll prepare the rollback checklist by 3 p.m.* | *by* = deadline. |
| *Decision is we are going deployment.* | *The decision is that we'll proceed with the deployment.* | Complete clause after *the decision is that*. |

## 10 — Workplace speaking checkpoint

Give a **60-90 second** update that includes: a standup, one blocker with a support request, one estimate with an assumption, and a two-line meeting summary.
