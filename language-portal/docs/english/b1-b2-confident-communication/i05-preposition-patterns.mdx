# Preposition Patterns

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Goal:** use natural preposition **chunks** instead of translating word by word.

At this level, most preposition errors are not about place and time — they are about **fixed patterns**: *responsible **for**, depend **on**, interested **in**, discuss ∅ the issue*. There is often no logic to learn, only chunks. Learn the whole chunk, in a sentence, and say it aloud until it sounds wrong any other way.

## The pattern

> **verb / adjective / noun + its preposition + object**
> *I'm **responsible for** the deployment. It **depends on** the budget. We need a **solution to** this problem.*

## Model sentences

- *I'm working **on** the migration and I'm responsible **for** the rollback plan.*
- *We discussed the issue **in** the meeting.* (no *about* after *discuss*)
- *The service is running **in** production.*
- *Please send it **by** Friday — I'll be **on** leave **from** Monday **until** Wednesday.*
- *We ran **into** a problem **with** the API, so we rolled **back**.*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I am working in this task.* | *I'm working on this task.* | *work **on** a task/project*. |
| *We discussed about the issue.* | *We discussed the issue.* | *discuss* takes a direct object. |
| *I am responsible of deployment.* | *I'm responsible for the deployment.* | *responsible **for***. |
| *It depends of the budget.* | *It depends on the budget.* | *depend **on***. |
| *Send it until Friday.* | *Send it by Friday.* | *by* = deadline; *until* = continues up to. |
| *I'm waiting since two hours.* | *I've been waiting for two hours.* | *for* + duration, present perfect continuous. |
| *He is good in English.* | *He is good at English.* | *good **at***. |
| *Explain me the process.* | *Explain the process to me.* | *explain something **to** someone*. |
| *I'm looking forward to meet you.* | *I'm looking forward to meeting you.* | *to* is a preposition here → -ing. |
| *Please revert back.* | *Please reply.* / *Please get back to me.* | *revert* means "go back to a previous state" outside South Asia. |

## Practice drills

1. **Chunk cards:** write ten chunks from lessons 4-6 as full sentences about your own work.
2. **By or until?** *I'll be in the office ___ 6. Please confirm ___ noon.*
3. **Phrasal verb swap:** replace the formal verb: *investigate → look into, continue → carry on, postpone → put off, cancel → call off.*
4. **Correction lab:** fix the ten mistakes in the table without looking.

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] use common verb, adjective and noun + preposition chunks automatically
- [ ] use *by / until / in / within / during* for time precisely
- [ ] use work phrasal verbs (*follow up, roll back, run into, figure out*) naturally
- [ ] avoid *discuss about, explain me, depend of, responsible of*
