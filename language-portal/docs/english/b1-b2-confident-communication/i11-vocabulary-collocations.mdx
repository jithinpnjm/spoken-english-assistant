# Vocabulary and Collocations

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Goal:** sound natural by using words in the combinations native speakers use.

A **collocation** is a pair of words that "go together": we *make a decision* but *do homework*, *take a break* but *have lunch*, *heavy traffic* not *strong traffic*. Using the wrong partner is rarely a grammar error, but it instantly sounds unnatural. The fix is to learn **chunks**, not single words.

## The pattern

> **Learn the word with its partner, in a sentence you would actually say.**
> Not *"decision = Entscheidung"* but *"We need to **make a decision** by Friday."*

### Make vs do

| make — create, produce, cause | do — tasks, work, activities |
|---|---|
| *make a decision, make a mistake, make a plan, make an effort, make progress, make a call, make money, make dinner, make a complaint, make a suggestion* | *do homework, do the dishes, do a course, do business, do research, do your best, do a favour, do exercise, do the shopping, do damage* |

### Say / tell / speak / talk

| Verb | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **say** | say something (to someone) | *She said (to me) that she was tired.* |
| **tell** | tell **someone** something | *She told me that she was tired. Tell me a story. Tell the truth.* |
| **speak** | speak a language; formal talk | *I speak Malayalam. Can I speak to the manager?* |
| **talk** | conversation | *We talked about the project. Can we talk later?* |

*She said me…* ✗ → *She told me…* ✓ · *Can you tell to him?* ✗ → *Can you tell him?* ✓

### Get / take / have

| get (receive, become, arrive) | take (time, transport, action) | have (experience, meals, events) |
|---|---|---|
| *get an email, get better, get home, get ready, get lost* | *take the bus, take a break, take time, take notes, take responsibility* | *have lunch, have a meeting, have a good time, have a look, have a problem* |

## Vocabulary upgrade lab

Weak words aren't wrong — they're vague. Upgrade them when precision matters:

| Weak | Stronger (in context) |
|---|---|
| *do the work* | *complete / handle / carry out the work* |
| *very big problem* | *a serious / major / critical problem* |
| *very good result* | *an excellent / outstanding result* |
| *say sorry* | *apologise* |
| *look at the data* | *analyse / review / examine the data* |
| *get better* | *improve* |
| *find out* (formal writing) | *identify / determine / establish* |
| *help* (a process) | *support / facilitate / enable* |
| *think about* (options) | *consider / weigh up* |
| *strong traffic, strong rain* | *heavy traffic, heavy rain* |

Choose the upgrade that fits the register: *Let's look at the numbers* is perfect in a meeting; *We analysed the figures* fits a report.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| *I did a mistake.* | *I made a mistake.* |
| *We need to do a decision.* | *We need to make a decision.* |
| *She said me the answer.* | *She told me the answer.* |
| *He talks English.* | *He speaks English.* |
| *I'll make a shower.* | *I'll take / have a shower.* |
| *strong rain* | *heavy rain* |
| *I attended to the meeting.* | *I attended the meeting.* |

## Practice drills

1. **Make or do?** *___ a phone call, ___ the laundry, ___ a profit, ___ a presentation (make or give), ___ your homework.*
2. **Say / tell / speak / talk:** fill five gaps from your own week.
3. **Collocation diary:** write three sentences a day using one work, one travel and one money collocation.
4. **Upgrade:** rewrite *"I did the work and it was very good and the boss said it was nice."*
