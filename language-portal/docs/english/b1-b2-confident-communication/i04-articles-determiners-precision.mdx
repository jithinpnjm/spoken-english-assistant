# Articles and Determiners

**Level:** B1-B2 · **Goal:** sound precise when talking about one item, specific items, general things, and quantities.

Articles and determiners tell the listener whether something is **new or known**, **specific or general**, **singular, plural, or an amount**. Missing or extra articles are among the most noticed errors for speakers of languages without articles — Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Russian, Chinese and many others. The good news: most errors come from a small set of rules.

## The core logic

| Question | Answer → article |
|---|---|
| Is it a singular countable noun? | It **must** have *a/an/the* or another determiner (*my, this, each…*). Never bare. |
| Does the listener know *which one*? | Yes → **the** · No, one of many / first mention → **a/an** |
| Are you talking about things **in general**? | Plural or uncountable → **no article** (*Dogs are loyal. Information is power.*) |

**The camera test:** can the listener point their camera at the exact thing you mean? Yes → *the*. No → *a/an*. No specific thing at all → no article.

## Model sentences

- *I found **an** issue in the configuration.*
- ***The** issue started after **the** deployment on Friday.*
- *This approach is simpler than **the** previous one.*
- *We need **some** time to verify the fix.*
- *There are **a few** changes in **the** release, but **few** of them are risky.*

## Rules that fix most errors

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Jobs take *a/an* | *She's **an** architect.* |
| First mention *a*, then *the* | *I bought **a** laptop. **The** laptop is great.* |
| Superlatives and *same / only / next / last / main* take *the* | ***the** best option, **the** same problem, **the** only way* |
| Uncountables never take *a/an* | *some advice, information, feedback, equipment, news, research, software* |
| Most countries and cities: no article | *India, Germany, Munich* — but ***the** UK, **the** US, **the** Netherlands* |
| *by* + transport: no article | *by train, by car* — but ***on** foot* |
| Sounds decide *a/an* | ***an** hour, **an** MBA, **a** university, **a** European team* |

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I found issue.* | *I found an issue.* | Singular countable noun needs a determiner. |
| *Deployment caused problem, problem is fixed.* | *The deployment caused a problem, and the problem is fixed.* | *a* on first mention, *the* after. |
| *I need an information.* | *I need some information.* | *information* is uncountable. |
| *She is best engineer in team.* | *She is the best engineer in the team.* | Superlatives and specific groups take *the*. |
| *I go to office by the car.* | *I go to the office by car.* | Specific office → *the*; *by* + transport → no article. |
| *The life is busy.* | *Life is busy.* | General abstract noun → no article. |
| *Every releases need tests.* | *Every release needs tests.* | *every / each* + singular noun and singular verb. |

## Practice drills

1. **Choose:** *a / an / the / — / some / any* in: *We had ___ outage last night. ___ outage lasted ___ hour. Do you have ___ logs?*
2. **Describe your desk:** use *a, the, some, this, these* in five sentences.
3. **Few vs a few:** make one positive and one negative sentence about your team's ideas or time.
4. **Repair:** fix *I am working in software company since two years, it is best company.*

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] never leave a singular countable noun without a determiner
- [ ] switch from *a* to *the* after first mention
- [ ] use no article for general plurals and uncountables
- [ ] use *the* with superlatives, *same / only / next*, and with rivers, ranges and plural countries
- [ ] use *each / every / all* and *a few / few / a little / little* correctly
