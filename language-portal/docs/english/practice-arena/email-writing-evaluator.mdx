# Email Writing Evaluator

Pick a task, write the email, get it evaluated, rewrite it. Structure and phrases are in [Professional Emails](../professional/emails.mdx).

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Subject line and first two lines make the purpose clear |
| **Structure** | context → point → reason → action with owner and deadline |
| **Tone** | Polite and direct; no blame; register fits the reader |
| **Clarity** | Specific dates, numbers and names; no vague *ASAP / some issues* |
| **Accuracy** | Grammar, articles, prepositions, spelling |
| **Length** | As short as possible while complete |

## Task ideas

1. Tell a client their delivery will be one week late and offer two options.
2. Ask your manager for two days of training budget.
3. Follow up politely on an unanswered request from last week.
4. Decline a meeting invitation and propose an alternative.
5. Summarise a meeting's decisions and action items for people who missed it.
6. Apologise to a customer for a billing error and explain the fix.
