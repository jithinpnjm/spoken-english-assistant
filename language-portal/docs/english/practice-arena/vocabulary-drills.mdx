# Vocabulary Drills

Knowing a word is not the same as **using** it. These four drill formats move a word from "I recognise it" to "I say it without thinking". Use them with any list — the word bank below, your [collocation notebook](../b1-b2-confident-communication/i11-vocabulary-collocations.mdx), or words from your job.

## Four drill formats

| Format | How it works | Trains |
|---|---|---|
| **Fill the gap** | Hear or read a sentence with the word missing: *She worked hard to ___ her goal.* → *achieve* | recognition in context |
| **Choose the right word** | Pick from three options, two of them plausible: *resolve / reveal / reduce the conflict* | precision |
| **Use it in a sentence** | Make your own sentence with the word; get it corrected | active use |
| **Word quiz** | Hear the meaning, recall the word: *"to examine something very carefully"* → *scrutinise* | fast recall |

**A good daily set:** 10 words — 3 basic, 4 intermediate, 3 advanced — going through them in order and ending with a short summary of which words to review. Review missed words using the [spacing schedule](../b1-b2-confident-communication/i12-intermediate-review.mdx).

## Word bank (high-value words for work and study)

**Basic:** *achieve, assist, benefit, challenge, confident, decide, describe, discuss, encourage, explain, focus, improve, include, increase, manage, mention, notice, offer, organise, prepare, progress, provide, reduce, require, solve, suggest, support.*

**Intermediate:** *accomplish, acknowledge, adequate, analyse, anticipate, assume, cautious, clarify, collaborate, consequence, consistent, contribute, convince, crucial, demonstrate, drawback, efficient, emphasise, enhance, evaluate, exceed, flexible, implement, indicate, inevitable, negotiate, objective, obstacle, outcome, overcome, perspective, prioritise, propose, relevant, reliable, reluctant, resolve, scenario, significant, strategy, summarise, sustain, thorough, transition, underestimate, verify.*

**Advanced:** *advocate, alleviate, ambiguous, articulate, coherent, concede, concise, contradict, diminish, entail, exacerbate, exhaustive, facilitate, foster, implication, inherent, intrinsic, meticulous, nuance, pragmatic, proficient, reconcile, relentless, scrutinise, speculate, subsequent, synthesise, tangible, undermine, unprecedented, viable.*

For each word you learn, write **one sentence about your own life** — for example *We need a **viable** plan before Friday* — not a dictionary definition.

## AI Practice

Tell the coach which format you want (or let it choose) and, optionally, paste your own word list.

   - Use-in-a-sentence: give the word and meaning; the learner makes a sentence; correct grammar and word use.
   - Word quiz: give only the meaning; the learner recalls the word; hint with the first letter if stuck.
4. Feedback rules:
   - After all 10 words, summarise: how many were correct and which words to review tomorrow. Offer to replay the missed words or switch format.
   - Correct grammar in the learner's sentences as well as word choice.`}
