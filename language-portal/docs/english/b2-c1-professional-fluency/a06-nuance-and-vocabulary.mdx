# Nuance and Vocabulary

**Level:** B2-C1 · **Goal:** choose words that say *exactly* what you mean — stronger when you need impact, softer when you need diplomacy, shorter when you need clarity.

At C1, vocabulary is less about knowing more words and more about **choosing**: *improve* vs *optimise* vs *streamline*; *problem* vs *issue* vs *blocker* vs *risk*; *We will* vs *We're likely to*.

## Lessons in this module

### 1 — Strong vs weak verbs

| Weak | Strong (choose by meaning) |
|---|---|
| *do* | *complete, carry out, deliver, handle, execute, conduct* |
| *make better* | *improve, strengthen, optimise, streamline, refine* |
| *look at* | *review, analyse, assess, examine, investigate* |
| *get* | *obtain, secure, gain, receive, achieve* |
| *say* | *state, point out, emphasise, confirm, suggest, argue* |
| *help* | *support, enable, facilitate, assist* |
| *show* | *demonstrate, indicate, illustrate, reveal* |

*I did the migration* → *I led the migration* (ownership) → *I delivered the migration two weeks early* (impact).

### 2 — Precise adjectives

*big problem* → *a major / serious / critical / widespread problem* · *good result* → *a strong / solid / measurable result* · *hard task* → *a complex / demanding / time-consuming task*. Pick the adjective that says **how** it is hard, big, or good.

### 3 — Collocations for leadership

*set priorities · take ownership · build consensus · drive change · manage expectations · delegate responsibility · raise concerns · give constructive feedback · make a judgement call · reach a compromise*

### 4 — Idiomatic workplace phrases (use, but don't overuse)

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *on my radar* | I'm aware of it and tracking it |
| *touch base* | have a short check-in |
| *take this offline* | discuss separately, not in this meeting |
| *on the same page* | shared understanding |
| *keep someone in the loop* | keep them informed |
| *a quick win* | an easy, fast improvement |
| *move the needle* | make a noticeable difference |

One or two per meeting sounds natural; five sounds like a parody.

### 5 — Hedging language

Hedging shows you are **careful, not uncertain** — expected in reports, estimates and analysis.

| Direct | Hedged |
|---|---|
| *This will solve the problem.* | *This is likely to address most of the issue.* |
| *The product failed because of poor quality.* | *The decline may be partly attributable to quality concerns.* |
| *Customers don't like the design.* | *Customer feedback suggests some dissatisfaction with the design.* |
| *We must change this immediately.* | *It would be advisable to review this as a priority.* |

Tools: *likely, probably, potentially, appears to, seems to, tends to, suggests, to some extent, in most cases.*

### 6 — Diplomatic disagreement vocabulary

*I'm not entirely convinced… · I have some reservations about… · I wonder whether… · That may be true, but… · I see it slightly differently. · My concern would be…*

### 7 — Cause-effect language

*X led to / resulted in / caused / triggered Y* · *Y was caused by / resulted from / was due to X* · *As a result, … Consequently, …*

*The config change **triggered** the outage.* · *The delay **resulted from** a missing approval.*

### 8 — Priority and urgency language

*This is a top priority. · This is time-sensitive. · This can wait until next sprint. · This is a nice-to-have, not a must-have. · This is blocking the release. · Let's deprioritise X in favour of Y.*

### 9 — Concise alternatives

| Wordy | Concise |
|---|---|
| *at this point in time* | *now* |
| *in order to* | *to* |
| *due to the fact that* | *because* |
| *make a decision about* | *decide* |
| *in the event that* | *if* |
| *it is important to note that* | (delete) |

### 10 — Vocabulary nuance checkpoint

Rewrite a vague one-paragraph update using stronger verbs, precise adjectives, one hedge, one cause-effect link, and no wordy phrases.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I did many things in the project.* | *I owned the deployment automation and cut release time by 40%.* | Specific verb + measurable result. |
| *It is very very important.* | *It's critical.* | Precise word instead of intensifiers. |
| *We will surely finish by Friday.* | *We're on track to finish by Friday.* | *surely* can sound uncertain; be accurate. |
| *The outage was because of the config was wrong.* | *The outage was caused by an incorrect configuration.* | Clean cause-effect structure. |
| *Kindly do the needful.* | *Could you update the ticket, please?* | Name the actual action. |

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] replace weak verbs and adjectives with precise ones
- [ ] use leadership collocations and a few idioms naturally
- [ ] hedge claims in reports and estimates
- [ ] express cause and effect clearly and cut wordy phrases
