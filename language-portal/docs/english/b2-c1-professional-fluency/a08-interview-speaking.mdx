# Interview Speaking

**Level:** B2-C1 · **Goal:** answer interview questions with context, action, result and reflection — not vague or memorised answers.

Interview English is not only grammar. It is the skill of explaining your **experience, decisions, impact, and learning** as a clear professional story. Interviewers listen for ownership (*I* vs *we*), specifics (numbers, technologies, scale), and self-awareness.

## The pattern

> **Direct answer → context → action → result → reflection or link to the role.**

## Lessons in this module

| # | Lesson | Answer shape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about yourself | Present role → key experience → a highlight → why this role (60-90 s) |
| 2 | Complex project explanation | Goal → your part → the hard problem → how you solved it → impact |
| 3 | Conflict example | Situation → the disagreement → how you handled it → outcome → learning |
| 4 | Failure and learning story | What went wrong → your responsibility → what you changed |
| 5 | Strengths and gaps | Strength + evidence · gap + what you're doing about it |
| 6 | Leadership example | Influence without authority, decisions, supporting others |
| 7 | Cross-functional collaboration | Different goals → alignment → result |
| 8 | System design explanation | Requirements → high-level components → trade-offs → scaling |
| 9 | Salary and expectation discussion | Range + reasoning, flexibility, total package |
| 10 | Interview checkpoint | A 15-minute mock interview |

## Model answers

**Tell me about yourself (opening):**
> *I'm a platform engineer with seven years' experience building reliable cloud infrastructure. At my current company, I lead the team that runs our Kubernetes platform, and last year I led a migration that cut our hosting costs by 30%. I'm interested in this role because it combines platform work with more ownership of developer experience.*

**Failure story (ending):**
> *…The release went out without a rollback plan, and we had a two-hour outage. That was my responsibility. Since then, I've made a tested rollback step mandatory in our release checklist, and we haven't had a similar incident.*

## Useful phrases

| Purpose | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Ownership | *I led… · I was responsible for… · My role was to… · I decided to…* |
| Results | *As a result, … · This reduced / increased … by … · The outcome was…* |
| Reflection | *What I learned was… · Looking back, I would… · Since then, I always…* |
| Buying time | *That's a good question — let me think of the best example.* |
| Clarifying | *Do you mean in terms of technical leadership or people management?* |
| Gaps | *I don't have direct experience with X, but I've worked with Y, which is similar because…* |
| Salary | *Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for a range of … I'm open to discussing the overall package.* |

## Questions to ask the interviewer

- *What does success look like in this role after six months?*
- *What are the biggest challenges the team is facing right now?*
- *How is the team structured, and who would I work with most closely?*
- *What are the next steps in the process?*

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *Myself I am working as engineer.* | *I'm an engineer with experience in cloud platforms and reliability.* | *I am*, not *Myself*; *an* before a vowel sound. |
| *I did many things in that project.* | *I owned the deployment automation and improved release validation.* | Specific responsibility and impact. |
| *It was very challenging but somehow completed.* | *It was challenging because of dependency risk, so I created a phased rollout plan and delivered it on time.* | Challenge → action → outcome; never *somehow*. |
| *We did…, we did…* (for your own work) | *I did…* | Show your contribution. |
| *My weakness is I work too hard.* | A real, minor gap + what you're doing about it | Clichés sound rehearsed. |
| *I am having 7 years experience.* | *I have seven years' experience.* | *have* is stative; *years'* (possessive). |

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] give a 60-90 second answer to *Tell me about yourself*
- [ ] tell conflict, failure and leadership stories with a clear result and reflection
- [ ] explain a complex project or system design at the right level
- [ ] talk about strengths, gaps and salary confidently
- [ ] ask the interviewer good questions
