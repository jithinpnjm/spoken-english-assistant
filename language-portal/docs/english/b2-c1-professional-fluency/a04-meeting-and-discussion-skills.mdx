# Meeting and Discussion Skills

**Level:** B2-C1 · **Goal:** contribute to senior meetings with clarity and diplomacy — get into the conversation, challenge safely, and move the group to a decision.

Advanced meeting English is about **controlling the flow of discussion**: entering at the right moment, clarifying ambiguity, challenging without creating conflict, summarising decisions, and leaving with clear actions. Many capable people stay silent in meetings not because of grammar but because they don't have ready-made *entry* and *steering* phrases.

## The pattern

> **Position → reason → evidence or concern → suggested action → check alignment.**
>
> *I'd like to add one concern before we decide (entry). I think the timeline is too tight (position) because the security review usually takes a week (reason, evidence). Could we plan the launch for the 20th instead? (action) Does that work for everyone? (alignment)*

## Running a meeting (when you chair)

| Stage | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Open | *Let's get started. The goal today is to decide…* |
| Agenda | *We have three items: …, …, and …. We have 30 minutes.* |
| Hand over | *Priya, could you take us through the numbers?* |
| Keep on track | *We're running short on time — let's focus on the decision.* |
| Invite quiet voices | *Tom, we haven't heard from you yet — what's your view?* |
| Close | *To wrap up: we decided…, the actions are…, and I'll send the notes by end of day.* |

## Model sentences

- *I'd like to add one concern before we decide.*
- *My understanding is that the main risk is the dependency on the release window.*
- *Could we clarify the assumption behind that timeline?*
- *I agree with the direction, but I think we should validate the rollback path first.*
- *To summarise, we've agreed to test this in staging and review the results tomorrow.*

## Agreeing and disagreeing — a range

| Strong agreement | Partial | Polite disagreement |
|---|---|---|
| *I'm fully on board with that.* | *I agree up to a point.* | *I take your point, but I see a risk.* |
| *That's exactly my thinking.* | *That makes sense in principle, but…* | *I'm not entirely convinced — here's why.* |
| *Absolutely — let's go with that.* | *I'd support that if we…* | *With respect, I see it slightly differently.* |

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| *I want to say something, this is wrong.* | *I'd like to add one concern about this approach.* | Enter diplomatically; focus on the approach. |
| *We discussed many things, so okay.* | *To summarise, we agreed on the rollout plan; the next action is staging validation.* | A summary states the decision and the next action. |
| *What is assumption?* | *Could we clarify the assumption behind this estimate?* | Complete, specific clarification question. |
| *Please wait, I am talking.* | *If I could just finish this point…* | Hold the floor politely. |
| *Everyone agree?* | *Is everyone comfortable with that?* / *Does anyone see it differently?* | Complete question; invite dissent. |

## Checkpoint: I can…

- [ ] enter a discussion and hold the floor politely
- [ ] challenge an idea or assumption without sounding aggressive
- [ ] handle an objection and park an off-topic issue
- [ ] summarise decisions and close with owners and deadlines
- [ ] chair a short meeting from opening to wrap-up
