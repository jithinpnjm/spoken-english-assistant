# Mastery Review

**Level:** B2-C1 · **Goal:** consolidate the professional course, prove it under realistic pressure, and leave with a concrete plan for the next 60 days.

## Lessons in this module

| # | Lesson | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personal advanced error map | Update your mistake map (see [Intermediate Review](../b1-b2-confident-communication/i12-intermediate-review.mdx)) with C1-level categories: register, precision, hedging, stress |
| 2 | Mixed tense precision | Explain a project history with past, present perfect, past perfect, conditionals |
| 3 | Advanced roleplay | Handle a difficult client with diplomacy |
| 4 | Professional simulation | Status update + escalation + decision framing |
| 5 | Live incident simulation | Explain an incident to a manager while it is still happening |
| 6 | Presentation simulation | Three-minute presentation + tough Q&A |
| 7 | Interview simulation | Senior-level mock interview |
| 8 | Fast correction challenge | Fix ten sentences in two minutes |
| 9 | Final speaking assessment | 15-minute mixed session with the coach |
| 10 | Next 60-day plan | Build your plan from the assessment results |

## C1 self-assessment

Rate yourself 1-5 on each skill after the simulations:

| Skill | Evidence of 5/5 |
|---|---|
| Grammar precision | Conditionals, past modals, passive and reported speech used correctly under pressure |
| Register | Adjusts directness; delivers bad news in four parts; no blame language |
| Technical explanation | Impact first, then mechanism, then action; adapts to audience |
| Meetings | Enters, challenges, parks topics, summarises with owners |
| Fluency | 2 minutes, few fillers, signposted, recovers from missing words |
| Vocabulary | Strong verbs, precise adjectives, hedging where appropriate |
| Pronunciation | Correct stress on key technical words; confident falling intonation |

## Fast correction challenge

Fix each sentence (answers below):

1. *If the deployment will fail, we rollback.*
2. *He must have forget to update the ticket.*
3. *I suggest you to review the plan.*
4. *The root cause was because the config was wrong.*
5. *We discussed about the budget and she said me it is approved.*
6. *I am having ten years experience in this domain.*
7. *Kindly revert with the needful.*
8. *The outage was happened at 3 a.m.*
9. *If we would have tested it, we would catch it.*
10. *I have joined this company in 2019.*

## Your next 60 days

The previous version of this course used a 60-day challenge with weekly themes. Use this structure to plan your own practice (15-20 minutes a day):

| Weeks | Theme | Daily practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation habits | Mistake map review + one AI practice block |
| 2 | Grammar for speaking | Your top two grammar error types, one drill each day |
| 3 | Real-world scenarios | One roleplay a day from [Real-Life Roleplay](../b1-b2-confident-communication/i08-real-life-roleplay.mdx) or [Practice Arena](../practice-arena/voice-negotiation-simulator.mdx) |
| 4 | Workplace speaking | Prepare every standup; one "professional register" rewrite a day |
| 5 | Fluency and storytelling | One 2-minute recording a day; count fillers |
| 6 | Correction and precision | Fast correction challenges; vocabulary upgrades |
| 7 | Pressure practice | Interview and presentation simulations |
| 8-9 | Consolidation | Repeat the final assessment and compare |

Rules that make the plan work:
- **Every day produces something:** a recording, a rewrite, or an AI practice block. Reading alone doesn't count.
- **Every correction becomes a rewrite.** Never just read a correction.
- **Review mistakes on the spacing schedule** — tomorrow, in 2 days, in 5 days, in 14 days.
