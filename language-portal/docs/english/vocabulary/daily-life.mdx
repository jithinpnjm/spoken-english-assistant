# Daily Life Vocabulary

Word banks for the situations in [Everyday Functions](../a1-a2-foundations/b11-everyday-functions.mdx) and [Real-Life Roleplay](../b1-b2-confident-communication/i08-real-life-roleplay.mdx). Learn words **in the phrase you'll use**, not alone. Where British and American English differ, both are given (UK / US).

## Essential vocabulary and meanings

| Word or phrase | Meaning | Natural example |
|---|---|---|
| **hallway** | a passage inside a home that connects rooms or the entrance | *Leave your shoes in the hallway, please.* |
| **wardrobe** (UK) / **closet** (US) | a space or piece of furniture for storing clothes | *My coat is in the wardrobe.* |
| **appliance** | a machine used at home, especially for cooking or cleaning | *The shop repaired our kitchen appliance.* |
| **savoury** (UK) / **savory** (US) | salty or spicy rather than sweet | *I usually prefer savoury snacks.* |
| **mild** | not strong, spicy, or intense | *Could I have a mild curry, please?* |
| **ingredient** | one of the foods used to make a dish | *What are the ingredients in this soup?* |
| **receipt** | a printed or digital record showing what you paid for | *Keep the receipt in case you need to return it.* |
| **refund** | money returned to you after you give something back or cancel a purchase | *Could I get a refund for this ticket?* |
| **exchange** | replacing an item with another one, often the same item in a different size | *I'd like to exchange this shirt for a larger one.* |
| **platform** | the area beside railway tracks where passengers get on or off a train | *Which platform does the train leave from?* |
| **direct** (a journey) | going to the destination without changing to another train or bus | *Is there a direct train to the airport?* |
| **delay** | a situation in which something happens later than planned | *The flight has a 30-minute delay.* |
| **symptom** | a change in your body that may show you are unwell | *A cough can be a symptom of a cold.* |
| **prescription** | an official instruction from a healthcare professional for medicine | *The pharmacy needs your prescription.* |
| **side effect** | an unwanted effect that a medicine can cause | *The leaflet explains possible side effects.* |
| **overcast** | covered with cloud, with little or no sunshine | *It's overcast, but it isn't raining.* |
| **drizzle** | very light rain with small drops | *We walked home in the drizzle.* |
| **humid** | warm and damp in the air | *It gets very humid here in August.* |
| **reliable** | someone or something you can trust to do what is expected | *The buses are usually reliable in the morning.* |
| **relieved** | feeling better because something difficult or worrying is over | *I was relieved when I found my passport.* |

## Home

| Rooms | Furniture and fittings | Appliances |
|---|---|---|
| living room, bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, hallway, study / home office, dining room, garage, garden (UK) / backyard (US), attic / loft | sofa / couch, bookshelf, wardrobe (UK) / closet (US), chest of drawers, curtains / blinds, carpet, rug, tap (UK) / faucet (US), light switch, plug socket (UK) / outlet (US), radiator | washing machine, dishwasher, microwave, fridge, freezer, oven / cooker, kettle, vacuum cleaner (UK also *hoover*), iron, tumble dryer |

*The bathroom is occupied — can you wait a moment? · Is there any milk left in the fridge? · Could you run the vacuum cleaner round the living room?*

## Food and restaurants

**Meals:** breakfast, brunch, lunch, dinner / supper, snack.
**Cooking:** boil, fry, grill, roast, bake, steam, stir-fry, simmer.
**Describing food:** fresh, frozen, tinned (UK) / canned, raw, spicy, mild, sweet, savoury, processed.

**Restaurant phrases:**
- *A table for two, please.*
- *Could I see the menu? What do you recommend?*
- *I'll have the chicken, please. Could I have it without onions?*
- *Is this suitable for vegetarians? Does this contain nuts?*
- *Could we have the bill (UK) / check (US), please?*

## Shopping

**Shops:** supermarket, bakery, butcher's, pharmacy / chemist's (UK), department store, market, charity shop (UK) / thrift store (US), hardware store.

**In the shop:** *Do you have this in a medium? · Where can I find the dairy section? · I'm just browsing, thanks. · Can I try this on? · Can I pay by card?*

**Prices and value:** a bargain · on sale / on offer · overpriced · receipt · refund · exchange.

**Complaints:** *I think there's been a mistake with my change. · I bought this last week and it's already broken. · I'd like a refund, please — here's the receipt. · This isn't what I ordered.*

## Transport

**Types:** bus, coach (long-distance bus), underground / tube (London) / metro / subway (US), tram, train, taxi / cab, ferry, flight.

**Tickets:** *A single (UK) / one-way ticket to…, please. · A return (UK) / round-trip ticket. · Which platform does it leave from? · Is it direct, or do I need to change? · I'd like a window / an aisle seat.*

**Flights:** departure, arrival, boarding pass, check-in, hand luggage / carry-on, delay, layover / stopover, gate.

**Directions:** *How do I get to…? · Is it within walking distance? · Go straight ahead for about 200 metres. · Turn left at the traffic lights. · Take the second exit at the roundabout. · It's on your left, just past the supermarket.*

## Health

**Body:** head, forehead, jaw, neck, shoulder, chest, back, waist, hip, elbow, wrist, thigh, knee, ankle, heel, toe.

**Symptoms:** headache, sore throat, fever / temperature, cough, nausea (*I feel sick*), rash, sprained ankle, dizzy, allergic to…, contagious.

**At the doctor's:** *I'd like to make an appointment. · I've been feeling unwell for three days. · I have a sharp pain in my lower back. · It hurts when I press here. · I'm allergic to penicillin. · Are there any side effects? · Do I need a prescription?*

**Pharmacy:** painkillers (paracetamol, ibuprofen), antihistamine, antiseptic cream, plasters (UK) / band-aids (US), cough syrup, lozenges, prescription.

## Weather

sunny · cloudy / overcast · drizzle · shower · downpour · foggy · humid · frost · hail · thunderstorm · heatwave · windy / gale-force winds

**Temperature scale:** freezing → cold → chilly / cool → mild → warm → hot → boiling / scorching.

*It's supposed to clear up later. · There's a 70% chance of showers. · Terrible weather, isn't it?* (a very common small-talk opener)

## Time and dates

- *five past three · quarter past three · half past three · quarter to four · ten to four*
- *the day before yesterday · the day after tomorrow · a fortnight (UK, two weeks)*
- *on time* (at the planned time) vs *in time* (early enough): *The train was on time. We arrived in time for the start.*
- Dates: *the 14th of March* (UK) / *March 14th* (US).

## People and relationships

**Family:** parent, sibling, spouse, partner, grandparent, aunt, uncle, cousin, niece, nephew, in-laws (*mother-in-law*), stepfather, half-sister.

**Appearance:** tall · average height · slim · athletic · curly / straight / wavy hair · bald · in his thirties · middle-aged.

**Personality:** outgoing, reliable, generous, patient, ambitious, easy-going / stubborn, bossy, moody, arrogant, unreliable.

**Relationships:** acquaintance, colleague, flatmate (UK) / roommate (US) · *get on well with · fall out with · make up (after an argument).*

## Feelings

| Positive | Negative |
|---|---|
| delighted, thrilled, relieved, content, grateful, proud, enthusiastic | anxious, nervous, frustrated, overwhelmed, disappointed, embarrassed, fed up, homesick |

*I'm feeling a bit under the weather* (slightly ill). *I'm in two minds about it* (undecided). *It gets on my nerves* (it annoys me). *I'm over the moon* (very happy).

**Checking on others:** *Are you alright? · How are you holding up? · Do you want to talk about it? · That sounds really tough — I'm sorry you're going through that.*

## Everyday conversation phrases

**Clarifying:** *Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. · Could you speak a bit more slowly? · What do you mean by…? · So what you're saying is…?*

**Polite requests:** *Could you possibly…? · Would you mind + -ing? · I was wondering if you could… · Would it be alright if I…?*

**Small talk:** *How do you know [the host]? · What do you do? · Are you from around here? · Long time no see! · Any plans for the weekend?*

**Ending a conversation:** *It was great chatting with you. · I'll let you get on. · Let's catch up properly soon. · Take care!*

**Would you mind…?** — *No* means yes: *Do you mind if I sit here? — **No, go ahead.***
