# Articles — Reference

Articles are tiny words with a big job: they tell the listener whether you both **share knowledge** of the thing you mean. Many languages (Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Russian, Japanese, Chinese…) have no articles, so they feel optional — they are not, and missing articles are among the most noticed errors.

**The core logic**
- **a / an** — the listener does *not* know which one; one of many; first mention.
- **the** — you both know *which* one.
- **no article** — things in general (plurals, uncountables, abstract ideas), most names.

**The camera test:** could the listener point a camera at the exact thing? *I saw **a** dog* (no) · *I saw **the** dog* (yes — the one we both know) · *I love dogs* (no specific dog at all → no article).

## a or an? Sound, not spelling

| an + vowel sound | a + consonant sound |
|---|---|
| *an apple, an idea, an egg* | *a book, a car* |
| silent *h*: *an hour, an honest person, an honour* | pronounced *h*: *a house, a hotel, a historian* |
| letter names that start with a vowel sound: *an MBA, an MP, an X-ray, an SQL query* (said *ess-queue-ell*) | *u* / *eu* with a /j/ sound: *a university, a useful tool, a European city* |
| | *one* with a /w/ sound: *a one-way street* |

Acronyms read as words follow the word's sound: ***a** FIFA ruling* (*FEE-fa*), ***a** NATO member*.

## Use a / an for…

1. **First mention:** *I bought a car. The car is blue.*
2. **Jobs and categories:** *She's a doctor. It's a great idea.*
3. **One of many:** *I need a pen* (any pen).
4. **Exclamations:** *What a day! Such a pity!*

a/an only goes with **singular countable** nouns: never *a books*, never *a water*.

## Use the for…

1. **Second mention:** *I have a dog. The dog is playful.*
2. **Things that are unique:** *the sun, the moon, the internet, the government.*
3. **Specific from context:** *Can you open the window?* (this room's window)
4. ***of*-phrases:** *the top of the mountain, the end of the film.*
5. **Superlatives, ordinals and "always-the" words:** *the best, the first, the last, the next, the same, the only, the main, the whole.*
6. **Geography:** rivers (*the Rhine, the Ganges*), seas and oceans (*the Pacific*), mountain ranges (*the Alps, the Himalayas*), island groups (*the Maldives*), plural or "United/Republic/Kingdom" countries (*the Netherlands, the UK, the US, the Czech Republic*), famous buildings (*the Taj Mahal*), many newspapers (*the Guardian, the Hindu*).

## Use no article for…

1. **Plurals in general:** *Dogs are loyal. Mistakes happen.*
2. **Uncountables in general:** *Water is essential. Knowledge is power.*
3. **Most countries, cities, continents, languages:** *India, Berlin, Asia, English.*
4. **Single mountains and lakes, streets:** *Mount Everest, Lake Geneva, Oxford Street.*
5. **Meals, sports, subjects:** *have breakfast, play cricket, study physics.*
6. **by + transport:** *by bus, by train* (but *on foot*).

Compare: *Breakfast is important* (general) vs *The breakfast at this hotel is great* (specific).

## Places that change meaning

| No article — the purpose | *the* — the building |
|---|---|
| *at school* (as a student) | *at the school* (visiting, as a parent) |
| *at university* (enrolled) | *at the university* (on campus for another reason) |
| *in prison* (as a prisoner) | *in the prison* (working, visiting) |
| *at church* (at a service) | *at the church* (the building) |
| *in hospital* (as a patient, UK) | *at the hospital* (visiting) — US English uses *in the hospital* for patients too |

Also: *go home, at home, at work, in bed, at sea* — no article.

## Uncountable nouns — never with a/an

*information, advice, news, furniture, luggage, baggage, equipment, traffic, weather, money, research, knowledge, feedback, software, homework, progress, work (as an activity)*

Use *some*, no article, or a unit: *some advice, a piece of advice, an item of news*.

## 20 common mistakes

| # | Wrong | Right | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *She is doctor.* | *She is a doctor.* | jobs → a/an |
| 2 | *The life is short.* | *Life is short.* | general abstract noun |
| 3 | *I play the cricket.* | *I play cricket.* | sports |
| 4 | *She speaks the English.* | *She speaks English.* | languages |
| 5 | *I went to the India.* | *I went to India.* | most countries |
| 6 | *Sun rises in the east.* | *The sun rises in the east.* | unique things |
| 7 | *She is best student.* | *She is the best student.* | superlatives |
| 8 | *He gave me an advice.* | *He gave me some advice.* | uncountable |
| 9 | *I saw a same movie.* | *I saw the same movie.* | *the same* |
| 10 | *He is a honest man.* | *He is an honest man.* | silent *h* |
| 11 | *The music is my passion.* | *Music is my passion.* | general |
| 12 | *I have the headache.* | *I have a headache.* | aches → a |
| 13 | *This is the good hotel.* (first mention) | *This is a good hotel.* | not yet specific |
| 14 | *I go to work by the bus.* | *I go to work by bus.* | by + transport |
| 15 | *He is the very smart.* | *He is very smart.* | no noun → no article |
| 16 | *The Amazon is river.* | *The Amazon is a river.* | classifying → a |
| 17 | *I need an information.* | *I need some information.* | uncountable |
| 18 | *I found issue in code.* | *I found an issue in the code.* | singular countable needs a determiner |
| 19 | *She's in the hospital* (UK, as a patient) | *She's in hospital.* | UK usage (US: *in the hospital*) |
| 20 | *an university* | *a university* | /j/ sound |

## Three questions to choose

1. Is the noun **singular and countable**? → It *must* have *a / an / the* (or *my, this, each…*).
2. Do **both of you know which one**? → *the*.
3. Is it **first mention, a job, or one of many**? → *a / an*. Otherwise → no article.
