# Passive and Causatives — Reference

In an **active** sentence the subject does the action; in a **passive** sentence the subject receives it.

> **Passive = be (in the right tense) + past participle**

## Forms

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | *They make cars here.* | *Cars are made here.* |
| Present continuous | *They are repairing the road.* | *The road is being repaired.* |
| Past simple | *Someone stole my wallet.* | *My wallet was stolen.* |
| Past continuous | *They were testing the app.* | *The app was being tested.* |
| Present perfect | *They have built a bridge.* | *A bridge has been built.* |
| Past perfect | *They had fixed it.* | *It had been fixed.* |
| Future (*will*) | *They will announce the results.* | *The results will be announced.* |
| Modal | *We must review the change.* | *The change must be reviewed.* |

## When to use the passive

- The doer is **unknown**: *My bike was stolen.*
- The doer is **obvious or unimportant**: *The road is being repaired.*
- The **process or result** is the focus: *The logs are collected every five minutes. The report was submitted on time.*
- Formal, technical, and news writing: *The issue was identified at 10:15 and has been resolved.*

## When to avoid it

- When active is shorter and clearer.
- When **responsibility** matters. *Mistakes were made* hides who made them; *I made a mistake* is honest and professional.
- When the sentence becomes long and awkward.

## The agent: *by*

Add *by + agent* only when it adds useful information: *The book was written **by Orwell**.* · *The window was broken.* (no *by* — unknown or irrelevant)

## Verbs that cannot be passive

Intransitive verbs (no object) have no passive: *happen, occur, arrive, die, exist, appear*. *The outage **was happened*** ✗ → *The outage **happened*** ✓.

## Causatives — someone else does it for you

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| *have + object + past participle* | *We had the contract reviewed by legal. I had my car serviced.* |
| *get + object + past participle* (more informal) | *I need to get my laptop fixed.* |
| *have + person + base verb* | *I'll have Priya check the numbers.* |
| *get + person + to + verb* | *I'll get IT to reset my password.* |

Compare: *I repaired my phone* (I did it) vs *I had my phone repaired* (a shop did it).

## Useful passive sentences

- *English is spoken in over 50 countries.*
- *The meeting has been postponed until Friday.*
- *You are requested to complete this form.*
- *The change will be rolled out in three phases.*
- *All requests must be approved by a manager.*
