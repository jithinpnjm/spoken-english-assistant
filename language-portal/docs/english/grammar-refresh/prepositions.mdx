# Prepositions — Reference

Prepositions show relationships of **time, place, movement and reason**. They rarely translate directly, and many combinations are fixed — so learn them as chunks.

**The core picture:** **AT** = a point · **ON** = a surface or a day · **IN** = inside a space or a longer period.

## Time

| AT — exact point | ON — days and dates | IN — longer periods |
|---|---|---|
| *at 7 o'clock, at noon, at midnight* | *on Monday, on weekdays* | *in January, in 2025* |
| *at night, at the weekend* (UK) | *on 15 August, on the 25th* | *in spring / summer* |
| *at Christmas, at Easter, at Diwali* | *on Christmas Day, on my birthday* | *in the morning / afternoon / evening* |
| *at the moment, at present, at first* | *on Monday morning* | *in the 1990s, in the 21st century* |
| *at breakfast / lunch / dinner* | *on the weekend* (US) | *in five minutes* (= after five minutes from now) |

**No preposition** before *next, last, this, every, today, tomorrow, yesterday*: *See you next Monday.*

**Deadlines and duration:**
- *by Friday* = no later than Friday · *until Friday* = continuing up to Friday
- *within a week* = inside a week · *during the meeting* = at some point in the meeting
- *for three years* (duration) · *since 2021* (starting point) · *two days ago* (past simple only)

## Place

| AT — point | ON — surface / line | IN — enclosed space |
|---|---|---|
| *at the bus stop, at the door* | *on the table, on the wall* | *in the box, in the room* |
| *at home, at work, at school* | *on the first floor* | *in London, in India* |
| *at the airport, at 10 Downing Street* | *on the left / right* | *in the north, in the middle* |
| *at the corner, at the end of the road* | *on the coast, on the border* | *in the sea, in a lake* |

**Streets:** American English says *on Baker Street*; British English says both *on* and *in Baker Street*. With a house number, use *at*: *at 21 Baker Street*.

**Institutions:** *at school / at university / at work / at home* describe the activity; *in the school / in the office* describe being physically inside. British *in hospital* (as a patient) = American *in the hospital*.

## Movement

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| to | destination | *She walked to the shop.* |
| towards | in the direction of | *He moved towards the door.* |
| into / out of | entering / leaving a space | *She walked into the room. He ran out of the building.* |
| onto / off | onto / away from a surface | *The cat jumped onto the table. She fell off the chair.* |
| through | from one side to the other of a 3D space | *We walked through the forest.* |
| across | from one side to the other of a surface | *She swam across the river.* |
| along | following a line | *We walked along the beach.* |
| past | going beyond a point | *The bus went past my stop.* |
| over / under | above / below | *The plane flew over the mountains.* |
| around | in a circle / in different directions | *They walked around the lake.* |

**home:** *go home, come home, arrive home, get home* — no *to*. But *at home* for position.
**arrive:** *arrive **in** a city or country*, *arrive **at** a building or place*, *arrive home*. Never *arrive to*.

## Transport

- **in** a car, a taxi, a van, a lift
- **on** a bus, a train, a plane, a ship, a bike, a horse
- **by** car / bus / train / plane (how you travel, no article) — but **on foot**

## Tricky pairs

| Pair | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| by / with | *by* = agent or method; *with* = tool | *written by Orwell · paid by card · cut it with a knife* |
| for / since / ago | duration / starting point / time before now | *for 3 years · since 2021 · 2 days ago* |
| by / until | deadline / continuing to | *Send it by Friday. I'm away until Friday.* |
| in time / on time | early enough / at the planned time | *We arrived in time for the start. The train was on time.* |

## Most common mistakes

| # | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *I arrived to London.* | *I arrived in London.* |
| 2 | *She is married with him.* | *She is married to him.* |
| 3 | *We discussed about the problem.* | *We discussed the problem.* |
| 4 | *She explained me the answer.* | *She explained the answer to me.* |
| 5 | *I've been waiting since three hours.* | *I've been waiting for three hours.* |
| 6 | *He is good in English.* | *He is good at English.* |
| 7 | *I am interested about cooking.* | *I am interested in cooking.* |
| 8 | *She depends of her parents.* | *She depends on her parents.* |
| 9 | *I went to home.* | *I went home.* |
| 10 | *She entered into the room.* | *She entered the room.* |
| 11 | *I listened the music.* | *I listened to the music.* |
| 12 | *He suffers of migraines.* | *He suffers from migraines.* |
| 13 | *I'm looking forward to see you.* | *I'm looking forward to seeing you.* |
| 14 | *Despite of the rain, we went out.* | *Despite the rain, …* / *In spite of the rain, …* |
| 15 | *See you on next Monday.* | *See you next Monday.* |
