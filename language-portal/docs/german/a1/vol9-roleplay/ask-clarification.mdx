# 9.5 Asking for Clarification (Nachfragen und Klären)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can verify that I understood important information correctly (**Habe ich das richtig verstanden...?**).
* I can clarify which option someone means (*Meinen Sie Gleis 4 oder Gleis 5?*).
* I can ask for explanations when I don't understand a term.
* I can avoid misunderstandings regarding appointments, prices, and names.

---

## 1. Why Clarification is a Superpower in German

When learning German, many beginners nod their heads and say *"Ja, ja"* even when they are completely lost. This leads to missed trains, wrong appointments, and unnecessary stress!

In German culture, **asking for clarification is respected as thorough and professional**:
* It shows you take the matter seriously.
* It prevents costly errors.
* It is a recognized core skill in CEFR language exams.

---

## 2. The 3 Master Clarification Formulas

### 1. „Habe ich das richtig verstanden...?“ (Did I understand correctly...?)
The single best formula to confirm times, numbers, and dates:
* *„**Habe ich das richtig verstanden**: Der Termin ist am Donnerstag um 10 Uhr?“*  
  *(Did I understand that correctly: The appointment is on Thursday at 10 AM?)*
* *„**Habe ich das richtig verstanden**: Das kostet 15 Euro?“*

### 2. „Meinen Sie...?“ (Do you mean...?)
When an instruction or location is ambiguous:
* *„**Meinen Sie** Gleis 4 oder Gleis 14?“* (Do you mean Track 4 or Track 14?)
* *„**Meinen Sie** heute oder morgen?“* (Do you mean today or tomorrow?)

### 3. „Was bedeutet...?“ (What does... mean?)
When someone uses an unfamiliar German word:
* *„Entschuldigung, **was bedeutet** ‚Nebenkosten‘?“* (What does 'Nebenkosten' mean?)
* *„**Was bedeutet** diese Abkürzung?“* (What does this abbreviation mean?)
