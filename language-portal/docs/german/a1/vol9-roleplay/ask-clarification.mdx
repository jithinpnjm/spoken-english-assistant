# 9.5 Ask for Clarification (A1)

## What you will learn

- Ask someone to repeat or speak more slowly.
- Ask what one word means.
- Check a day, time, or number by repeating it back.
- Ask someone to write or spell a name or number.

You do not need a long sentence to clarify. A short question or a repeated detail is enough.

## 1. Four useful repair phrases

| Problem | A1 phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| You did not hear | *Wie bitte?* / *Noch einmal, bitte.* | Pardon? / One more time, please. |
| The person spoke too fast | *Langsamer, bitte.* | More slowly, please. |
| You do not know a word | *Was bedeutet „…“?* | What does “…” mean? |
| You want to check a detail | *Also: Dienstag um zehn Uhr?* | So: Tuesday at ten? |

Useful writing requests:

- *Können Sie das bitte aufschreiben?* — Can you write that down, please?
- *Können Sie den Namen bitte buchstabieren?* — Can you spell the name, please?

## 2. Check common details

Repeat the important word, then ask a short either/or question:

- *Montag oder Mittwoch?*
- *Gleis 4 oder Gleis 14?*
- *Halb vier? Also 15:30?*
- *Eine Kopie oder das Original?*

For German time, **halb vier = 3:30**. If you are unsure, check the time aloud: *Halb vier – 15:30?*

## 3. Model dialogue: confirm an appointment

> **Empfang:** Sie haben am Mittwoch um halb neun einen Termin. <br />
> **Sie:** Mittwoch oder Montag? <br />
> **Empfang:** Mittwoch. <br />
> **Sie:** Halb neun – also 8:30? <br />
> **Empfang:** Ja, genau. <br />
> **Sie:** Also: Mittwoch, 8:30 Uhr. Danke!

The learner repeats only the important details. No extra grammar is needed.

## 4. Guided practice

### A. Choose a useful phrase

1. You did not hear the street name. — **Wie bitte? Können Sie das buchstabieren?**
2. The person is speaking too quickly. — **Langsamer, bitte.**
3. You do not know the word *Termin*. — **Was bedeutet „Termin“?**
4. You want to check *Dienstag um elf*. — **Also: Dienstag um elf Uhr?**

### B. Repeat the key detail

Say one short check-back for each message:

1. *„Der Zug fährt von Gleis 5.“* <br />
   **Model:** *Gleis 5?*
2. *„Ihr Termin ist am Freitag um 10 Uhr.“* <br />
   **Model:** *Freitag, 10 Uhr?*
3. *„Bitte bringen Sie eine Kopie mit.“* <br />
   **Model:** *Eine Kopie? Nicht das Original?*

### C. Roleplay

With a partner, practise two situations:

1. Call a doctor's office. Confirm the day and time of an appointment.
2. Ask for directions. Ask the other person to repeat one street or place name.

Use at least two phrases from this page. Then switch roles.
