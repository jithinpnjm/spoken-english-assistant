# 9.6 Asking for Repetition (Um Wiederholung bitten)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can politely ask native speakers to repeat what they said (**Wie bitte?**, **Noch einmal, bitte**).
* I know how to use the formal verb **wiederholen** (*Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?*).
* I know what to say when background noise prevents me from hearing clearly.
* I can ask friends casually what they said (*Was hast du gesagt?*).

---

## 1. Never Feel Ashamed to Ask for Repetition!

Every language learner experiences moments where a sentence sounds like a blur of fast sounds. 
Native German speakers do not expect beginners to catch every mumble or train loudspeaker echo on the first try.

Asking for repetition politely is a **sign of active listening**:

```text
The Universal Ladder of Repetition:
1. Quick & Polite:      ➔  „Wie bitte?“               (Pardon? / What was that?)
2. Clear Request:       ➔  „Noch einmal, bitte.“      (Once again, please.)
3. Formal & Complete:   ➔  „Könnten Sie das bitte     (Could you please repeat that?)
                            wiederholen?“
```

---

## 2. In Person vs. On the Phone / In Noise

### 1. In Everyday Shops & Offices:
* *„Entschuldigung, **wie bitte**?“* (Pardon?)
* *„**Könnten Sie das bitte noch einmal sagen**?“* (Could you say that once more?)

### 2. When There is Loud Background Noise (Train stations, construction):
Germans have a specific, useful phrase when noise is the problem:
* *„Entschuldigung, **ich habe Sie akustisch nicht verstanden**.“*  
  *(Literally: "I did not understand you acoustically / I couldn't hear you over the noise.")*
* *„Hier ist es sehr laut. Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?“*

### 3. Informal with Friends:
* *„Wie bitte? **Was hast du gesagt**?“* (Pardon? What did you say?)
* *„Sag das noch mal!“* (Say that again!).
