# 9.10 Agreeing & Disagreeing (Zustimmen und Widersprechen)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can express simple personal opinions using **Ich finde...** (I think / find).
* I can agree with someone enthusiastically (**Das stimmt!**, **Das finde ich auch!**).
* I can disagree politely without sounding aggressive (**Das sehe ich anders**).
* I can negotiate a mutual agreement with a partner.

---

## 1. Expressing an Opinion in German

Before you agree or disagree, you need to state your own view:

* **„Ich finde...“** (I think / I find...)
  * *„Ich finde den Film sehr spannend.“* (I find the film very exciting.)
  * *„Ich finde die Wohnung etwas teuer.“* (I think the apartment is a bit expensive.)
* **„Ich glaube...“** (I think / I believe...)
  * *„Ich glaube, der Zug kommt gleich.“* (I think the train is arriving soon.)
* **„Was denkst du?“ / „Was meinen Sie?“** (What do you think?)

---

## 2. How to Agree (Zustimmen)

| German Expression | English Meaning | When to use it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **„Das stimmt!“** | That's true! / That's right! | Confirming a fact or statement |
| **„Genau! / Richtig!“** | Exactly! / Right! | Enthusiastic, everyday |
| **„Das finde ich auch.“** | I think so too. | Sharing an opinion |
| **„Gute Idee!“** | Good idea! | In planning activities |
| **„Einverstanden!“** | Agreed! / It's a deal! | After concluding a plan |

---

## 3. How to Disagree Politely (Widersprechen)

Germans are famous for directness, but disagreeing still requires polite language:

* **„Das sehe ich anders.“** (I see that differently — very professional and respected).
* **„Das finde ich nicht.“** (I don't think so.)
* **„Das stimmt nicht ganz.“** (That is not quite true.)
* **„Ich bin nicht sicher.“** (I am not sure.)
* **„Nein, das ist mir zu teuer / zu spät.“** (No, that is too expensive / too late for me.)
