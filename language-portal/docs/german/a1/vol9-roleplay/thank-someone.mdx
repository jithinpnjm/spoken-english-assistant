# 9.9 Expressing Gratitude: "Vielen Dank!" (Sich bedanken)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can express gratitude across different levels of warmth and formality.
* I can thank someone for specific things (*für die Hilfe, für das Geschenk, für die Einladung*).
* I know the 4 standard ways to respond when someone thanks me (**Gerne!**, **Bitte schön!**).
* I know how to write thank-you notes for invitations and gifts.

---

## 1. The 4 Levels of Saying "Thank You"

| German Phrase | English Meaning | Warmth / Formality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **„Danke!“** | Thanks! | Quick, casual, everyday |
| **„Danke schön! / Danke sehr!“**| Thank you very much! | Friendly, polite standard |
| **„Vielen Dank!“** | Many thanks! | Professional & written standard |
| **„Herzlichen Dank!“** | Heartfelt thanks! | Very warm, deep gratitude |

### Thanking for Specific Things: "Vielen Dank für..." (+ Accusative)
* *„Vielen Dank **für Ihre Hilfe**.“* (Many thanks for your help.)
* *„Danke **für die Einladung**.“* (Thanks for the invitation.)
* *„Vielen Dank **für das schöne Geschenk**.“* (Thank you for the lovely gift.)
* *„Danke **für deine Zeit**.“* (Thanks for your time.)

---

## 2. How to Say "You're Welcome" (Auf Dank reagieren)

Never remain silent when someone says *Danke*. Use these natural replies:

* **„Bitte schön! / Bitte sehr!“** — *You're very welcome.* (Traditional polite standard).
* **„Gerne! / Sehr gerne!“** — *With pleasure / Glad to help!* (Extremely common in modern everyday German).
* **„Nichts zu danken!“** — *Don't mention it! / Nothing to thank for.*
* **„Keine Ursache!“** — *No trouble at all.*
