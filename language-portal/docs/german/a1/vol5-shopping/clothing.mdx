# 5.8 Clothing & Sizes (Kleidung und Größen)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can name everyday clothing items with their articles and plurals (*Hose, Hemd, Pullover, Jacke*).
* I can name colors (*schwarz, weiß, rot, blau, grün...*).
* I can state my clothing size (**die Größe**) and ask for a different size.
* I can ask where the changing room is (**die Umkleidekabine**) and try items on (**anprobieren**).
* I know the difference between *passen* (fitting in size) and *stehen* (looking good on someone).

---

## 1. Everyday Clothes Vocabulary (Die Kleidung)

Notice how clothing items are distributed across all three genders:

| Masculine (der) | Feminine (die) | Neutral (das) | Plural (die) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Pullover / Pulli** (sweater)| **die Hose** (pants / trousers) | **das Hemd** (button-down shirt) | **die Schuhe** (shoes) |
| **der Mantel** (overcoat) | **die Jacke** (jacket) | **das T-Shirt** (T-shirt) | **die Stiefel** (boots) |
| **der Rock** (skirt) | **die Bluse** (blouse) | **das Kleid** (dress) | **die Socken** (socks) |
| **der Anzug** (suit) | **die Mütze** (beanie / winter hat)| **das Kleidungsstück** (garment) | **die Handschuhe** (gloves) |

⚠️ **Plural Difference from English:** In English, "pants" and "jeans" are plural (*"these pants are tight"*). In German, **die Hose** is singular!
* *Die Hose ist sehr schön.* (Singular verb *ist*!).

## 2. Colors (Die Farben)

* **schwarz** (black)
* **weiß** (white)
* **grau** (grey)
* **blau** (blue)
* **rot** (red)
* **grün** (green)
* **gelb** (yellow)
* **braun** (brown)

*Example:* *„Ich suche eine Jacke in **Schwarz** oder **Dunkelblau**.“* (dark blue).

---

## 3. Sizes & The Changing Room (Größe und Umkleide)

### 1. Asking for Sizes
* **die Größe** (die Größen) — *clothing / shoe size*
* Salesperson: *„Welche Größe haben Sie?“* (What size are you?)
* You: *„Ich habe **Größe M**.“* (or: *„Größe 38 / Größe 42“*).

### 2. Trying Clothes on: "anprobieren"
*Anprobieren* is a separable verb (Chapter 3.12):
* *„Kann ich die Jacke **anprobieren**?“* (Can I try the jacket on?)
* *„Wo ist die **Umkleidekabine**?“* (Where is the fitting room?)

### 3. Does it Fit? "passen" vs. "stehen"
* **passen** = The physical size is right (fits your body):
  * *Die Hose **passt** mir gut.* (The pants fit me well.)
  * *Die Schuhe sind **zu klein**.* (The shoes are too small.)
  * *Der Pullover ist **zu groß** / **zu eng**.* (Too big / too tight).
* **stehen** = The style or color looks attractive on you:
  * *Die Farbe **steht** dir super!* (That color looks great on you!)
  * *Das Kleid **steht** Ihnen sehr gut.* (The dress suits you very well.)
