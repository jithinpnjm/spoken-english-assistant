# 5.9 Returns & Complaints (Umtausch und Reklamation)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can return an item (**zurückgeben**) or exchange it for a different size (**umtauschen**).
* I can explain why an item is being returned (*kaputt, passt nicht, hat ein Loch*).
* I understand the crucial role of the receipt (**der Kassenbon**) in German stores.
* I can ask whether I will receive a refund (**Geld zurück**) or a store voucher (**Gutschein**).

---

## 1. The Two Key Verbs: "umtauschen" vs. "zurückgeben"

Both of these verbs are **separable** (see Chapter 3.12):

* **umtauschen** = To exchange an item for another size, color, or model.
  * *„Ich möchte diesen Pullover **umtauschen**. Er ist mir zu klein.“* (I'd like to exchange this sweater. It's too small for me.)
* **zurückgeben** = To return an item completely and ask for your money back.
  * *„Ich möchte das Gerät **zurückgeben**.“* (I'd like to return the device.)

---

## 2. 🧾 The Golden Rule in Germany: No Return Without a Receipt!

In Germany, customer service is governed strictly by rules:
* If you want to return or exchange an item, the salesperson will immediately ask:
  > *„Haben Sie den **Kassenbon** dabei?“* *(Do you have the receipt with you?)*
* If you have the receipt, you can say:
  > *„Ja, hier ist der **Kassenbon**.“*
* Without the receipt (*ohne Kassenbon*), stores are legally not required to take the item back.

---

## 3. Explaining the Problem Simply

When returning an item, state the reason clearly:

| Problem | German Phrase | English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wrong Size** | *„Die Hose **passt mir nicht**.“* | The pants do not fit me. |
| **Defective Device** | *„Das Gerät **funktioniert nicht**.“* | The device doesn't work. |
| **Broken / Damaged** | *„Das Glas **ist kaputt**.“* | The glass is broken. |
| **Damaged Fabric** | *„Das T-Shirt **hat einen Fleck / ein Loch**.“* | The T-shirt has a stain / hole. |
| **Missing Parts** | *„Ein Teil **fehlt**.“* | A part is missing. |

---

## 4. Refund or Voucher: "Geld zurück" vs. "Gutschein"

Depending on the store's policy, you may receive cash back or store credit:
* **das Geld zurück:** Refund / money back.
  * *„Bekomme ich das **Geld zurück**?“* (Do I get the money back?)
* **der Gutschein** (die Gutscheine): Store credit voucher / gift card.
  * *„Wir können Ihnen einen **Gutschein** ausstellen.“* (We can issue you a voucher.)
