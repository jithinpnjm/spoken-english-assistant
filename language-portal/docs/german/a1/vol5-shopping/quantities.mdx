# 5.3 Quantities & Units (Mengen und Einheiten)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can order food by weight using **das Kilo**, **das Gramm**, and the traditional German unit **das Pfund**.
* I can order liquids by the liter (**der Liter**) and fractions (*ein halber Liter*).
* I know the common packaging containers (*die Flasche, die Packung, die Dose, das Glas*).
* I know that German does NOT use the word "of" between the quantity and the food (*ein Kilo Äpfel*).

---

## 1. Units of Weight (Gewichte)

When you buy fruit, vegetables, cheese, or meat at a market stall (*Wochenmarkt*) or counter (*Frischetheke*), you order by weight:

| Unit | German Word | Abbreviation | Everyday Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kilogram** | **das Kilo / Kilogramm** | kg | *ein Kilo Äpfel* (1 kg of apples) |
| **Gram** | **das Gramm** | g | *200 Gramm Käse* (200 g of cheese) |
| **Half-kilo (Pound)**| **das Pfund** | — | *ein Pfund Kirschen* (500 grams!) |

### ⚠️ The German "Pfund": 500 Grams!
In English, a pound (lb) is about 453 grams. 
**In Germany, *ein Pfund* is standardized to exactly 500 grams (half a kilo).**
You will hear older Germans and market vendors use it constantly:
* *„Ich hätte gern ein Pfund Erdbeeren.“* (= 500 g of strawberries).
* *„Ein halbes Pfund Butter.“* (= 250 g of butter).

---

## 2. ⚠️ The Grammar Relief: No "OF" in German!

In English, you must always put the word "of" between the container/weight and the food:
* *"a bottle **of** water"*
* *"a package **of** pasta"*
* *"two kilos **of** potatoes"*

**German drops "of" completely! You place the quantity directly before the noun:**

```text
English: a kilo OF apples     ➔  German: ein Kilo Äpfel
English: a bottle OF wine     ➔  German: eine Flasche Wein
English: a pack OF rice       ➔  German: eine Packung Reis
English: a cup OF coffee      ➔  German: eine Tasse Kaffee
```

## 3. Packaging & Containers (Verpackungen)

Learn these essential container nouns with their genders and plurals:

* **die Flasche** (die Flaschen) — *bottle*
  * *eine Flasche Mineralwasser / eine Flasche Öl*
* **die Packung** (die Packungen) — *package / carton / box*
  * *eine Packung Nudeln / eine Packung Eier*
* **die Dose** (die Dosen) — *tin / can*
  * *eine Dose Tomaten / eine Dose Thunfisch*
* **das Glas** (die Gläser) — *jar / drinking glass*
  * *ein Glas Marmelade / ein Glas Senf (mustard)*
* **der Becher** (die Becher) — *cup / tub*
  * *ein Becher Joghurt / ein Becher Sahne (cream)*
* **das Stück** (die Stücke) — *piece / slice*
  * *zwei Stück Kuchen / ein Stück Käse*
* **die Scheibe** (die Scheiben) — *thin slice (bread/cheese/cold cuts)*
  * *vier Scheiben Käse / zwei Scheiben Brot*

---

## 4. Liquids: Liter & Halber Liter

* **der Liter** = *liter (1.000 ml)*
  * *ein Liter Milch*
* **ein halber Liter** = *half a liter (500 ml)*
  * *ein halber Liter Bier* (Commonly called *eine Halbe* in Bavaria!)
