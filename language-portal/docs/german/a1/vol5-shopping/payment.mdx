# 5.5 Payment: Cash, Card & "Stimmt so!" (Bezahlen)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can state whether I want to pay with cash (**bar**) or by card (**mit Karte**).
* I understand German cash culture (**Bargeld**) and where cards might not be accepted.
* I can handle change (**das Wechselgeld**) and receipts (**der Kassenbon**).
* I know the magic German tipping phrase: **„Stimmt so!“** (Keep the change).

---

## 1. Cash Culture vs. Card in Germany

Germany is famous for its love of cash (**das Bargeld**). While card payment has expanded rapidly across major supermarkets and retail chains, many bakeries, traditional restaurants, kiosks (*Spätis*), and small cafés still display the sign:
> **„Nur Barzahlung!“** *(Cash only!)*

Always carry some cash and coins with you in Germany!

---

## 2. The Two Payment Methods: "bar" vs. "mit Karte"

When the cashier tells you the total (*„Das macht zusammen 14 Euro 20“*), they will ask:

> *„Zahlen Sie **bar** oder **mit Karte**?“*  
> *(Are you paying in cash or with card?)*

### Your 2 Standard Answers:
1. **„Bar, bitte.“** (In cash, please).
2. **„Mit Karte, bitte.“** (By card, please).

If paying by card, hold your card or phone near the terminal when it beeps:
* *„Bitte auflegen.“* (Please tap contactless).
* *„Ihre PIN, bitte.“* (Enter your PIN, please).

---

## 3. Change, Receipts & Coins

* **das Bargeld:** Cash (banknotes and coins).
* **das Kleingeld / das Wechselgeld:** Small change / coins.
  * Cashier: *„Haben Sie es passend?“* (Do you have the exact change, e.g. 20 cents?)
  * Answer: *„Ja, hier sind 20 Cent.“* or *„Nein, leider nicht.“*
* **der Kassenbon / die Quittung:** Receipt.
  * Cashier: *„Brauchen Sie den Bon?“* (Do you need the receipt?)
  * Answer: *„Nein, danke.“* or *„Ja, bitte.“*

---

## 4. The Magic Tipping Phrase: "Stimmt so!"

In German cafés, bakeries, bars, and taxis, you do not leave tip money sitting on the table after you walk away. You tell the server your rounded-up total **as you hand them the cash**:

* If the bill is 8,20 € and you hand the waiter a 10 € bill:
  * Say: *„Neun Euro, bitte.“* ➔ The waiter will hand you back exactly 1 € change!
* If the bill is 9,50 € and you hand them a 10 € bill:
  * Say: **„Stimmt so!“** *(literally: "It is correct like this" ➔ "Keep the change!")*
