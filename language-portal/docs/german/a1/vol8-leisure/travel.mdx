# 8.6 Travel Planning & Packing (Reisen und Kofferpacken)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can talk about vacation plans and destinations (**das Reiseziel**).
* I know travel nouns like **der Koffer** (suitcase), **der Rucksack** (backpack), and **das Gepäck** (luggage).
* I can list what I pack inside my suitcase (*Reisepass, Ladekabel, Zahnbürste*).
* I know that luggage (**das Gepäck**) has no plural in German.

---

## 1. Planning a Trip (Eine Reise planen)

Germans are passionate travelers (often dubbed *Reiseweltmeister* — world champions of travel!). Use these core travel expressions:

* **die Reise** (die Reisen): Trip / journey.
  * *„Wir planen eine Reise nach Österreich.“* (We are planning a trip to Austria).
  * *„Gute Reise!“* (Have a good trip! / Safe travels!).
* **verreisen:** To go away on a trip (separable!).
  * *„Am Wochenende **verreisen** wir.“* (We are going away on a trip this weekend).
* **das Reiseziel** (die Reiseziele): Travel destination.
  * *„Mein liebstes Reiseziel ist Italien.“* (My favorite destination is Italy).
* **der Strand:** Beach (*Wir fahren an den Strand*).
* **die Berge:** Mountains (*Wir wandern in den Bergen*).

---

## 2. Luggage & Packing (Koffer und Gepäck)

| Item | Article & Plural | English Meaning | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Koffer** | die Koffer | suitcase | Plural has no ending! |
| **das Gepäck** | (uncountable) | luggage / baggage | Always singular *das Gepäck* |
| **das Handgepäck** | (uncountable) | carry-on / cabin baggage | Max 8 kg on flights |
| **der Rucksack** | die Rucksäcke | backpack | In German, rucksack is standard |
| **die Reisetasche** | die Reisetaschen | duffel / travel bag | Casual travel |

### 🧳 The Verb "packen" / "einpacken"
* *„Ich muss heute Abend meinen Koffer **packen**.“* (I have to pack my suitcase tonight).
* *„Hast du das Ladekabel **eingepackt**?“* (Did you pack the charging cable?)

---

## 3. What Goes in the Suitcase? (Packliste)

* **der Reisepass / der Ausweis:** Passport / ID card.
* **das Ticket / die Fahrkarte:** Travel ticket.
* **das Ladekabel:** Phone/laptop charger cable.
* **die Zahnbürste:** Toothbrush (*Zahnpasta* = toothpaste).
* **die Sonnenbrille & Sonnencreme:** Sunglasses and sunscreen.
* **die Medikamente:** Personal travel medications.
