# 8.3 Sports & Fitness (Sport und Fitness)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can name popular sports in German (**Fußball, Schwimmen, Laufen, Radfahren**).
* I know how to talk about going to the gym (**ins Fitnessstudio gehen**).
* I can say how often I exercise (*zweimal pro Woche*).
* I understand how bike culture and sports clubs operate in Germany.

---

## 1. Popular Sports in Germany

Germany has an active sports culture. Notice how sports are expressed with specific verbs:

| Sport | German Activity | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Football / Soccer**| **Fußball spielen** | *Viele Leute in Deutschland schauen gern Fußball.* |
| **Cycling** | **Fahrrad fahren / Rad fahren** | *Ich fahre jeden Tag mit dem Rad zur Arbeit.* |
| **Running / Jogging** | **joggen / laufen gehen** | *Ich gehe dreimal pro Woche im Park joggen.* |
| **Swimming** | **schwimmen gehen** | *Wir gehen am Wochenende ins Schwimmbad.* |
| **Gym / Fitness** | **ins Fitnessstudio gehen** | *Er trainiert im Fitnessstudio.* |
| **Tennis** | **Tennis spielen** | *Spielst du gern Tennis?* |
| **Hiking** | **wandern gehen** | *Am Sonntag gehen viele Familien wandern.* |

## 2. Talking About Frequency: "Wie oft machst du Sport?"

To describe your fitness routine, combine numbers with **pro Woche** (per week) or **im Monat** (per month):

* *„Wie oft machst du Sport?“* (How often do you exercise?)
* *„Ich trainiere **einmal pro Woche**.“* (Once a week).
* *„Ich jogge **zweimal pro Woche**.“* (Twice a week).
* *„Ich gehe **dreimal pro Woche** ins Fitnessstudio.“* (Three times a week).
* *„Ich mache **jeden Tag** Sport.“* (Every day).
* *„Ich mache leider **nie** Sport.“* (Unfortunately, I never do sports).

## 3. Real-Life Germany: Bike Culture & The Badesee

### 1. Das Fahrrad (Biking)
Germany has thousands of kilometers of dedicated bike lanes (**der Radweg**). For many city residents, the bicycle is their primary daily commuting vehicle.

### 2. Das Schwimmbad vs. Der Badesee
* **das Hallenbad:** Indoor heated swimming pool (for winter).
* **das Freibad:** Outdoor public swimming pool (usually open roughly from May to September – check your city's dates).
* **der Badesee:** Natural freshwater swimming lakes found all around German cities.
