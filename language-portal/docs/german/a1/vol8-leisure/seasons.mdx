# 8.2 Seasons & Climate (Jahreszeiten und Klima)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can name the four seasons in German and know that they are ALL masculine (**der**).
* I know that seasons always use the preposition **im** (*im Sommer, im Winter*).
* I can associate months with their seasons.
* I can talk about typical German seasonal activities (like Christmas markets and outdoor lakes).

---

## 1. The 4 Seasons: All Masculine (der)!

In German, all four seasons share the exact same masculine gender (**der**):

| Season | Article | Months Included | Typical Climate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Frühling** | der | März, April, Mai | Mild, Blumen blühen, sonnig |
| **der Sommer** | der | Juni, Juli, August | Heiß, lange Tage, 25–35 °C |
| **der Herbst** | der | September, Oktober, November| Bunte Blätter, windig, regnerisch |
| **der Winter** | der | Dezember, Januar, Februar | Kalt, Schnee, kurze Tage, minus Grade |

### The Golden Preposition: Always "im"!
Just like months (Chapter 1.5), seasons always take the preposition **im** (*in + dem*):
* *„**Im Sommer** fahre ich gern an den See.“* (In summer, I like going to the lake).
* *„**Im Winter** trinke ich gern heißen Tee.“* (In winter, I like drinking hot tea).

---

## 2. Seasonal Traditions & Activities in Germany

Germany's lifestyle transforms dramatically across the four seasons:

### 1. Im Sommer (Summer)
* **an den Badesee fahren:** Going to swimming lakes.
* **im Biergarten sitzen:** Sitting outdoors in beer gardens under chestnut trees.
* **grillen:** Having a barbecue in the park or garden (*„Wir grillen am Samstag!“*).

### 2. Im Herbst (Autumn)
* **wandern:** Hiking in forests or the Alps.
* **Kürbissuppe essen:** Eating pumpkin soup.
* **Pilze sammeln:** Mushroom foraging in forests.

### 3. Im Winter (Winter)
* **auf den Weihnachtsmarkt gehen:** Visiting the traditional Christmas markets in December (glühwein, roasted almonds, wooden stalls).
* **Ski fahren / Schlitten fahren:** Skiing and sledding in the mountains.

### 4. Im Frühling (Spring)
* **Fahrrad fahren:** Biking when the weather warms up.
* **Spargelzeit:** The sacred German white asparagus season from April to June 24th!
