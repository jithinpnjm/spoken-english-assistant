# 8.2 Seasons & Climate (Jahreszeiten und Klima)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can name the four seasons in German and know that they are ALL masculine (**der**).
* I know that seasons use the preposition **im** (*im Sommer, im Winter*).
* I can associate months with their seasons.
* I can talk about typical German seasonal activities (like Christmas markets and outdoor lakes).

---

## 1. The 4 Seasons: All Masculine (der)!

In German, all four seasons share the exact same masculine gender (**der**):

| Season | Article | Months Included | Typical Climate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Frühling** | der | März, April, Mai | Mild, Blumen blühen, sonnig |
| **der Sommer** | der | Juni, Juli, August | Warm bis heiß, lange Tage, oft 20–30 °C |
| **der Herbst** | der | September, Oktober, November| Bunte Blätter, windig, regnerisch |
| **der Winter** | der | Dezember, Januar, Februar | Kalt, Schnee, kurze Tage, minus Grade |

### The Golden Preposition: "im"
Just like months (Chapter 1.5), seasons take the preposition **im** (*in + dem*):
* *„**Im Sommer** fahre ich gern an den See.“* (In summer, I like going to the lake).
* *„**Im Winter** trinke ich gern heißen Tee.“* (In winter, I like drinking hot tea).

---

## 2. Seasonal Traditions & Activities in Germany

Germany's lifestyle transforms dramatically across the four seasons:

### 1. Im Sommer (Summer)
* **an den Badesee fahren:** Going to swimming lakes.
* **im Biergarten sitzen:** Sitting outdoors in beer gardens under chestnut trees.
* **grillen:** Having a barbecue in the park or garden (*„Wir grillen am Samstag!“*).

### 2. Im Herbst (Autumn)
* **wandern:** Hiking in forests or the Alps.
* **Kürbissuppe essen:** Eating pumpkin soup.
* **Pilze sammeln:** Mushroom foraging in forests (only pick what you know for certain – some mushrooms are poisonous).

### 3. Im Winter (Winter)
* **auf den Weihnachtsmarkt gehen:** Visiting the Christmas markets, which usually open in late November and run until
  shortly before Christmas (*Glühwein*, roasted almonds, wooden stalls).
* **Ski fahren / Schlitten fahren:** Skiing and sledding in the mountains.

### 4. Im Frühling (Spring)
* **Fahrrad fahren:** Biking when the weather warms up.
* **Spargelzeit:** The much-loved white asparagus season – roughly April to June; traditionally it ends on 24 June.

### Season and holiday words with articles
| Word | Plural | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **die Jahreszeit** | die Jahreszeiten | season | *Meine Lieblingsjahreszeit ist der Herbst.* |
| **der Monat** | die Monate | month | *Der Juli ist oft heiß.* |
| **der Schnee** | — | snow | *Im Winter gibt es manchmal Schnee.* |
| **das Blatt** | die Blätter | leaf | *Im Herbst sind die Blätter bunt.* |
| **der Feiertag** | die Feiertage | public holiday | *Am 3. Oktober ist Feiertag.* |
| **der Weihnachtsmarkt** | die Weihnachtsmärkte | Christmas market | *Der Weihnachtsmarkt ist sehr voll.* |
| **das Fest** | die Feste | festival, celebration | *Frohes Fest!* |

**Holidays and dates you will hear:** *Weihnachten* (24–26 December; the 24th evening, *Heiligabend*, is the main family
evening), *Silvester* (31 December) and *Neujahr*, *Ostern* (Easter, date changes), *der Tag der Deutschen Einheit*
(3 October, national holiday). Other holidays differ between federal states.
Wishes: *Frohe Weihnachten! · Guten Rutsch! (for New Year) · Frohe Ostern!*

**Clock change:** Germany switches to summer time (*die Sommerzeit*) on the last Sunday in March and back to winter time
on the last Sunday in October – *„Heute Nacht wird die Uhr umgestellt.“*
