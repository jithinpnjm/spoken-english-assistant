# 10.2 Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Listening (Hören)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the 3-part structure, audio pacing, and rules of the Goethe A1 Listening exam.
* I know which parts are played **twice** and which part is played **only once**!
* I know how to handle **distractors** (when several numbers or times are mentioned).
* I can extract key facts from train station announcements, voicemails, and short dialogues.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Audio Pacing: The 20-Minute Overview

The Listening module (**Hören**) lasts approximately **20 minutes** and consists of **15 items**:

| Section | Audio Type | How Many Times Played? | Question Format | What is tested? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Teil 1** (items 1–6) | Short everyday conversations (shops, train, café)| **TWICE (2x)** | Multiple choice: a, b, c | Prices, quantities, times, places |
| **Teil 2** (items 7–10) | Public announcements (station, airport, store)| ⚠️ **ONLY ONCE (1x)!** | Richtig / Falsch | Platform changes, gates, closing times |
| **Teil 3** (items 11–15) | Phone / answering-machine style messages | **TWICE (2x)** | Multiple choice: a, b, c | Times, days, places, what to bring or do |

Source: official Goethe A1 Modellsatz and the Goethe *Durchführungsbestimmungen* for Start Deutsch 1 (valid from
1 September 2025). For each text you first read the task, then hear the text.

**Important:** listening practice must start with spoken German, before you see a transcript. Use voice practice below or ask a partner to read the hidden scripts. Text-only chat checks reading comprehension, not listening.

**Exam-day facts (verified against the official regulations):**
* Hören usually comes first; the recording is played for the whole room. You mark your answers on the task sheets first
  and then transfer them to the answer sheet (*Antwortbogen*) – about **5 minutes** for this are included in the ~20 minutes.
* **Scoring:** 1 point per item, so **15 raw points**. Each written module is converted to the 100-point scale
  (raw points × 1.66), so Hören is worth about **25 of the 100 points**.
* Dictionaries are not allowed. Some Goethe centres also offer a **digital** version on a laptop with headphones – the tasks
  are the same, and you enter answers directly (no transfer to an answer sheet).
* Everything is heard **twice** except Teil 2 (announcements), which is heard **once**.

## 2. ⚠️ The #1 Listening Trap: The "Distractor" (Der Ablenker)

In Teil 1 and Teil 3 you will often hear **more than one** of the answer options in the audio. A typical pattern is that a first suggestion is changed or corrected:

> **Transcript:**  
> *„Treffen wir uns um **zwei Uhr**?“*  
> *„Nein, um zwei habe ich einen Termin. Geht es um **halb vier**?“*  
> *„Ja, halb vier passt mir gut.“*

*Question:* Wann treffen sich die Freunde?  
* Option A: 14:00 Uhr  
* Option B: 15:30 Uhr  
* Option C: 16:00 Uhr  

*The Trap:* If you only listen for the first time you hear (*„zwei Uhr“*), you will choose A and lose the point! **Always listen to the entire sentence to hear the final agreed-upon answer (B: 15:30 Uhr).**

---

## 3. Teil 2 Warning: Played Only ONCE!

Teil 2 consists of automated public loudspeaker announcements:
* **Department store:** *„Kunden im 2. Stock: Das Café schließt in 10 Minuten.“*
* **Airport:** *„Passagiere für Flug LH 402 nach New York: Bitte begeben Sie sich sofort zu Gate A 18.“*
* **Railway station:** *„Achtung an Gleis 3: Der ICE nach München hält heute an Gleis 5.“*

### 🔍 Strategy:
You read the statement before the announcement is played – and you hear it only once. 
**Underline the key noun or number while you read**, so you know exactly what your ears need to catch.

## 5. Practice all three A1 listening task types

Use voice practice or ask a partner to read each hidden script. Read the question first, listen without looking at the script, and answer before revealing it.

### Teil 1 — Short conversation (heard twice)

**Question:** Wann beginnt der Film? <br />
A) Um 18 Uhr · B) Um 19:30 Uhr · C) Um 20 Uhr

<details>
  <summary>Partner/coach script — reveal after answering</summary>

  *„Der Film beginnt um 18 Uhr.“ – „Um sechs? Das schaffe ich nicht. Gibt es noch eine Vorstellung?“ – „Ja, um halb acht.“ – „Gut, dann nehme ich die Vorstellung um halb acht.“*

  **Answer: B.** The first time is changed; *halb acht* is 19:30.
</details>

### Teil 2 — Public announcement (heard once)

**Statement:** *Der Zug nach Mainz fährt heute von Gleis 3.* **Richtig oder falsch?**

<details>
  <summary>Partner/coach script — reveal after answering</summary>

  *„Achtung an Gleis 2: Der Zug nach Mainz fährt heute von Gleis 5. Bitte achten Sie auf die Anzeige.“*

  **Answer: Falsch.** The train leaves from Gleis 5.
</details>

### Teil 3 — Voicemail (heard twice)

**Question:** Wann soll Leonie zum Deutschkurs kommen? <br />
A) Am Montag um 9 Uhr · B) Am Dienstag um 9 Uhr · C) Am Dienstag um 10 Uhr

<details>
  <summary>Partner/coach script — reveal after answering</summary>

  *„Hallo Leonie, hier ist Frau Weber vom Deutschkurs. Der Kurs am Montag um neun fällt aus. Bitte kommen Sie am Dienstag um zehn Uhr. Bringen Sie Ihr Buch mit. Auf Wiederhören.“*

  **Answer: C.** Monday is cancelled; the new time is Tuesday at 10.
</details>
