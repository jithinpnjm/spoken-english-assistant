# 10.2 Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Listening (Hören)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the 3-part structure, audio pacing, and rules of the Goethe A1 Listening exam.
* I know which parts are played **twice** and which part is played **only once**!
* I know how to spot the notorious **"Distractor Trap"** (where multiple numbers/times are mentioned).
* I can extract key facts from train station announcements, voicemails, and short dialogues.

---

## 1. Exam Structure & Audio Pacing: The 20-Minute Overview

The Listening module (**Hören**) lasts approximately **20 minutes** and consists of **15 items**:

| Section | Audio Type | How Many Times Played? | Question Format | What is tested? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Teil 1** | Short everyday dialogues (shops, train, café)| **TWICE (2x)** | Multiple choice: A, B, C | Prices, quantities, drinks, times |
| **Teil 2** | Public announcements (station, airport, store)| ⚠️ **ONLY ONCE (1x)!** | True / False (Richtig/Falsch) | Platform changes, flight gates, closing |
| **Teil 3** | Voicemails & answering machine messages | **TWICE (2x)** | Multiple choice: A, B, C | Phone numbers, appointment times, days |

---

## 2. ⚠️ The #1 Listening Trap: The "Distractor" (Der Ablenker)

In almost every question in Teil 1 and Teil 3, **you will hear ALL THREE options mentioned in the audio!**
Examiners design the dialogue so that the first option mentioned is changed or corrected:

> **Transcript:**  
> *„Treffen wir uns um **zwei Uhr**?“*  
> *„Nein, um zwei habe ich einen Termin. Geht es um **halb vier**?“*  
> *„Ja, halb vier passt mir gut.“*

*Question:* Wann treffen sich die Freunde?  
* Option A: 14:00 Uhr  
* Option B: 15:30 Uhr  
* Option C: 16:00 Uhr  

*The Trap:* If you only listen for the first time you hear (*„zwei Uhr“*), you will choose A and lose the point! **Always listen to the entire sentence to hear the final agreed-upon answer (B: 15:30 Uhr).**

---

## 3. Teil 2 Warning: Played Only ONCE!

Teil 2 consists of automated public loudspeaker announcements:
* **Department store:** *„Kunden im 2. Stock: Das Café schließt in 10 Minuten.“*
* **Airport:** *„Passagiere für Flug LH 402 nach New York: Bitte begeben Sie sich sofort zu Gate A 18.“*
* **Railway station:** *„Achtung an Gleis 3: Der ICE nach München hält heute an Gleis 5.“*

### 🔍 Strategy:
You have approximately **10 seconds** before the audio plays to read the statement. 
**Underline the target noun or number before the audio starts**, so you know exactly what your ears need to catch!
