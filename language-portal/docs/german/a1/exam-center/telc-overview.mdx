# 10.5 telc Deutsch A1: Overview & Structure (Start Deutsch 1)

## 🎯 What you will learn

* I know how telc A1 compares to the Goethe-Zertifikat A1.

## 1. What is telc Deutsch A1?

**telc** (*The European Language Certificates*) is an international testing body headquartered in Frankfurt. 
Along with the Goethe-Institut, telc co-developed the **Start Deutsch 1** examination framework. 

Both the **Goethe-Zertifikat A1** and **telc Deutsch A1** certificates are:
* Based on the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) level A1.
* Commonly used as proof of basic German, for example for the spouse/family reunification visa (*Ehegattennachzug*). Which certificates are accepted is decided by the German mission or authority handling your case – check their current information.

## 2. Exam Structure & Time Allocation

The telc Deutsch A1 exam is divided into two parts:

### A. Schriftliche Prüfung (Written Exam — ~65 minutes of tasks; telc's timetable shows 75 minutes including 10 minutes for formalities)
1. **Hören (Listening — ~20 minutes):**
   * Teil 1: 6 everyday dialogues played twice (Multiple Choice A, B, C).
   * Teil 2: 4 public announcements played once (Richtig / Falsch).
   * Teil 3: 5 telephone answering messages played twice (Multiple Choice A, B, C).
2. **Lesen & Schreiben (Reading & Writing — 45 minutes together):**
   * *Lesen (15 items):* Emails, website ads, and public notices.
   * *Schreiben:* Form-filling (5 items) + 30-word written message.

### B. Mündliche Prüfung (Oral Exam — ~15 minutes, usually a group of 4 candidates)
* **Teil 1 (~3 min):** Self-introduction from a keyword sheet + spelling and numbers.
* **Teil 2 (~4 min):** Asking for and giving information on two everyday themes (2 cards per candidate).
* **Teil 3 (~4 min):** Making and reacting to requests (2 picture/word cards per candidate).
* There is no preparation time. You talk with the other candidates and with the examiner (*Interlokutor*).

*Source: telc Deutsch A1 (Start Deutsch 1) Übungstest 1 and the exam page on telc.net (checked September 2026).*

---

## 3. How telc Scoring Works

telc reports this exam on a **60-point scale** – 15 points each for Hören, Lesen, Schreiben and Sprechen (the tasks and the
marking criteria are the same as Goethe's; Goethe converts the same raw points to 100).

| Result points (telc) | Grade |
| :--- | :--- |
| 54–60 | sehr gut |
| 48–53,5 | gut |
| 42–47,5 | befriedigend |
| 36–41,5 | ausreichend (pass) |
| 0–35,5 | teilgenommen (participated – not passed) |

So you need **at least 36 of 60 points (60 %)**. The official telc Übungstest shows only this overall scale; some
unofficial websites additionally claim a separate 60 % minimum for the written and the oral part. **We could not confirm
this in telc's own material – check the current telc exam regulations (*Prüfungsordnung*) or ask your test centre.**
(Goethe's rule: 60 of 100 points in total, all parts taken – see 10.6.)

Like Goethe's version, telc Deutsch A1 cannot be taken as a partial exam.

## 4. Note on "telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer"

Some test providers offer different A1 exam versions or formats. Before booking, ask your course provider or test centre for the exact A1 exam name and format you need.
