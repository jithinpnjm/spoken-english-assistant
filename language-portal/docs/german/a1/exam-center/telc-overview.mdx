# 10.5 telc Deutsch A1: Overview & Structure (Start Deutsch 1)

## 🎯 What you will learn

* I know how telc A1 compares to the Goethe-Zertifikat A1.

* I know what A1 certificates are commonly used for in Germany – and what they are *not* enough for.

---

## 1. What is telc Deutsch A1?

**telc** (*The European Language Certificates*) is an international testing body headquartered in Frankfurt. 
Along with the Goethe-Institut, telc co-developed the **Start Deutsch 1** examination framework. 

Both the **Goethe-Zertifikat A1** and **telc Deutsch A1** certificates are:
* Based on the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) level A1.
* Commonly used as proof of basic German, for example for the spouse/family reunification visa (*Ehegattennachzug*). Which certificates are accepted is decided by the German mission or authority handling your case – check their current information.
* **Not** enough for everything: permanent residence or naturalisation usually require higher levels (typically B1). Rules change, so always check the current official requirements.

---

## 2. Exam Structure & Time Allocation

The telc Deutsch A1 exam is divided into two parts:

### A. Schriftliche Prüfung (Written Exam — Total ~65 Minutes)
1. **Hören (Listening — ~20 minutes):**
   * Teil 1: 6 everyday dialogues played twice (Multiple Choice A, B, C).
   * Teil 2: 4 public announcements played once (Richtig / Falsch).
   * Teil 3: 5 telephone answering messages played twice (Multiple Choice A, B, C).
2. **Lesen & Schreiben (Reading & Writing — 45 minutes together):**
   * *Lesen (15 items):* Emails, website ads, and public notices.
   * *Schreiben:* Form-filling (5 items) + 30-word written message.

### B. Mündliche Prüfung (Oral Exam — Total ~15 Minutes in Pairs)
* **Teil 1:** Self-introduction from prompt card + spelling and numbers.
* **Teil 2:** Exchanging information using word prompt cards.
* **Teil 3:** Making and answering requests using picture prompt cards.

---

## 3. How telc Scoring Works (The 60% Rule)

The exam is scored out of a maximum of **100 total points**:

```text
Schriftliche Prüfung (Written):  75 points max  ➔  Need at least 45 points (60%)
Mündliche Prüfung (Oral):        25 points max  ➔  Need at least 15 points (60%)
================================================================================
TOTAL:                          100 points max  ➔  Passing mark: 60 points overall
```

⚠️ **Dual-pass rule (telc):** telc's published rules for this exam require 60 % in the written part *and* 60 % in the oral part. Check the current telc handbook before your exam. (Goethe's rule for the same exam format is 60 points in total – see 10.6.)

---

## 4. Note on "telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer"

telc also offers **telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer**, a version aimed at immigrants with more everyday-Germany topics. Do not confuse it with the exam at the end of an integration course (*Integrationskurs*): that is the **DTZ – Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer**, which tests A2–B1. Ask your course provider or the authority which exam you actually need.
