# 10.4 Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Speaking (Sprechen)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the 3-part group exam structure of the Goethe A1 Speaking exam (~15 minutes).
* I can introduce myself with short sentences in **Teil 1** and answer the spelling and number questions.
* I can formulate W-questions and Yes/No questions from theme cards in **Teil 2**.
* I can make a short everyday request from a picture card and respond to my partner.
* I know how to interact naturally with other test candidates.

---

## 1. Exam Atmosphere & Group Format (~15 Minutes)

The Speaking exam is a **group exam with up to four candidates** and **two examiners**:
* The **moderator** gives all instructions and asks follow-up questions if something was unclear.
* The **assessor** scores the candidates; both examiners agree on the result at the end.
* In Teil 2 and Teil 3 **you talk mainly with the other candidates**; in Teil 1 you speak to the examiners.

*Source:* official Goethe A1 Modellsatz (examiner notes) and *Durchführungsbestimmungen* (from 1 September 2025).

**Exam-day facts:**
* There is **no preparation time** – you get the cards during the exam. Each of the three parts takes about **5 minutes**.
* Before each part the examiner explains the task and shows an example – listen to it, it tells you exactly what to do.
* **Scoring:** Teil 1 = 3 points, Teil 2 = 6 points, Teil 3 = 6 points (15 raw points, ≈ 25 of 100 after conversion).
  For each task you get full points (done and understandable), half points (only partly, because of language or content
  problems) or 0.
* Two examiners mark you independently; the result is the average. The Sprechen result is not influenced by your
  written result (the examiners don't see it).
* If an exam has only one candidate, you do Teil 2 and 3 with an examiner.
* Asking for repetition (*Wie bitte? Noch einmal, bitte.*) is normal communication and not a mistake.

## 2. Teil 1: Self-Introduction (Sich vorstellen)

A sheet with keywords is placed where everyone can see it, for example:

```text
       Name?         Alter?         Land?
       Wohnort?      Sprachen?      Beruf?
                     Hobby?
```

### One possible self-introduction
> *„Guten Tag! Ich heiße Maria. Ich bin 28 Jahre alt und komme aus Spanien. Ich wohne in Bonn. Ich spreche Spanisch und ein bisschen Deutsch. Ich bin Lehrerin. In meiner Freizeit lese ich gern.“*

Use the keywords on the sheet and give your own information. You do not need to memorize a model paragraph.

### ⚠️ The Follow-Up Questions from the Examiner:
After your introduction, the moderator asks you to **spell** something and to say a **number**:
1. **Spelling** (e.g. first name, surname, street, home town): *„Können Sie bitte Ihren Nachnamen buchstabieren?“*  
   ➔ You spell: *„J - O - S - E - P - H“* (using German letter sounds from Chapter 1.1!).
2. **A number** (phone, mobile, postcode, house number …): *„Wie ist Ihre Telefonnummer / Postleitzahl?“*  
   ➔ You say: *„Null - eins - sieben - sechs...“*

## 3. Teil 2: Asking & Giving Information (Theme Cards)

There are **two themes** (for example *Einkaufen*, then *Wochenende*). For each theme every candidate draws a word card, asks the next person a question about it, and answers the question they are asked. Examples:
* *Theme:* **Essen und Trinken** | *Word:* **Kaffee**  
  ➔ You ask your partner: *„Trinken Sie morgens gern Kaffee?“*  
  ➔ Partner answers: *„Ja, ich trinke jeden Tag zwei Tassen Kaffee.“*
* *Theme:* **Freizeit** | *Word:* **Wochenende**  
  ➔ You ask: *„Was machen Sie am Wochenende?“*  
  ➔ Partner answers: *„Am Wochenende gehe ich im Park spazieren.“*

---

## 4. Teil 3: Requests from Picture Cards (Bitten formulieren)

You draw picture cards showing common everyday objects or actions (for example, a glass of water, a pen, or an open window). Make one short request to your partner. Useful A1 frames include:
* *„Ein Glas Wasser, bitte.“*
* *„Geben Sie mir bitte einen Stift.“*
* *„Können Sie bitte das Fenster öffnen?“*
* *„Könnten Sie bitte …?“* is also useful as a memorized polite phrase; you do not need to study its verb form in this exam lesson.
* Your partner must react naturally:
  * *„Ja, natürlich, hier bitte!“* or *„Gerne, mache ich sofort.“*
