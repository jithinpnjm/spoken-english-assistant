## 1. The Full Exam Day Schedule (~80 Minutes Total)

A typical A1 exam day looks like this (the exact schedule – including whether Sprechen is on the same day – is set by your exam centre):

```text
1. HÖREN (Listening)       ➔  ~20 minutes  (15 questions)
2. LESEN (Reading)         ➔   25 minutes  (15 questions)
3. SCHREIBEN (Writing)     ➔   20 minutes  (Form + 30-word Email)
------------------------------------------------------------------
[Break / separate slot]    ➔   depends on the exam centre
------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SPRECHEN (Speaking)     ➔  ~15 minutes  (group of up to 4 candidates)
```

## 2. The 100-Point Scoring Matrix

According to Goethe's published information for Start Deutsch 1:

| Part | Content | Maximum |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Written exam** | Hören (15 items) + Lesen (15 items) + Schreiben (form + ca. 30-word message) | **75 points** |
| **Oral exam** | Sprechen (3 parts, group exam) | **25 points** |
| **Total** | | **100 points** |

You **pass with at least 60 points** in total, and you must take all parts. Each of Hören, Lesen, Schreiben and Sprechen
has 15 raw points, which are multiplied by 1.66 and added up (then rounded). (telc reports telc Deutsch A1 on its own
scale – see 10.5.)

**Three more official rules worth knowing (Goethe, from 1 September 2025):**
* If you score **under 35 points in the written part**, 60 in total is no longer reachable – the regulations say taking
  Sprechen then makes no sense.
* Start Deutsch 1 can **only be retaken as a whole** (no single-module resits at A1).
* If written and oral are on different days, they are at most 14 days apart.

### Grade Bands (official Goethe bands for Start Deutsch 1):
* **90–100 points:** Sehr gut (Very Good)
* **80–89 points:** Gut (Good)
* **70–79 points:** Befriedigend (Satisfactory)
* **60–69 points:** Ausreichend (Sufficient / Pass)
* **0–59 points:** Nicht bestanden (Did not pass)

## 3. Targeted Weakness-Based Error Analysis

If you take a mock test and miss points, do not simply redo the whole test. Connect your error directly back to the relevant chapter in this portal:

| If you lost points on... | What to review in the Portal: |
| :--- | :--- |
| Missing appointment times or prices in **Hören** | ➔ Chapter 1.5 (Numbers & Time) & Chapter 10.2 (Distractor Trap) |
| Mixing up dates or street signs in **Lesen** | ➔ Chapter 1.2 (Compound Words) & Chapter 4.6 (City Places) |
| Losing points on the **Formular** in Schreiben | ➔ Chapter 2.1 (Personal Info) & Chapter 7.8 (Anmeldung Forms) |
| Missing points on the **Email** in Schreiben | ➔ Chapter 6.8 (Email Basics) & Chapter 3.1 (Noun Capitalization) |
| Hesitating during self-introduction in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 1.6 (First Sentences) & Chapter 1.1 (Spelling Names) |
| Struggling to ask questions from cards in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 3.7 (V2 Rule) & Chapter 3.8 (Questions) |
| Struggling to make requests from pictures in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 3.15 (Imperative) & Chapter 9.3 (Making Requests) |

<details>

## 4b. Doing a Real Mock Exam (free official material)

This portal gives you original practice, not official tests. For a realistic full mock:
1. Download the free **Goethe A1 Modellsatz / Übungssatz** (with audio) from goethe.de (search "Start Deutsch 1 Übungsmaterialien")
   and/or the free **telc Deutsch A1 Übungstest** (with audio) from telc.net.
2. Do the written part **in one sitting without a break**: Hören (~20 min) → Lesen + Schreiben (45 min). Set a timer.
3. Mark Hören and Lesen with the answer key (1 point per item). For Schreiben Teil 2, score yourself with the 3/1.5/0 + 1/0.5/0
   scheme from [10.3](./goethe-schreiben.mdx) – or paste it into the AI writing practice for feedback.
4. Do Sprechen with a study partner or the AI simulation in [10.4](./goethe-sprechen.mdx), recording yourself if possible.
5. Put every lost point into the table in section 3 and review those chapters before your next mock.

### Check that your mock covers all four skills

| Skill | A1 practice to complete |
|---|---|
| **Hören** | Listen to the three A1 task types with audio; answer before checking the transcript. |
| **Lesen** | Complete all three reading task types, including signs/notices and short ads. |
| **Schreiben** | Fill all five form entries, then write one short message covering all three points. |
| **Sprechen** | Complete all three oral parts: self-introduction, ask/give information, request/react. |

The oral AI activity below practises **speaking only**. It is not a full four-skill mock or a readiness score.
