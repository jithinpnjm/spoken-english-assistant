## 1. The Full Exam Day Schedule (~80 Minutes Total)

A typical A1 exam day looks like this (the exact schedule – including whether Sprechen is on the same day – is set by your exam centre):

```text
1. HÖREN (Listening)       ➔  ~20 minutes  (15 questions)
2. LESEN (Reading)         ➔   25 minutes  (15 questions)
3. SCHREIBEN (Writing)     ➔   20 minutes  (Form + 30-word Email)
------------------------------------------------------------------
[Break / separate slot]    ➔   depends on the exam centre
------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SPRECHEN (Speaking)     ➔  ~15 minutes  (group of up to 4 candidates)
```

## 2. The 100-Point Scoring Matrix

According to Goethe's published information for Start Deutsch 1:

| Part | Content | Maximum |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Written exam** | Hören (15 items) + Lesen (15 items) + Schreiben (form + ca. 30-word message) | **75 points** |
| **Oral exam** | Sprechen (3 parts, group exam) | **25 points** |
| **Total** | | **100 points** |

You **pass with at least 60 points** in total, and you must take all parts. (telc sets its own rules for telc Deutsch A1 – see 10.5.)

### Grade Bands (as commonly shown on results; check your certificate information):
* **90–100 points:** Sehr gut (Very Good)
* **80–89 points:** Gut (Good)
* **70–79 points:** Befriedigend (Satisfactory)
* **60–69 points:** Ausreichend (Sufficient / Pass)
* **0–59 points:** Nicht bestanden (Did not pass)

## 3. Targeted Weakness-Based Error Analysis

If you take a mock test and miss points, do not simply redo the whole test. Connect your error directly back to the relevant chapter in this portal:

| If you lost points on... | What to review in the Portal: |
| :--- | :--- |
| Missing appointment times or prices in **Hören** | ➔ Chapter 1.5 (Numbers & Time) & Chapter 10.2 (Distractor Trap) |
| Mixing up dates or street signs in **Lesen** | ➔ Chapter 1.2 (Compound Words) & Chapter 4.6 (City Places) |
| Losing points on the **Formular** in Schreiben | ➔ Chapter 2.1 (Personal Info) & Chapter 7.8 (Anmeldung Forms) |
| Missing points on the **Email** in Schreiben | ➔ Chapter 6.8 (Email Basics) & Chapter 3.1 (Noun Capitalization) |
| Hesitating during self-introduction in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 1.6 (First Sentences) & Chapter 1.1 (Spelling Names) |
| Struggling to ask questions from cards in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 3.7 (V2 Rule) & Chapter 3.8 (Questions) |
| Struggling to make requests from pictures in **Sprechen** | ➔ Chapter 3.15 (Imperative) & Chapter 9.3 (Making Requests) |
