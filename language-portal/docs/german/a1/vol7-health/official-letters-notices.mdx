# 7.9 Official Letters & Notices (Behördenbriefe)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can identify who sent an official German letter (**der Absender**).
* I know what the file reference number (**das Aktenzeichen**) is and why to quote it.
* I can find the payment or reply deadline (**die Frist**).
* I understand terms for reminders (**die Mahnung**) and official decisions (**der Bescheid**).
* I know how to ask a colleague or neighbor for help understanding a letter.

---

## 1. Why Official Mail Matters in Germany

Germany is famous for communicating important matters via physical paper mail (**die Post**). 
Whether it is from the tax office (*Finanzamt*), health insurance (*Krankenkasse*), or the television fee service (*Beitragsservice / GEZ*), **never throw away an unopened official letter!**

Ignoring official letters leads to automated late penalties and warning notices (**Mahnungen**).

---

## 2. The 4 Essential Clues on Every German Letter

When you open an official letter, scan for these four key fields:

```text
1. ABSENDER (Top Left / Header)
   ➔ Who is writing to you? (e.g. Stadt Frankfurt am Main / Finanzamt)

2. AKTENZEICHEN / GESCHÄFTSZEICHEN (Subject Line)
   ➔ Your unique file reference number. Whenever you reply or call, quote this number!

3. FRIST / ZAHLBAR BIS (Dates in Bold)
   ➔ The deadline! By which date must you pay or respond? (e.g. „Frist: 14 Tage“ / „bis zum 15.07.“)

4. BETRAG / ÜBERWEISUNG (Numbers with €)
   ➔ Do you owe money, or are you getting a refund?
```

---

## 3. Core Administrative Letter Vocabulary

| German Term | Article & Plural | English Meaning | What to do? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **das Aktenzeichen** | die Aktenzeichen | case reference number | Quote in all replies |
| **die Frist** | die Fristen | deadline / time limit | Respond before this date! |
| **der Bescheid** | die Bescheide | official notice / decision | Keep in your files |
| **die Mahnung** | die Mahnungen | payment reminder / warning | Pay immediately to avoid extra fees |
| **die Gebühr** | die Gebühren | administrative fee | Transfer amount |
| **der Nachweis** | die Nachweise | proof / document evidence | Send copies of requested papers |
| **der Beitragsservice** | — | TV/radio public license fee | Universal fee per household in DE |

---

## 4. How to Ask a Native Speaker for Help

If you receive a complicated letter from an authority (*Behörde*) and don't understand the legal German, ask a German friend, neighbor, or colleague:

> *„Entschuldigung, ich habe diesen Brief von der Krankenkasse bekommen. Mein Deutsch ist noch nicht so gut. Könnten Sie mir kurz helfen? Was muss ich hier tun?“*  
> *(Excuse me, I received this letter from my health insurance. My German isn't that good yet. Could you help me for a moment? What do I need to do here?)*
