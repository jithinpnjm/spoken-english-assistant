# 7.6 Phone & Internet Services (Handy und Internet)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can talk about mobile phones (**das Handy**) and internet connections.
* I know how to ask for the Wi-Fi password in a café or hotel (**das WLAN-Passwort**).
* I can report an internet outage (**die Störung**) to customer support.
* I can understand mobile contract terms (**Prepaid**, **Vertrag**, **Datenvolumen**).

---

## 1. 📱 The German Word "Handy"

In Germany, almost everyone calls a mobile phone **das Handy**:
* *„Wo ist mein **Handy**?“* (Where is my phone?)
* *„Ich lade mein **Handy** auf.“* (I am charging my phone).
* *Note:* *Handy* is a German-invented word! In English, native speakers say *mobile phone* or *cell phone*.

### Core Mobile Vocabulary:
| Word | Plural | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **das Handy** | die Handys | mobile phone | *Mein Handy ist kaputt.* |
| **die SIM-Karte** | die SIM-Karten | SIM card | *Ich brauche eine neue SIM-Karte.* |
| **der Handyvertrag** / **der Vertrag** | die Verträge | (mobile) contract | *Der Vertrag läuft 24 Monate.* |
| **die Prepaid-Karte** | die Prepaid-Karten | prepaid SIM | *Ich lade meine Prepaid-Karte auf.* |
| **der Tarif** | die Tarife | plan, tariff | *Welcher Tarif ist günstig?* |
| **das Datenvolumen** | — | data allowance | *Ich habe 20 Gigabyte Datenvolumen pro Monat.* |
| **das Netz / der Empfang** | — | network / reception | *Ich habe hier im Zug kein Netz!* |
| **das Ladekabel** | die Ladekabel | charging cable | *Hast du ein Ladekabel für mich?* |
| **die Rechnung** | die Rechnungen | bill | *Die Rechnung ist zu hoch.* |
| **die Kündigung** | die Kündigungen | cancellation (of a contract) | *Ich schicke die Kündigung per E-Mail.* |

**Good to know (as of 2026 – check current rules):**
* For a **prepaid SIM** you must verify your identity (passport/ID, often by video call or in a shop) before it works.
* A **contract** can have an initial term of at most 24 months; after that it can usually be cancelled with one month's
  notice. Many providers also offer monthly plans – ask: *„Gibt es auch einen Vertrag ohne Mindestlaufzeit?“*

## 2. 📶 Wi-Fi in Germany: "Das WLAN"

In Germany, Wi-Fi is usually called **das WLAN** (pronounced **„Weh-lahn“** – the German letter name of W, *weh*, plus *lan*; to English ears it sounds like "vay-lahn"):

* **das WLAN-Passwort:** Wi-Fi password.
* When asking in a café, hotel, or at a friend's house:
  * *„Entschuldigung, **haben Sie WLAN**?“* (Excuse me, do you have Wi-Fi?)
  * *„Wie ist das **WLAN-Passwort**, bitte?“* (What is the Wi-Fi password, please?)

---

## 3. Reporting Internet Problems: "Die Störung"

If your home internet suddenly stops working, call or message technical support:

* **der Router** (die Router) — *home internet router*
* **die Störung** (die Störungen) — *technical outage / disruption*
  * *„Guten Tag, mein Internet funktioniert seit heute Morgen nicht. Gibt es eine **Störung**?“*
  * *„Starten Sie bitte den **Router** neu.“* (Please restart your router).
