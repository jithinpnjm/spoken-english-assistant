# 7.8 The "Anmeldung" & Official Forms (Die Wohnsitzanmeldung)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the legal requirement of the **Anmeldung** (address registration) in Germany.
* I know what the landlord confirmation document (**die Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**) is.
* I know what the registration certificate (**die Meldebescheinigung**) is and why it is needed.
* I can accurately fill out an official German paper form (*das Anmeldeformular*).

---

## 1. What is the "Anmeldung" and Why is it Mandatory?

In Germany, the law (*Bundesmeldegesetz*) requires **people who move into a home to register their address within two weeks** at the local citizens' office (*Bürgeramt*):

```text
Why the "Anmeldung" matters so much:
• After your first registration, your tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer) is sent to you by post.
• Banks, landlords, employers and phone/internet providers often ask for your Meldebescheinigung.
• Many other offices (e.g. the Ausländerbehörde, Kindergeld, Kita registration) need your registered address.
```

The registration certificate you receive at the end is called **die Meldebescheinigung**. Treat this piece of paper like gold!

---

## 2. The 2 Mandatory Documents You Must Bring

To your appointment, you must bring two items:

1. **Gültiger Reisepass / Ausweis:** Valid passport or EU identity card for every person registering.
2. **Die Wohnungsgeberbestätigung:** A 1-page paper signed by your landlord confirming that you have moved into the apartment on a specific date. (Your rental contract alone is **not** accepted!).

---

## 3. Decoding the German Registration Form (Das Formular)

When filling out the paper form, you must match your details to these standard German fields:

| German Field | English Meaning | Example Entry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Familienname / Nachname**| Last name / Surname | *Joseph* |
| **Vorname(n)** | First name(s) | *Jithin* |
| **Geburtsdatum** | Date of birth (DD.MM.YYYY) | *12.05.1994* |
| **Geburtsort** | City / Town of birth | *Kochi* |
| **Geburtsland** | Country of birth | *Indien* |
| **Staatsangehörigkeit** | Citizenship / Nationality | *indisch* (or *Indien*) |
| **Familienstand** | Marital status | *verheiratet / ledig* |
| **Religionsgesellschaft** | Religion (affects German church tax)| *keine (none) / römisch-katholisch* |
| **Einzugsdatum** | Move-in date | *01.06.2025* |
| **Neue Wohnung** | New street address & postal code | *Goethestraße 14, 60313 Frankfurt* |
