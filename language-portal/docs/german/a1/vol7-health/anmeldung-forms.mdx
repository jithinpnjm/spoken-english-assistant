# 7.8 The "Anmeldung" & Official Forms (Die Wohnsitzanmeldung)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the legal requirement of the **Anmeldung** (address registration) in Germany.
* I know what the landlord confirmation document (**die Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**) is.
* I know what the registration certificate (**die Meldebescheinigung**) is and why it is needed.
* I can accurately fill out an official German paper form (*das Anmeldeformular*).

---

## 1. What is the "Anmeldung" and Why is it Mandatory?

In Germany, the law (*Bundesmeldegesetz*) requires **people who move into a home to register their address within two weeks** at the local citizens' office (*Bürgeramt*):

```text
Why the "Anmeldung" matters so much:
• After your first registration, your tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer) is usually sent to you by post.
• Banks, landlords, employers and phone/internet providers often ask for your Meldebescheinigung.
• Many other offices (e.g. the Ausländerbehörde, Kindergeld, Kita registration) need your registered address.
```

The registration certificate you receive at the end is called **die Meldebescheinigung** (some offices say
*Meldebestätigung*). Keep it safe – you will need it often.

**Practical notes (check your city's website – procedures differ):** in many cities you need to book an appointment,
sometimes weeks ahead; some cities now also offer online registration. When you move within Germany you register at
the new address (*ummelden*); when you leave Germany you deregister (*sich abmelden*).

---

## 2. The Documents You Usually Need

To your appointment, you usually bring the completed form (*das Anmeldeformular*, often downloadable) plus:

1. **Gültiger Reisepass / Ausweis:** Valid passport or EU identity card for every person registering.
2. **Die Wohnungsgeberbestätigung:** A 1-page paper signed by your landlord confirming that you have moved into the apartment on a specific date. (Your rental contract alone is **not** accepted!).

---

## 3. Decoding the German Registration Form (Das Formular)

When filling out the paper form, you must match your details to these standard German fields:

| German Field | English Meaning | Example Entry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Familienname / der Nachname** | Last name / Surname | *Joseph* |
| **der Vorname** (pl. *die Vornamen*) | First name(s) | *Jithin* |
| **das Geburtsdatum** | Date of birth (DD.MM.YYYY) | *12.05.1994* |
| **der Geburtsort** | City / Town of birth | *Kochi* |
| **das Geburtsland** | Country of birth | *Indien* |
| **die Staatsangehörigkeit** | Citizenship / Nationality | *indisch* (or *Indien*) |
| **der Familienstand** | Marital status | *verheiratet / ledig* |
| **die Religionsgesellschaft** | Religion (relevant for church tax) | *keine (none) / römisch-katholisch* |
| **das Einzugsdatum** | Move-in date | *01.06.2025* |
| **die neue Wohnung** | New street address & postal code | *Goethestraße 14, 60313 Frankfurt* |
| **die bisherige Wohnung** | Previous address | *(your last address, also abroad)* |
| **die Unterschrift** | Signature | *(sign here)* |

### More registration words
| Word | Plural | English | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **die Anmeldung** | die Anmeldungen | registration | *Ich brauche einen Termin für die Anmeldung.* |
| **der Wohnsitz** | die Wohnsitze | (registered) residence | *Mein Wohnsitz ist in Köln.* |
| **der Umzug** | die Umzüge | move (house) | *Nach dem Umzug melde ich mich um.* |
| **der Vermieter / die Vermieterin** | die Vermieter / -innen | landlord / landlady | *Der Vermieter unterschreibt die Bestätigung.* |
| **der Nachweis** | die Nachweise | proof, document | *Welche Nachweise brauche ich?* |
| **die Frist** | die Fristen | deadline | *Die Frist ist zwei Wochen.* |
