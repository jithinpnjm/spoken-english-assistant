# 7.3 The Pharmacy: Buying Medicine (In der Apotheke)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I understand the crucial difference between an **Apotheke** (pharmacy) and a **Drogerie** (drugstore) in Germany.
* I know the difference between prescription medicines (**rezeptpflichtig**) and over-the-counter remedies (**rezeptfrei**).
* I can name common medicines (*Tabletten, Hustensaft, Nasenspray, Salbe, Pflaster*).
* I can ask for dosage instructions (*Wie oft muss ich das nehmen?*).

---

## 1. ⚠️ The Big German Trap: "Apotheke" vs. "Drogerie"

In English, the word "drugstore" can mean a place where you buy toothpaste, shampoo, and prescription medicine. 
**In Germany, these are two strictly separate stores:**

1. **Die Apotheke (Marked with the giant red letter "A"):**
   * This is a licensed pharmacy staffed by pharmacists.
   * You **must** go here to buy prescription drugs, antibiotics, and even basic painkillers like Ibuprofen and Paracetamol!
2. **Die Drogerie (e.g. dm, Rossmann, Müller):**
   * Sells cosmetics, shampoo, cleaning supplies, baby diapers, and tea.
   * In Germany, a *Drogerie* is legally **not allowed** to sell pharmaceuticals or pain relievers!

---

## 2. Filling a Prescription vs. Over-the-Counter

* **rezeptpflichtig:** Prescription-only (you cannot buy it without a signed doctor's *Rezept*).
  * *„Hier ist mein Rezept vom Arzt.“* (Here is my prescription from the doctor).
* **rezeptfrei:** Over-the-counter (available without a prescription).
  * *„Haben Sie etwas Rezeptfreies gegen Halsschmerzen?“* (Do you have something over-the-counter for a sore throat?)
* **die Zuzahlung:** Co-payment (on German statutory health insurance, you usually pay a co-pay of 5 € to 10 € per prescription item).

## 3. Common Medicines & Remedies (Medikamente)

| Remedy | Article & Plural | English Meaning | What is it for? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **die Tablette** | die Tabletten | tablet / pill | Kopfschmerzen, Fieber |
| **der Hustensaft** | die Hustensäfte | cough syrup | Husten |
| **das Nasenspray** | die Nasensprays | nasal spray | verstopfte Nase / Schnupfen |
| **die Salbe** | die Salben | ointment / healing cream | Haut, Wunden |
| **die Tropfen** | (plural) | drops | Augentropfen, Ohrentropfen |
| **das Pflaster** | die Pflaster | band-aid / adhesive plaster| Schnittwunden |
| **der Verband** | die Verbände | bandage | Verletzungen |

---

## 4. Understanding Dosage Instructions (Die Einnahme)

When the pharmacist explains how to take the medicine, listen for these standard phrases:

* *„Nehmen Sie **eine Tablette dreimal täglich**.“* (Take one pill three times daily).
* *„Vor dem Essen“* = *before meals*.
* *„Nach dem Essen“* = *after meals*.
* *„Vor dem Schlafen“* = *before going to sleep*.
* *„Mit viel Wasser einnehmen.“* (Take with plenty of water).
