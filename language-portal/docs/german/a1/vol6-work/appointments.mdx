# 6.6 Making Appointments: "Einen Termin vereinbaren"

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can schedule (**vereinbaren**), reschedule (**verschieben**), and cancel (**absagen**) appointments.
* I can ask whether a proposed time works (*Passt es Ihnen am...?*).
* I can accept (*Das passt mir gut*) or decline (*Da kann ich leider nicht*) a suggested time.
* I understand why appointment culture (**Terminkultur**) is central to German life.

---

## 1. German "Terminkultur": Everything Needs an Appointment!

In Germany, you rarely walk into an office, medical practice, bank, or city hall without an appointment:
> **„Haben Sie einen Termin?“** *(Do you have an appointment?)*

Whether you need to see a doctor (*Arzttermin*), register your address (*Bürgeramtstermin*), or talk to a client (*Geschäftstermin*), scheduling appointments is an essential daily skill.

---

## 2. The 4 Essential Appointment Verbs

| Action | German Verb | Everyday Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **To schedule / make** | **vereinbaren / ausmachen** | *Ich möchte einen Termin **vereinbaren**.* |
| **To reschedule / move** | **verschieben** | *Können wir den Termin auf Freitag **verschieben**?* |
| **To cancel** | **absagen** (separable!) | *Ich muss meinen Termin leider **absagen**.* |
| **To confirm** | **bestätigen** | *Ich **bestätige** unseren Termin am Montag.* |

---

## 3. Negotiating Days & Times: "Passt es Ihnen?"

When negotiating a meeting time, use the versatile verb **passen** (to suit / fit into your schedule):

### Asking if a time works:
* *„**Passt es Ihnen** am Donnerstag um 10 Uhr?“* (Formal: Does Thursday at 10 AM suit you?)
* *„**Passt es dir** morgen Nachmittag?“* (Informal: Does tomorrow afternoon work for you?)

### Answering:
* **Positive:**  
  * *„Ja, das **passt mir sehr gut**.“* (Yes, that suits me very well.)
  * *„Ja, da habe ich Zeit.“* (Yes, I have time then.)
* **Negative:**  
  * *„Nein, da **kann ich leider nicht**.“* (No, unfortunately I can't then.)
  * *„Nein, das **passt mir leider gar nicht**.“* (No, that doesn't suit me at all.)
  * *„Haben Sie vielleicht am Nachmittag Zeit?“* (Do you perhaps have time in the afternoon?)
