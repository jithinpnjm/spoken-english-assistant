# 6.8 Email & Messaging Basics (E-Mails und Nachrichten)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can choose the correct formal greeting (*Sehr geehrte Frau... / Sehr geehrter Herr...*).
* I can choose the correct informal greeting (*Liebe... / Lieber...*).
* I know the German **lowercase rule after commas** in email openings.
* I can conclude an email with appropriate sign-offs (*Mit freundlichen Grüßen* vs. *Viele Grüße*).
* I can write a short, clear 30-word message for daily situations in Germany.

---

## 1. Formal vs. Informal Email Framework

Writing in German requires picking the right "track" right from the first line:

| Component | Formal Track (Office, Landlord, Doctor, Officials) | Informal Track (Friends, Classmates, Family) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greeting** | *Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,* (Unknown name)<br/>*Sehr geehrte Frau Müller,* (Woman)<br/>*Sehr geehrter Herr Schmidt,* (Man — adds -er!) | *Liebe Sarah,* (Female friend)<br/>*Lieber Lukas,* (Male friend — adds -er!)<br/>*Hallo Thomas,* |
| **Pronouns** | **Sie**, **Ihnen**, **Ihr** (Always capitalized!) | **du**, **dir**, **dein** |
| **Sign-off** | *Mit freundlichen Grüßen* | *Viele Grüße* / *Liebe Grüße* / *Bis bald* |

## 2. ⚠️ The Unique German Email Comma Rule

In English, you write:
> *"Dear Mr. Smith,*  
> ***I** am writing to you today..."* (Capital "I" on the next line).

**In German, after the greeting comma, the sentence continues on the next line with a LOWERCASE letter:**

```text
Sehr geehrte Frau Müller,
ich schreibe Ihnen heute, weil...  (Notice: lowercase "ich"!)

Lieber Max,
vielen Dank für deine Einladung...  (Notice: lowercase "vielen"!)
```

*Exception:* If the first word on the next line is a noun (which is always capitalized in German) or the formal pronoun *Sie*, it remains capitalized.

### No Comma After the Sign-off!
In English, you put a comma after your closing (*"Best regards,"*). 
**In German, there is NO COMMA after the sign-off:**
* ✅ *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*  
  *Jithin Joseph*

## 3. Standard Everyday Email Starters & Closings

### Starters:
* *„Ich schreibe Ihnen, weil...“* (I am writing to you because...)
* *„Vielen Dank für Ihre E-Mail.“* (Thank you for your email.)
* *„Ich habe eine Frage zu...“* (I have a question regarding...)

### Closings:
* *„Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort.“* (I look forward to your reply — formal).
* *„Können Sie mir bitte Bescheid geben?“* (Could you please let me know?)
* *„Schreib mir bald!“* (Write to me soon! — informal).
