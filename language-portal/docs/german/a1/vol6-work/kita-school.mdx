# 6.5 Childcare & School Basics (Kita und Schule)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can talk about daycare (**die Kita**) and primary school (**die Grundschule**).
* I know how to say I am bringing (**bringen**) or picking up (**abholen**) my child.
* I can call the daycare or school to report my child sick.
* I understand parent-teacher communications like **der Elternabend** (parents' evening).

---

## 1. Childcare in Germany: The Kita System

For families with young children in Germany, **die Kita** (*Kindertagesstätte*) is central to everyday life:
* **die Krippe:** Nursery for babies and toddlers (ages 0 to 3).
* **der Kindergarten:** Daycare for children (ages 3 to 6).
* **die Kita:** The universal umbrella word for both.
* **die Grundschule:** Primary school (starts at age 6, grades 1 to 4).
* **die Erzieherin / der Erzieher:** Daycare teacher / educator.

---

## 2. The Two Daily Actions: "bringen" & "abholen"

Every morning and afternoon, parents use these two verbs:

* **bringen** (to bring / drop off):
  * *„Ich **bringe** meine Tochter morgens um 08:00 Uhr in die Kita.“*
* **abholen** (to pick up — separable verb!):
  * *„Wer **holt** das Kind heute **ab**?“* (Who is picking up the child today?)
  * *„Ich **hole** meinen Sohn um 15:30 Uhr **ab**.“* (I am picking up my son at 3:30 PM).

---

## 3. Reporting a Child Sick (Krankmeldung in der Kita)

If your child has a fever or cough, you must call the daycare early:

> *„Guten Morgen, hier ist Jithin Joseph, der Vater von Maya. Maya hat leider Fieber und kann heute nicht in die Kita kommen.“*  
> *(Good morning, this is Jithin Joseph, Maya's father. Maya unfortunately has a fever and cannot come to daycare today.)*

### Other Everyday Kita Topics:
* **die Brotzeit / das Vesper:** Morning snack brought from home in a lunchbox (*die Brotdose*).
* **die Gummistiefel:** Rubber rain boots (a permanent requirement in German Kitas!).
* **der Elternabend:** The evening meeting where parents and teachers discuss group activities.
