# 6.3 Work Schedules & Vacation (Arbeitszeit und Urlaub)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can describe my work hours and work models (**Vollzeit**, **Teilzeit**, **Gleitzeit**).
* I know the difference between vacation (**der Urlaub**) and school holidays (**die Ferien**).
* I can request time off or talk about my vacation plans.
* I know how to report sick to my employer (**die Krankmeldung**).

---

## 1. Working Hours & Work Models

In German employment contracts, you will see these terms describing your hours:

* **Vollzeit:** Full-time (typically 38–40 hours per week).
  * *„Ich arbeite in **Vollzeit**.“*
* **Teilzeit:** Part-time (e.g. 20–30 hours per week).
  * *„Sie arbeitet in **Teilzeit**.“*
* **die Gleitzeit:** Flexible working hours (you choose when to start and finish within a core window).
  * *„Wir haben **Gleitzeit** im Büro.“*
* **die Schichtarbeit:** Shift work (e.g. nurses, factory workers, drivers).
  * *„Er macht **Frühschicht** und **Spätschicht**.“*
* **die Überstunden:** Overtime hours.
  * *„Ich habe diese Woche viele **Überstunden** gemacht.“*

---

## 2. 🌴 "Urlaub" vs. "Ferien" (A Classic Beginner Trap)

In English, people use "vacation" or "holiday" for both adults and school children. 
In German, there is a strict legal and cultural divide:

* **der Urlaub:** Paid annual leave for **working adults / employees**:
  * *„Ich habe nächste Woche **Urlaub**.“* (I am on vacation next week.)
  * *„Ich fahre in den **Urlaub** nach Italien.“* (I'm going on holiday to Italy.)
* **die Ferien (always plural!):** School and university vacations for **students**:
  * *„Die Kinder haben **Sommerferien**.“* (The kids have summer holidays.)
  * ❌ *Ich habe heute Ferien vom Büro.* (Unnatural! Adults have *Urlaub*, not *Ferien*).

---

## 3. 🤒 The German Sick Leave Protocol (Die Krankmeldung)

If you wake up with a fever or flu in Germany, you must follow strict notification rules:

1. **Notify your manager early in the morning** (before your shift starts) by phone or email:
   > *„Guten Morgen, ich bin leider krank und kann heute nicht zur Arbeit kommen.“*
2. **The Sick Note (Die AU-Bescheinigung):**
   * Traditionally called *der gelbe Schein* (the yellow slip), now called the **eAU** (electronic certificate). 
   * If you are sick for more than 3 days (or on day 1 depending on company rules), you must visit a doctor (*Hausarzt*) to obtain an official medical certificate.
