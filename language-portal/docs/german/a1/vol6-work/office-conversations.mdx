# 6.2 Office Conversations (Im Büro)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can ask a colleague for a moment of their time or for help (*Hast du kurz Zeit?*).
* I can talk about everyday office equipment (*der Drucker, der Computer, das Meeting*).
* I understand the unique German lunchtime greeting: **„Mahlzeit!“**.
* I can invite a colleague for coffee or lunch in the company canteen (*Kantine*).

---

## 1. Asking a Colleague for Time & Help

In a busy office, you don't just interrupt someone who is typing. Use these polite entry phrases:

* **„Hast du kurz Zeit?“** (Do you have a moment? — informal with a teammate).
* **„Haben Sie kurz Zeit, Herr Meyer?“** (Do you have a moment? — formal with a senior colleague).
* **„Ich habe eine kurze Frage.“** (I have a quick question).
* **„Kannst du mir kurz helfen?“** (Can you help me for a second?)

### How to reply if you are busy:
* *„Ja, gerne! Worum geht es?“* (Yes, glad to! What is it about?)
* *„Im Moment leider nicht, ich bin in einem Meeting. Geht es in 20 Minuten?“* (Not right now, I'm in a meeting. Does in 20 minutes work?)

## 2. Office Equipment & Everyday Hiccups

| German Item | Article & Plural | Everyday Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **der Computer / Laptop** | die Computer | *Mein Laptop startet nicht.* (My laptop won't start). |
| **der Drucker** | die Drucker | *Der Drucker hat kein Papier.* (The printer has no paper). |
| **das Dokument** | die Dokumente | *Ich drucke das Dokument aus.* (I'll print the document). |
| **das Meeting / die Besprechung**| die Meetings / Besprechungen | *Wir haben um 14 Uhr ein Meeting.* |
| **die E-Mail** | die E-Mails | *Ich schicke dir die E-Mail sofort.* |
| **der Bildschirm** | die Bildschirme | *Mein Bildschirm ist schwarz.* (My monitor is black). |

---

## 3. 🍲 The German Workplace Institution: "Mahlzeit!"

Between 11:30 and 13:30 in almost every German workplace, you will pass colleagues in the hallway and they will look at you, nod, and say:
> **„Mahlzeit!“**

* **What it means:** Literally "mealtime". It is an all-in-one greeting that means: *"Hello, enjoy your lunch break!"*
* **How to reply:** Simply say **„Mahlzeit!“** back with a smile!
* *Inviting someone:*  
  * *„Kommst du mit in die Kantine?“* (Are you coming along to the canteen?)
  * *„Gehen wir zusammen Mittagessen?“* (Shall we go have lunch together?)
