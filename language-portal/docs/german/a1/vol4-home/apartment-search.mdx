# 4.5 Apartment Search & Real Estate Ads (Die Wohnungssuche)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can read and decode German real estate listings and abbreviations (*KM, WM, NK, EBK*).
* I understand the crucial difference between **Kaltmiete** and **Warmmiete**.
* I know what the security deposit (**die Kaution**) is.
* I can write a polite, professional request for an apartment viewing (**die Besichtigung**).

---

## 1. The Cost of Renting: "Kalt" vs. "Warm"

In Germany, rent is never quoted as a single simple price. You must understand how rent is calculated to avoid budgeting surprises:

```text
  Kaltmiete (Base Rent)          ➔  The cost of the empty apartment space alone
+ Nebenkosten (Operating costs)  ➔  Heating, water, trash disposal, building maintenance
========================================================================================
= Warmmiete (Total Monthly Rent) ➔  What you actually transfer to the landlord each month
```

### The Caution on Electricity & Internet:
⚠️ **Crucial Expat Detail:** *Warmmiete* includes heating and water, but in Germany it almost **never includes private household electricity (*Strom*) or internet**! You must register for your own electricity contract separately with a provider.

* **die Kaution:** The security deposit (by law, maximum 3 months of *Kaltmiete*). Held in a blocked account until you move out.

## 2. Decoding German Apartment Abbreviations

Rental portals (like ImmoScout24 or WG-Gesucht) use a dense shorthand of abbreviations to save space:

| Abbreviation | Full German Word | English Meaning | Example in Ad |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Whg.** | die Wohnung | apartment | *Schöne 3-Zi.-Whg.* |
| **Zi.** | das Zimmer | room | *2-Zi.-Whg. (Living room + 1 bedroom)* |
| **qm / m²** | der Quadratmeter | square meters | *ca. 65 qm* |
| **KM** | die Kaltmiete | cold/base rent | *750 € KM* |
| **NK** | die Nebenkosten | utility/service costs | *+ 200 € NK* |
| **WM** | die Warmmiete | warm/total rent | *950 € WM* |
| **KT** | die Kaution | security deposit | *3 KM KT (3 months cold rent)* |
| **EBK** | die Einbauküche | fitted kitchen | *mit moderner EBK* |
| **Balk.** | der Balkon | balcony | *Südbalkon (South-facing)* |
| **EG** | das Erdgeschoss | ground floor | *im EG* |
| **OG** | das Obergeschoss | upper floor | *im 2. OG* |
| **ab sof.** | ab sofort | available immediately | *frei ab sof.* |
| **WBS** | Wohnberechtigungsschein | subsidized housing certificate | *WBS erforderlich (Only with permit!)* |

## 3. Reading a Real Listing: An Example

Look at this typical rental advertisement:

> **Helle 2,5-Zi.-Whg. in Frankfurt-Bornheim**  
> *62 qm, 3. OG mit Balkon & EBK.  
> Kaltmiete: 850 € | NK: 220 € | Warmmiete: 1.070 € | Kaution: 3 KM.  
> Frei ab: 01. Juli. Nichtraucher erwünscht. Keine Haustiere.*

### What does this tell you?
* It has approximately 62 square meters on the 3rd floor.
* It has a living room, bedroom, half-room (study/nook), a balcony, and a fitted kitchen.
* Your monthly bank transfer is 1.070 € (*Warmmiete*).
* The deposit is 3 × 850 € = 2.550 €.
* Smokers and pets are not wanted.

## 4. Writing an Apartment Viewing Request (Die Besichtigungsanfrage)

Demand for apartments in German cities is very high. A message like *"Is this available?"* will be ignored. Use this structured, professional German template:

> **Betreff:** Anfrage zur Wohnungsbesichtigung — 2-Zimmer-Wohnung in Bornheim
>
> *Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,*
>
> *mein Name ist Jithin Joseph. Ich interessiere mich sehr für Ihre Wohnung in Frankfurt-Bornheim. Ich arbeite als Software-Ingenieur in Festanstellung (ungekündigt).*
>
> *Wäre ein Besichtigungstermin in dieser Woche möglich? Gerne sende ich Ihnen vorab meine Unterlagen (Gehaltsnachweise, SCHUFA-Auskunft).*
>
> *Mit freundlichen Grüßen*  
> *Jithin Joseph*  
> *Tel: 0176-12345678*
