# 4.1 Apartment & Rooms (Wohnung und Zimmer)

## 🎯 What you will learn
* I can name the different types of housing in Germany (*die Wohnung, das Haus, die WG*).
* I understand how Germans count rooms (e.g. what a *3-Zimmer-Wohnung* actually means).
* I can name all the rooms of an apartment (*Küche, Bad, Wohnzimmer, Schlafzimmer, Flur, Balkon*).
* I can describe an apartment using everyday adjectives (*hell, ruhig, groß, gemütlich*).

---

## 1. Types of Housing in Germany

When finding a place to live in Germany, you will encounter three primary types of housing:
* **die Wohnung** (die Wohnungen) — *apartment / flat* (The most common form of living in German cities).
* **das Haus** (die Häuser) — *house* (Usually single-family or semi-detached in suburbs).
* **die WG (Wohngemeinschaft)** — *flatshare / shared apartment* (Very popular among students and young professionals; you have your own private bedroom and share the kitchen and bathroom).

---

## 2. ⚠️ The Big German Real Estate Trap: Counting Rooms

In English-speaking countries, apartments are categorized by the number of bedrooms: *"a 2-bedroom apartment"*.
**In Germany, ALL living rooms and bedrooms are counted together as "Zimmer":**

```text
A "3-Zimmer-Wohnung" does NOT mean 3 bedrooms!
It almost always means:
• 1 Wohnzimmer (Living room)
• 2 Schlafzimmer (Bedrooms)
• + Küche, Bad, Flur (Kitchen, Bathroom, Hallway are NOT counted in the number!)
```

So if you are looking for an apartment with two bedrooms, you must search for a **3-Zimmer-Wohnung**!

---

## 3. The Rooms of an Apartment (Die Räume)

Notice the genders of the rooms: words ending in **-zimmer** are ALWAYS neutral (**das**):

| Room | Article & Plural | English Meaning | What is in it? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **das Wohnzimmer** | die Wohnzimmer | living room | Sofa, Fernseher, Tisch |
| **das Schlafzimmer** | die Schlafzimmer | bedroom | Bett, Kleiderschrank |
| **das Kinderzimmer** | die Kinderzimmer | children's room | Schreibtisch, Spielzeug |
| **das Arbeitszimmer** | die Arbeitszimmer | home office / study | Computer, Schreibtisch |
| **die Küche** | die Küchen | kitchen | Herd, Kühlschrank, Spüle |
| **das Bad / Badezimmer** | die Bäder | bathroom | Dusche, Badewanne, Waschbecken |
| **der Flur** | die Flure | hallway / corridor | Garderobe, Schuhe |
| **der Balkon** | die Balkone | balcony | Pflanzen, Stühle |
| **der Keller** | die Keller | basement / storage unit| Waschmaschine, Kisten |

## 4. Describing Your Home with Adjectives

To describe whether an apartment is pleasant to live in, German uses contrasting pairs of adjectives:

* **groß** (big)  ⟷  **klein** (small)
  * *Die Wohnung hat 80 Quadratmeter (qm). Sie ist sehr groß.*
* **hell** (bright with lots of sunlight)  ⟷  **dunkel** (dark)
  * *Das Wohnzimmer hat große Fenster und ist wunderbar hell.*
* **ruhig** (quiet)  ⟷  **laut** (noisy)
  * *Die Wohnung liegt im Hinterhof und ist sehr ruhig.* (In the courtyard and very quiet).
* **gemütlich** (cozy / comfortable)
  * *Unsere Küche ist klein, aber sehr gemütlich.*
* **teuer** (expensive)  ⟷  **günstig / billig** (affordable / cheap)

---

## 5. Real-Life Germany: The "Hinterhof" & "Erdgeschoss"

When looking at German residential buildings:
* **das Vorderhaus:** The building facing the main street. Can be noisier, but often has better light.
* **der Hinterhof:** The interior courtyard behind the building. Usually much quieter (*ruhig*).
* **das Erdgeschoss (EG):** The ground floor.
* **das 1. Obergeschoss (1. OG):** The 1st floor above ground (what Americans call the 2nd floor).
* **das Dachgeschoss (DG):** The top attic floor with slanted walls (*Dachschrägen*).
