# F. Listening Practice (Hören)

## The listening loop

Listening improves fastest when you **try before you read**:

```text
1. Read the question.                         (What do I listen for? A time? A place?)
2. Listen to spoken German WITHOUT the text.   (Once or twice – like the exam part you are training.)
3. Answer.
4. Check the answer.
5. Now read the transcript.                   (Where exactly was the answer? What confused you?)
6. Take 2–3 useful phrases into your Vocabulary Vault.
7. Listen again and repeat one key sentence aloud (shadowing).
```

## What A1 listening is about

Real life and the Goethe A1 *Hören* part use the same kinds of texts:

| Text type | Where you meet it | In Goethe A1 Hören |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Short everyday conversations (shop, café, office) | daily | Teil 1 – heard **twice** |
| Announcements (station, airport, shop, public building) | trains, platforms | Teil 2 – heard **once** |
| Phone / voicemail messages | doctor's practice, Kita, friends | Teil 3 – heard **twice** |

What you mostly need to catch: **numbers, prices, times, days, places, and what someone wants you to do**.

## Five listening strategies

1. **Wait for the final answer.** People change plans: *„Um zwei? – Nein, um zwei kann ich nicht. Um halb vier?“* The answer is *halb vier* (3:30).
2. **halb looks forward:** *halb vier* = 3:30, not 4:30. → [1.5](../a1/vol1-foundations/numbers-dates-time.mdx)
3. **Similar numbers:** *vierzehn/vierzig, fünfzehn/fünfzig, dreizehn/dreißig* – the ending *-zehn* vs *-zig* decides.
4. **Listen for the prefix at the end:** *Der Zug fährt um 9 Uhr **ab**.* / *kommt um 9 Uhr **an**.* → [3.12](../a1/vol3-grammar/separable-verbs.mdx)
5. **Negation changes everything:** *Der Kurs fällt heute **aus*** = the course is cancelled today.

## Practice (use spoken German)

Ask a partner to read each script aloud, or use **voice practice** with the AI. Keep the transcript hidden until after you answer. A transcript shown in text chat is a reading exercise, not a listening exercise.

**Voicemail.** Question: *Wann soll Frau Novak kommen?* a) Montag 9 Uhr b) Dienstag 9 Uhr c) Dienstag 11 Uhr
<details>
  <summary>Show transcript and answer</summary>

  *„Guten Tag, Frau Novak, hier ist die Praxis Dr. Berg. Ihr Termin am Montag um 9 Uhr geht leider nicht. Können Sie am Dienstag kommen? Nicht um 9, sondern um 11 Uhr. Bitte rufen Sie uns zurück. Danke!“*

  **c) Dienstag 11 Uhr.** Monday was cancelled, and 9 o'clock was corrected (*nicht um 9, sondern um 11*).
</details>

**Announcement (once only!).** Richtig oder falsch: *Der ICE nach Hamburg fährt heute von Gleis 7.*
<details>
  <summary>Show transcript and answer</summary>

  *„Achtung an Gleis 5: Der ICE nach Hamburg, Abfahrt 14:22 Uhr, fährt heute von Gleis 7.“*

  **Richtig** – the train is announced at platform 5 but leaves from platform 7 today.
</details>
