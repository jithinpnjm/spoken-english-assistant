# H. Speaking Practice (Sprechen)

## The speaking loop

```text
Teacher/AI asks ONE question in German  →  you answer aloud
→  correction in English + the corrected German sentence
→  YOU REPEAT the corrected sentence  →  only then the next question
```

The repeat is not optional. Saying the correct version once – out loud – is what replaces the wrong version in your
memory. The AI practice boxes in every chapter follow this loop.

## Self-practice routines (no partner needed)

- **Shadowing (5 min):** play a short recording, pause after each sentence, repeat it with the same melody.
- **Record yourself (5 min):** answer three questions from the cards below on your phone, listen back, fix one thing, record again.
- **Talk through your day:** describe what you are doing in simple sentences (*Jetzt koche ich. Dann rufe ich meine Mutter an.*).
- **Mistake log:** after each session, write down one sentence you got wrong and its correct form. See [Mistake Log & Repair](./progress-dashboard.mdx).

## Goethe A1 practice cards (original)

The A1 speaking exam is a group exam with up to four candidates and three parts (details:
[10.4 Goethe A1 Sprechen](../a1/exam-center/goethe-sprechen.mdx)).

### Teil 1 – Introduce yourself
Keywords: **Name · Alter · Land · Wohnort · Sprachen · Beruf · Hobby**
Then practise the follow-ups: *Können Sie Ihren Namen buchstabieren?* · *Wie ist Ihre Telefonnummer / Postleitzahl?*

### Teil 2 – Ask and answer on a theme
Pick a card, ask a partner (or the AI) a question, then answer theirs.

| Thema: Essen und Trinken | Thema: Wochenende | Thema: Einkaufen | Thema: Wohnen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Frühstück | Sport | Supermarkt | Zimmer |
| Obst | Freunde | Preis | Balkon |
| Kaffee | Kino | Brot | Miete |
| Restaurant | Samstag | Kleidung | Nachbarn |

*Example:* Card *Frühstück* → *„Was essen Sie zum Frühstück?“* – *„Ich esse Brot mit Käse und trinke Kaffee.“*

### Teil 3 – Make a request and react
Make a polite request from each "card", then react to your partner's request.

| Card | Request | Reaction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🥛 a glass of water | *Ein Glas Wasser, bitte!* / *Kann ich bitte ein Glas Wasser haben?* | *Ja, natürlich. Hier, bitte.* |
| 🪟 open window | *Können Sie bitte das Fenster zumachen?* | *Ja, gern.* |
| ✏️ pen | *Haben Sie einen Kuli für mich?* | *Tut mir leid, ich habe keinen.* |
| 📱 phone | *Darf ich kurz Ihr Handy benutzen?* | *Ja, klar.* |
| 🕐 clock | *Wie spät ist es, bitte?* | *Es ist halb drei.* |

## What changes at A2 and B1

- **A2:** exchange personal information with cards, talk about your own life for a longer turn, and **plan something together** with a partner.
- **B1:** plan together (≈3 min), present a topic with slides (≈3 min), discuss your topic and your partner's.
  Useful phrases: [Redemittel](./redemittel-phrases.mdx).
