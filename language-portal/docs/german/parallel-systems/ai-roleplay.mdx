# I. AI Roleplay Lab

## What the AI teacher is (and isn't)

The AI in the practice boxes is **a teacher playing a role**, not a chat partner. It plays the baker, the receptionist
or your colleague – and at the same time it watches your German and corrects it.

Every roleplay has:
1. **One learning goal** (e.g. "use *können* + infinitive at the end").
2. **Short German turns** (1–2 sentences at A1) and **explanations in English**.
3. **The correction loop** after each of your turns:

```text
Result:             Correct / Almost correct / Needs correction
Corrected German:   …
Why:                one short sentence in English
Now repeat:         …          ← you say or type it again before the roleplay continues
```

## How to get the most out of it

- Answer in German even if it's only three words. Short and correct beats long and English.
- Use repair phrases when you're lost: *Wie bitte? Noch einmal, bitte. Was bedeutet …?*
- Always do the repeat step.
- At the end, ask: *"What were my three most frequent mistakes?"* and put them in your [Mistake Log](./progress-dashboard.mdx).

## Safety rules the AI follows

- **Doctor, pharmacy, health:** the AI practises *communication* – describing symptoms, answering questions,
  understanding instructions. It will **not** diagnose you, choose a medicine or tell you a dose. For real health
  questions, ask a doctor or pharmacist; the non-emergency medical service in Germany is **116 117**, emergencies **112**.
- **Offices, contracts, official letters:** the AI teaches the *language* of the process (Termin, Formular, Frist,
  Aktenzeichen). Fees, deadlines and rules change and depend on your case – check your letter or ask the office.
- **Exams:** mock tasks are practice. The AI does not predict your exam result.
