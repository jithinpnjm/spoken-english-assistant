# K. Mistake Log & Repair (Fehler reparieren)

Most learners don't make hundreds of different mistakes – they make **the same few mistakes hundreds of times**.
Find yours, and repair them one at a time.

## 1. Keep a mistake log

| Date | What I said/wrote | Correct | Type | Repaired? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12.05. | *Ich habe ein Termin.* | *Ich habe einen Termin.* | article + accusative | ☐ ☐ ☐ |
| 13.05. | *Heute ich arbeite.* | *Heute arbeite ich.* | word order (V2) | ☐ ☐ ☐ |

Tick "repaired" once each time you produce it correctly **without help** on a different day. Three ticks = repaired.

**Mistake types:** article/gender · case (nominative/accusative/dative) · verb ending · tense · modal verb ·
word order · connector (*weil/denn/deshalb*) · preposition · spelling · capital letters · du/Sie · pronunciation ·
vocabulary choice · **task completion** (a missing point in a message – the most expensive writing error in exams).

## 2. The repair routine

```text
1. Understand: read the rule in English (link below).
2. See the pattern: copy two correct model sentences.
3. Drill: 5–10 quick sentences of the same kind (below, or ask the AI).
4. Produce: say/write the sentence from your log correctly – out loud.
5. Retest later: check it again in 2–3 days and in a week.
```

**Wrong-first technique:** when you revise, look only at the *wrong* sentence first and find the error yourself before you
look at the correction. Finding the error is what trains your "inner corrector" for speaking.

### After every chapter: a 5-point mastery check and a 4-step revision plan

| Mastery check (tick all five) | Revision plan |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. I can explain the rule in simple English. | **Same day:** read each model sentence, cover it, say it from memory. |
| 2. I can say three model sentences aloud. | **Next day:** rebuild one sentence with a new noun, place or time. |
| 3. I can use the key vocabulary without looking. | **After 3 days:** look at the chapter's "common mistake" first and explain the fix. |
| 4. I can correct the chapter's common mistake. | **After 7 days:** do the chapter's AI practice again without reading the lesson. |
| 5. I can say one new sentence of my own. | |

**Higher levels:** A2 repair drills → [A2 Error Analysis](../a2/vol10-exam/error-analysis.mdx) · B1 → [B1 Repair Bank](../b1/exam/b1-repair-bank.mdx).

## 3. The ten most common A1 mistakes – and their repair drills

**1. *ein* instead of *einen* after haben/brauchen/möchten** → [3.9](../a1/vol3-grammar/accusative.mdx)
Drill: *Ich brauche ___ Termin / ___ Stift / ___ Kaffee / ___ Arzt.* → always *einen* (all masculine).
*Ich brauche ___ Fahrkarte / ___ Ticket.* → *eine / ein* (only masculine changes).

**2. Verb not in position 2 after a time word** → [3.7](../a1/vol3-grammar/sentence-structure-v2.mdx)
Drill: start each with the time word: *Heute (ich / arbeiten)* → *Heute arbeite ich.* · *Morgen (wir / fahren) nach Köln.* · *Am Montag (ich / haben) einen Termin.*

**3. Second verb not at the end** → [3.6](../a1/vol3-grammar/modal-verbs.mdx)
Drill: *(kann / ich / nicht / heute / kommen)* → *Ich kann heute nicht kommen.* · *(muss / ich / den Arzt / anrufen)* → *Ich muss den Arzt anrufen.*

**4. *nicht* vs *kein*** → [3.11](../a1/vol3-grammar/negation.mdx)
Drill: *Ich habe ___ Zeit. · Ich komme ___. · Das ist ___ mein Schlüssel. · Wir haben ___ Kinder.* → keine · nicht · nicht · keine

**5. Wrong verb ending / missing vowel change** → [3.4](../a1/vol3-grammar/present-tense-verbs.mdx)
Drill: *er (sprechen) · du (nehmen) · sie (fahren) · du (heißen)* → spricht · nimmst · fährt · heißt

**6. du/Sie mix-up** → [1.4](../a1/vol1-foundations/personal-pronouns.mdx)
Drill: ask the same question to a colleague you say *du* to and to the receptionist: *Hast du …? / Haben Sie …?*

**7. Separable prefix forgotten or in the wrong place** → [3.12](../a1/vol3-grammar/separable-verbs.mdx)
Drill: *anrufen: Ich ___ dich morgen ___.* · *einkaufen: Wir ___ am Samstag ___.* · with a modal: *Ich muss heute ___.* (einkaufen)

**8. *sein* for hunger, fever, pain** → [3.5](../a1/vol3-grammar/sein-and-haben.mdx)
Drill: *I'm hungry / I have a fever / I'm cold* → *Ich habe Hunger. · Ich habe Fieber. · Mir ist kalt.*

**9. Time prepositions um/am/im** → [1.5](../a1/vol1-foundations/numbers-dates-time.mdx)
Drill: *___ Montag · ___ 10 Uhr · ___ Juli · ___ Wochenende · ___ Sommer* → am · um · im · am · im

**10. Lowercase nouns / capital letters in messages** → [3.1](../a1/vol3-grammar/nouns-gender.mdx), [6.8](../a1/vol6-work/email-messages.mdx)
Drill: rewrite correctly: *ich habe morgen einen termin beim arzt.* → *Ich habe morgen einen Termin beim Arzt.*

## 4. Weekly review (15 minutes, e.g. Sunday)

1. Count your log entries by type. Which type appears most?
2. Choose **one** type for next week. Do its repair drill three times during the week.
3. Move repaired sentences into your [flashcards](./flashcards.mdx) for long-term review.
4. Check the [Recommended Learning Path](../course-guide/learning-path.mdx) – what's your next chapter?
