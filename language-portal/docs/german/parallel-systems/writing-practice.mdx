# G. Writing Practice (Schreiben)

## The loop: write → review → rewrite

Writing only improves if you **rewrite** after feedback. Every writing task in this course follows:

```text
Prompt → you write → review (task points first!) → corrected version → mistake table
       → a more natural version → YOU REWRITE → short check → next task
```

Keep your rewritten texts in one notebook. After a month, read the first and the last – you will see the difference.

## A1 review checklist (use it on your own texts)

1. **Task points:** did I answer every point? (In Goethe A1 Schreiben Teil 2, a missing point costs more than small grammar errors.)
2. **Understandable?** Could a German reader act on this message?
3. **Greeting and closing** fit the person (du/Sie)?
4. **Verb position:** verb in position 2; second verb (modal/Perfekt/separable prefix) at the end.
5. **Articles/case:** *einen Termin*, *mit dem Bus*, *zur Arbeit*.
6. **Capitals:** all nouns, *Sie/Ihnen*, first word after a full stop.
7. **Length:** about 30 words for a short message.

## What changes at A2 and B1

| Level | Typical tasks | Extra review points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A2 | 20–30-word personal message and 30–40-word semi-official message (apologise, give a reason, suggest a new time, ask for information, explain a problem) | *weil/dass* clauses (verb at the end), Perfekt, du/Sie register, logical order |
| B1 | personal email ≈80 words, opinion/forum post ≈80 words, semi-formal email ≈40 words | text structure (opening, order of points, closing), connectors (*deshalb, trotzdem, außerdem*), opinion phrases, polite requests (*Könnten Sie …?*) |

## Task bank (A1, real life)

Write each in about 30 words, then check with the list above.

1. **Kita:** Your daughter is ill today. Say why she isn't coming, how long (probably), and ask if you need to bring anything tomorrow.
2. **Language course:** You can't come on Thursday. Give a reason and ask for the homework.
3. **Doctor's practice:** Cancel your appointment on Monday at 9:00 and ask for a new one next week.
4. **Friend:** Accept an invitation to a birthday party. Ask what you can bring and what time it starts.
5. **Colleague:** You'll be 20 minutes late for a meeting. Say why and suggest a new time.
6. **Hausmeister:** The heating in your flat doesn't work. Since when? When can he come?
7. **Language school:** Ask about an evening A1 course: days, start date, price.
8. **Neighbour:** Ask if they can accept a parcel for you tomorrow.
9. **Friend:** Decline a weekend trip. Give a reason and suggest another weekend.
10. **Landlord:** You saw an ad for a flat. Ask if it is still free, when you can see it, and how high the rent is.
