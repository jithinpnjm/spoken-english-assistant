# J. Flashcards & Spaced Repetition

## A good German flashcard

**Front (what you must produce):** English meaning + a situation
> *appointment* — "at the doctor's reception"

**Back (what you check):**
> **der Termin, die Termine**  
> *Ich brauche einen Termin.* (haben/brauchen + **einen**)  
> ⚠️ not *ein Termin* after *haben*

Why this format works:
- You **produce** German (front → back), which is harder and more useful than recognising it.
- The article and plural are part of the answer, not an extra.
- The example sentence gives you a chunk you can say tomorrow.

**Colour trick:** write masculine nouns in blue, feminine in red, neuter in green. Many learners find they start to
"see" the colour – and the article – when they think of the word.

## Card types (mix them)

| Type | Front | Back |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Article | *Wohnung* | *die Wohnung, die Wohnungen* |
| Plural | *der Bahnhof → ?* | *die Bahnhöfe* |
| English → German | *to pick up (a child)* | *abholen – Ich hole Maya um 15 Uhr ab.* |
| Pattern | *need + appointment* | *Ich brauche einen Termin.* |
| Cloze | *Ich fahre ___ Bus.* | *mit dem Bus* |
| Phrase | *Can you repeat that?* | *Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?* |

## A spaced-repetition schedule you can keep

Use an app with spaced repetition (e.g. Anki) **or** a simple paper box with five compartments:

```text
Box 1: every day        → correct? move to Box 2. Wrong? stays in Box 1.
Box 2: every 2–3 days   → correct → Box 3, wrong → back to Box 1
Box 3: once a week      → correct → Box 4, wrong → Box 1
Box 4: every 2 weeks    → correct → Box 5, wrong → Box 1
Box 5: once a month     → correct → "known" (keep a few for review)
```

**Budget:** 10–15 minutes a day, max. 10 new cards a day. Consistency beats long sessions.

## What to put in your deck first

1. The 30 [Survival Kit](../a1/vol1-foundations/survival-kit.mdx) sentences (as phrase cards).
2. The survival words from the [Vocabulary Vault](./vocabulary-vault.mdx).
3. Every sentence you got wrong more than once (from your [Mistake Log](./progress-dashboard.mdx)).
4. The irregular forms you need: *sein, haben*, the vowel-change verbs (*nimmt, fährt, spricht*), common participles (*gegessen, gegangen*).

## Review views that help

- **By situation:** doctor · office · shopping · transport · Kita · work
- **By gender:** only *der* words, only *die* words … (great for article drills)
- **Mistakes first:** cards you got wrong this week
- **Exam words:** numbers, times, dates, prices, days, months – the official Goethe A1 word groups
