# 10.1 Goethe-Zertifikat A2: Overview

:::info
The A2 course is still being written. This overview is complete and verified against the official Goethe A2
Modellsatz (adults); the detailed A2 exam chapters will follow. Always check the current Modellsatz on goethe.de
before your exam.
:::

## Structure

| Module | Time | Parts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lesen** | 30 min | Teil 1: a newspaper/internet text, multiple choice (5) · Teil 2: information boards, programmes etc., multiple choice (5) · Teil 3: correspondence (email/letter), multiple choice (5) · Teil 4: match people's needs to ads (5) |
| **Hören** | 30 min | Teil 1: radio, answering machine, announcements – multiple choice, **heard twice** (5) · Teil 2: one connected conversation, match pictures/text – **heard once** (5) · Teil 3: five short conversations, multiple choice – **heard once** (5) · Teil 4: radio interview, Richtig/Falsch – **heard twice** (5) |
| **Schreiben** | 30 min | Teil 1: personal message, **20–30 words**, three points · Teil 2: semi-official message, **30–40 words**, three points |
| **Sprechen** | ≈15 min per pair | Teil 1: exchange personal information with your partner using cards · Teil 2: talk to the examiner in more detail about your own life · Teil 3: **plan something together** with your partner and agree |

Dictionaries and mobile phones are not allowed.

## Scoring and passing

- Maximum **100 points**; each module is worth **25**. (Lesen, Hören and Schreiben: 20 raw points × 1.25.)
- You pass with **at least 60 points**, **and** at least **45 of 75** in the written modules, **and** at least **15 of 25** in Sprechen. If either minimum is missed, the whole exam is not passed.

## How A2 differs from A1

| | A1 | A2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Writing | form + ca. 30-word message | two messages (20–30 and 30–40 words) |
| Speaking | introduce yourself, questions on a theme, requests | personal information, talk about your life, **plan together** |
| Listening | short texts, mostly everyday details | longer conversations, radio, an interview |
| Grammar you need | present tense, modal verbs, accusative | + Perfekt, dative, *weil/dass/wenn*, comparatives |
