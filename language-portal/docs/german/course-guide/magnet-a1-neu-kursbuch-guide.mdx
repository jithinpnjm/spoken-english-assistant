## How *Magnet neu A1 Kursbuch* Is Organized (Beginner Guide)

Unlike a pure exercise workbook, *Magnet neu A1 Kursbuch* is a **story- and dialogue-driven German coursebook**. Every unit (*Lektion*) follows a predictable, beginner-friendly structure:

1. **Sub-lessons A, B, C:** Dialogues, photo stories, reading texts, listening tasks, and speaking/writing activities following teenagers and families in **Nürnberg and Erlangen** (*Oliver, Steffi, Markus, Melanie, Tobias,* and *Klasse 7b*). Each sub-lesson introduces one clear communicative goal and one core grammar building block in context.
2. **Phonetik:** Focused pronunciation drills (such as the German *h*, *Ich-Laut*, glottal stop *Vokalneueinsatz*, umlauts *ö/ü*, W-question intonation, and long vs. short vowels) integrated into the units.
3. **Landeskunde:** Cultural pages with real photos, maps, and short texts about everyday life in **Germany, Austria, and Switzerland (D-A-CH)**—covering German-speaking countries, youth clubs, school life, street food (*Wurst im Brötchen*), slow food vs. fast food, and the Cologne Marathon.
4. **Grammatik auf einen Blick:** A one-page visual grammar summary at the end of every unit that puts all verb tables, article charts, and sentence-order boxes in one place for fast review.
5. **Wortschatz: Das ist neu!:** A complete unit vocabulary list at the end of every unit, organized by sub-lesson (`A`, `B`, `C`) with **definite articles (*der, die, das*), plural forms, and real example sentences**.
6. **Zwischenstation 1–5:** Exam-preparation checkpoints after every two units (`Lektion 2, 4, 6, 8, 10`) that systematically train the four official A1 skills—**Lesen (Reading), Hören (Listening), Schreiben (Writing), and Sprechen (Speaking)**—plus review games and project pages.
