# Recommended Learning Path (A0 → A1)

The **Complete German A1 Book (Chapters 0–17)** is arranged in the exact order a beginner should study. Every chapter only uses grammar and vocabulary that have been explained from zero in that chapter or earlier chapters.

:::tip How to use this page
Work from top to bottom. After each chapter, complete its Guided Practice and AI Practice, and note any repeated errors in your [Mistake Log & Repair](../parallel-systems/progress-dashboard.mdx).
:::

## Stage 0 – First-Week Survival German (Optional A0 Starter)

| Chapter | What You Learn | Real-Life Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Chapter 0: Survival Kit](../a1/book/chapter-00-survival-kit.mdx)** | Your first 30 survival sentences · Polite basics (*bitte, danke, Entschuldigung*) · Asking people to repeat (*Wie bitte?*), speak slowly (*Langsamer, bitte*), or clarify | Handle basic interactions at the bakery, supermarket, doctor's reception, bus/train station, and Kita in your very first week |

✅ **Checkpoint 0:** You can greet people, ask someone to repeat or slow down, and say 30 everyday survival sentences aloud.

## Stage 2 – Describing, Daily Routine, Past Tense & Living in Germany (Chapters 6–9)

✅ **Checkpoint 2:** You can use modal and separable verbs, talk about yesterday and the weekend using *war/hatte* and *Perfekt*, navigate German trains/buses, and handle apartment searches and repairs.
