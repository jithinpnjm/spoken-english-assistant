## A2 – independent everyday German

**What changes from A1:** you stop living on fixed phrases. You talk about the past, give reasons, compare,
explain a problem and ask for a solution, and write short connected messages.

### Grammar you will learn (roughly in this order)

1. **Perfekt** – *Ich habe gearbeitet. Ich bin gefahren.* (haben vs. sein, regular/irregular/separable participles)
2. **Präteritum of sein, haben, modal verbs** – *war, hatte, konnte, musste*; *es gab*
3. **Dative in full** – *dem/der/dem/den*; *mit, zu, bei, nach, aus, von, seit*; verbs like *helfen, danken, gefallen*
4. **Two-way prepositions** – *Ich hänge das Bild an die Wand* (movement, accusative) vs. *Das Bild hängt an der Wand* (location, dative)
5. **Subordinate clauses** with *weil, dass, wenn* – the verb goes to the end: *…, weil ich krank **bin**.*
6. **Comparative & superlative** – *größer, besser, am besten*
7. **Reflexive verbs** – *sich freuen, sich interessieren für, sich treffen*
8. **Adjective endings** – recognition first, then common phrases
9. Connectors *deshalb, trotzdem, obwohl*; indirect questions; infinitive with *zu*; polite *würde/könnte/hätte*

### Real life at A2
Explaining an apartment defect and arranging a repair, rescheduling appointments, a simple complaint (delivery,
bill, service), calling in sick at work, talking to the Kita or school, understanding doctor's instructions and
referrals, reading contracts and notices for the key facts (*Frist, Kündigung*) – as **language practice**, not legal
advice.

### Goethe-Zertifikat A2 at a glance
| Module | Time | What you do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lesen | 30 min | 4 parts: a media text, information boards/programmes, correspondence, matching ads |
| Hören | 30 min | 4 parts: radio/answering-machine/announcements (twice), a longer conversation (once), five short conversations (once), a radio interview (twice) |
| Schreiben | 30 min | a personal message of **20–30 words** and a semi-official message of **30–40 words**, three points each |
| Sprechen | ≈15 min in pairs | exchange personal information using cards, talk about your life, **plan something together** |

Scoring: 100 points (25 per module). You need **60 overall**, and at least **45 of 75** in the written modules and
**15 of 25** in Sprechen. (Based on the official Goethe A2 Modellsatz – always check the current version on goethe.de.)

## B1 – the "independent user" level

**What changes from A2:** you express and defend opinions, plan with someone, give a short presentation, write
personal and semi-formal emails and a forum-style opinion, and understand longer announcements and discussions.
In Germany, B1 is often the level asked for in work contexts and in residence or naturalisation procedures –
requirements change, so always check the current rules with the responsible office.

### Grammar focus
Subordinate clauses (*als, obwohl, damit / um … zu, indem*), Konjunktiv II (*würde, könnte, sollte, hätte, wäre*),
relative clauses, passive, adjective endings, verbs with prepositions (*warten auf, sich interessieren für*),
*da-/wo-*compounds, connectors for argument (*deshalb, trotzdem, außerdem*), formal vs. informal register.

### Useful fixed phrases (Redemittel)
See [Redemittel & Useful Phrases](../parallel-systems/redemittel-phrases.mdx) – they start at A1 and grow into the B1
opinion, suggestion and presentation phrases.

### Goethe-Zertifikat B1 at a glance (modular)
| Module | Time | What you do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lesen | 65 min | 5 parts (blog/correspondence, articles, matching ads to situations, opinions, rules/instructions) |
| Hören | 40 min | 4 parts (short announcements twice, a talk once, a conversation once, a radio discussion twice) |
| Schreiben | 60 min | personal email ≈80 words, opinion post ≈80 words, semi-formal email ≈40 words |
| Sprechen | 15 min in pairs + 15 min preparation | plan something together, present a topic (5 slides), discuss |

Each module is scored out of 100; you pass a module with **60 points**, and modules can be taken separately.

## How to prepare now
- Finish the [A1 learning path](./learning-path.mdx).
- Keep your mistake log – A2 grammar builds directly on A1 word order and cases.
- Start noticing *weil*-sentences and Perfekt in what you hear around you. You will learn them properly at A2.
